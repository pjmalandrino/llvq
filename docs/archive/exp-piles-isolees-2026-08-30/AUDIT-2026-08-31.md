# Audit of the "isolated stacks" protocol, as executed rather than as written (2026-08-31)

> **Why this audit.** The 30-31 August wave is over: 12 jobs, $1.29, 13 commits.
> The operator wants to **re-run** this protocol after every kernel improvement.
> A protocol is not reused as it was written. It is reused as **execution
> corrected it**. This document sets the two side by side, item by item, and
> feeds
> [`protocole-piles-isolees-v2`](../../proofs/protocole-piles-isolees-v2-2026-08-31.md),
> which is the anchored, reusable version.
>
> Provenance: every verdict cites its journal in `docs/mesures/` or its row in
> `docs/data/jobs.csv`. Nothing here is from memory.

## 2. What execution produced that the protocol had not asked for

1. **Cross-stack transfer is measured, no longer assumed.** AWQ exists on both
   sides: 70.04 (ours, dequant) against 69.82 (vLLM), 0.22 pp. That was the
   "central design control" of `MACHINES.md`, and it delivered.
2. **The f16 scored by 4 independent implementations** (range 0.52 pp), a
   standard reusable by every future scorer: any new instrument must return
   70.3-70.9 on this model or it is broken.
3. **The pick-level divergence between dequantization kernels** (95.79%), the
   arXiv:2607.08734 citation that §6 has carried from the start, observed for
   the first time in our data. It **motivates** the business benchmark we have never built.
4. **Our dense arm's handicap corroborated from outside**: vLLM 83.09 and
   llama.cpp 84.83 agree within 2.1% on the same L40S, at 1.95× our 43.60. The
   same-head ×1.11 stops being a self-diagnosis.
5. **The dividing line of 2 bits** confirmed by two failures and one success:
   affine scalar (GPTQ: gibberish) against codebook (IQ2_XXS: 39.39, LLVQ:
   55.59). That is the premise of the field, measured here.

## 3. What this audit does NOT cover

- The **vLLM ppl** half still does not exist; no vLLM ppl is publishable.
- **QTIP** remains the 2-bit state of the art with no quality measurement (no
  Qwen3 artifact), the box that neither this wave nor the previous one filled.
- The calibration variance of the **third-party arms**: one imatrix draw, one
  GPTQ draw. The σ of 2.92 pp is ours, applied to the others by declared
  analogy.
- The equivalence of the llama.cpp ppl chunks with our 12 windows: the
  intra-stack ratio is legitimate, its transfer between engines remains an argument
  rather than a measurement.
