Tetra decode path

“Walk me through your decode path. How many instructions per weight, and where does the 2.7 come from?”

Qwen3-4B, the served object: 216 Tetra matrices, 36 v_proj in int4 g128, q8 embedding. Kernel tv_tetra48_h, the one-row kernel of token-by-token decoding, compiled for sm_89 (L40S). Written 2026-09-25; every number carries its label and its journal.

Decode cost 10.0instr / weight 241 SASS instructions per 24-weight block, one FFMA per weight among them. The fp16 control: 3.86. measured, static SASS

The answer in thirty seconds

Say it this way

Each block of 24 weights is one 48-bit word in VRAM: a 47-bit label of a Leech lattice point and one gain bit. Decoding walks a small trellis for the 12 Golay bits and reads three rows of a 16 KiB rank table. That is 18,816 bytes of constant tables for the whole model, so no codebook is stored per model. Byte permutes turn ranks into integer coordinates, an exponent trick turns them into floats without the int-to-float unit, and the 24 values meet the activation in 24 FMAs. Compiled for Ada, that loop is 241 instructions per block: about 10 per weight, one of which is the FMA itself. The fp16 control compiles to about 4.

The 2.7 is the whole model. The projection matrices hold 2.13 bits per weight: the word, plus fp16 for the 0.45 % of weights that do not fill a 24-block, one fp32 scale per row, and v_proj in int4. The embedding is 9.7 % of this 4B's parameters and stays at 8.5 bits. 2.13 × 90.3 % + 8.5 × 9.7 % ≈ 2.75.

1One block, one lane

One warp computes one output row; each lane takes one block of 24 weights per iteration and walks the row in steps of 32 blocks. The figure is one iteration, top to bottom, with the SASS count of each stage in its pill.

  • A · 14Fetch. Two 32-bit loads. A row starts on a u32 boundary, so an odd block's 6-byte record starts mid-word and is realigned by a 16-bit funnel shift.
  • B · 49Table reads. The 12 path bits walk the Golay trellis: prefixes[s8][b1] gives c1, branches[s8][b2] gives c2 and the next state s16, suffixes[s16][b3] gives c3. The rank bits pick three rows of the rank table, the third in class p⊕r⊕δ. Six loads from constant tables that share L1 with the activation tile.
  • C · 64Rank to value. Coordinate j is y[j] = val(p + 2·c[j], ρ[j]). The value tables are bytes held in registers, so one PRMT does four lookups; one LOP3 per four bytes lets the pattern bit choose between two candidates.
  • D · 56Byte to float. One PRMT writes each byte into 0x4b0000__, which reads as the float 2²³ + byte, and one FADD removes 2²³ + 128. The result is an exact integer in [−10, 10], with no I2F.
  • E · 33Dot. 24 FFMA against the activation tile staged in shared memory. The trio permutation is a choice of register, fixed at compile time.
  • F · 20Magnitude and gain. ‖y‖² comes from the same bytes with six IDP.4A. m = ‖y‖²/16 indexes invnorm, and bit 47 picks one of the matrix's two gain centroids.
  • G · 5Accumulate. One FMUL, one FFMA, and the loop step.

2Where the 241 instructions go

Both loops pay one FFMA per weight. The dense control spends the rest loading 48 bytes per 24 weights and widening them from fp16. Tetra reads 6 bytes for the same 24 weights and spends the rest decoding them.

3Where 2.7 comes from

Every term is a rate times a share of the model's 4,022,468,096 parameters. The projections are 90.3 % of them and hold 2.1309 bits each; the embedding is 9.7 % and holds 8.5.

Codebook and metadata

  • Codebook: none stored per model. The lattice is the codebook. The card holds 18,816 bytes of constant tables for every matrix at once: rank rows 16,384, branches 2,048, prefixes 128, suffixes 128, invnorm 128. That is 0.00004 b/param, not billed in 2.7475.
  • Per matrix: two fp32 gain centroids, 1,728 bytes over the 216 matrices.
  • Per row: the fp32 row scale, in the ledger, and an 8-byte rounding of the row stride so the last block's 6-byte read cannot fault: 4.1 MB of landing pad, reported by the bench and not billed (+0.008 b/param).
  • Per block: nothing. No header and no base table. Every 48-bit word is a valid label, so there is no index to check at load.
  • On the card: 1.381 GB accounted plus the 4.1 MB pad gives 1.386 GB. The card's own counter prints 1.39 GB, and 2.140 b per projection weight, which matches 2.1309 plus the pad to the third decimal.

What these numbers do not say

  • The instruction count is static: NVRTC 12.4 compiled to sm_89 SASS on a machine with no card. The served path hands PTX to the driver, whose compiler may schedule differently. The register count agrees with the card's report (40); the card's own SASS was not read.
  • An instruction count is not a time. Nothing here says whether the loop is bound by the ALU, the table reads or DRAM.
  • Only the one-row kernel is counted. The prefill kernel (tv_tetra48_rows_h) decodes each word once for 4 rows; the int4 kernel (tv_q4_h) that reads v_proj is not counted.
  • The whole-model rate depends on the embedding's share. At 8B the embedding and the untied head are 15.2 % of the parameters, and bare Tetra reads 3.0672 b/param (ETAT §5 septies).
