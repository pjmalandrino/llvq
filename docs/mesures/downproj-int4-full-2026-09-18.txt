THE PREDICTION WAS WRONG, AND THE PREREG SAID HOW IT WOULD BE
--------------------------------------------------------------
Predicted +1.5 to +3.0 pp, point +2.1, reasoning that the winner's curse would
take half of the exploration's +3.79 as it took half of `o_proj`'s +2.77. The
prereg also wrote: "a point estimate at or above +3.79 would mean the selection
bias did not operate on the largest of six arms, which would itself need
explaining."

Measured +4.05, above the exploration. The bias did not operate.

The explanation is post-hoc and is labelled as such. The winner's curse bites
hardest when the true effect sits near the noise floor. On the 2,280-question
subset the paired SE is 1.19 pp, so `o_proj`'s explored +2.77 stood 2.3 standard
errors above zero and was half noise. `down_proj`'s true effect is +4.05 with an
SE of 0.40 on the held-out split: it clears the noise by so much that being
picked best of six barely inflated it. One case each. This is not promoted to a
rule.
