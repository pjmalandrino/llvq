WHERE IT PUTS THE 8B
---------------------
68.03 micro at 2.9260 kernel b/weight, 6.27 pp above the 4B's best arm. The
paper's 4B numbers are not a comparison for it, and no 8B f16 exists on this
split, so the retained fraction cannot be stated on one population.

WHAT IT SAYS ABOUT THE CAP
---------------------------
The lever loses 26 % of its return per bit from 4B to 8B. If that continues,
buying quality with bits pays little at 14B and beyond, which is where the
memory argument actually lives. The two levers that are **free in bits**,
calibration and compensation, have no measured reason to fade with size, and
the diagnostic of the same morning put the compensation at 7.5 times the
codebook.
