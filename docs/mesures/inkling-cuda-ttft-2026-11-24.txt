INKLING 2 MINI ON CUDA, THE READ OF A PROMPT AND ITS MEMORY, 2026-11-24

RESULT
  A 9,000-token prompt is read in 23.85 s on an L40S and 77.12 s on an L4, and peaks at 12.0 and 11.5 GB
  (*measured*), with the prompt in pieces of 512 tokens, the sliding slots bounded and the scores in chunks of 128
  rows. In the morning the same card held 19.6 GB at the peak and read it in 64.12 s (L40S), 152.06 s (L4).
  A 2,000-token prompt is read in 5.88 s on an L40S against 16.56 s, with the same bytes.
  The steps that keep every bit: the experts' batched read, the blocks of 1,024 tokens, the weighted sum in one
  launch, the bias of the scores on the card, the bounded sliding slots, the copy of the convolution state.
  The step that does not: the prompt in pieces. Its perplexity against one call reads -0.042 % [-0.127, +0.044] over
  6,141 positions, PASS on its rule (point inside +-0.12 %).
  Nothing here is a default yet. Every figure is one run.

  `contiguous` and `copy` both keep the buffer behind a contiguous view. A gather of the last rows replaces it
  (`layers.rs`, `conv_context`; test `conv_state_after_a_prompt_owns_its_rows`, killed by its mutant).
  End of run after 2,000 tokens, L40S: 13.997 GB before, 10.373 after; batched read 16.043 before, 10.507 after.
  Same bytes. After load: 8.387 GB on the L40S, 8.133 on the L4.
