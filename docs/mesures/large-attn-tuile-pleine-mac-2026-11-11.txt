THE ATTENTION TILE'S FULL-TILE PATH: BUILT AND GATED ON THE MAC, 2026-11-11. $0.
=====================================================================================================================
Result: on a full tile (8 positions) the attention tile now runs a body with no guard between its positions. ptxas
interleaves the 8 score chains: the 8 K loads issue back to back and the 32 shuffles sit in 57 consecutive
instructions. The full-tile path is 552 instructions against 731 for the guarded loop, with 1 branch region against 34
(*measured*, devel ptxas 12.4 sm_89 on the Mac). One warp's in-order issue of a tile call drops from 2,900 to 1,117
cycles on the tree arms and from 2,936 to 1,548 on 4E (*estimated*, assumed latencies, section 3). The ex2 calls are
branch-free but ptxas keeps them as 9 consecutive chains. Every arm equals its guarded parent bit for bit on the host
twin, and two mutants are killed on each side (section 4).
Nature: Mac only. No job, no image, no prereg, no model loaded. Branch claude/arbre-prefill-2026-11-11, with
claude/arbre-nuit-2026-11-10 merged in locally (E1's arm mechanism), not pushed.
Lead: docs/mesures/large-prefill-r32-mac-2026-11-11.txt section 2 (the tile serialised by its nv guards).

2. THE SASS (*measured*, nvcc 12.4 -arch=sm_89 -O3 in nvidia/cuda:12.4.1-devel-ubuntu22.04, cuobjdump -sass)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The card's driver ptxas compiles NVRTC's PTX, so these counts are indicative.
Tile loop of a12ipg32ft (pers_4et_a12ipg32_f1_t384, 0x329b0-0x37490): 1,199 instructions, both paths. The guarded
path stays for partial tiles; `ISETP.GT.U32 nv, 0x7` sends a full tile to 0x351e0.
Full-tile path (0x351e0 to the shared store and the closing BSYNC B0): 552 instructions.
  FFMA 182, HADD2 128, FADD 59, SHFL 32, LDS 27, IMAD 19, FSETP 19, FSEL 18, FMUL 17, FRND 9, F2I 9, FMNMX 8.
  Control: 1 BSSY / BSYNC pair (the carry, behind `fresh`), 5 BRA (the own branch, the fresh branch, two __syncwarp
  fallbacks, one shuffle divergence fallback). The guarded loop of a12ipg32: 731 instructions, BSSY 34, BSYNC 34,
  BRA 64.
Interleaving, read in the emitted order:
  - scores: the 8 K LDS.128 at positions 5 to 12 of the path; the fma of the 8 positions alternate from there on;
    the 32 SHFL.BFLY span 57 instructions, 8 at each level (0x8, 0x4, 0x2, 0x1) between the FADDs of the level
    before. The 8 score chains are interleaved.
  - ex2: no branch region left, but the 9 polynomial chains are emitted one after the other (first and last poly
    FFMA of each 5 instructions apart, chains 15 to 35 instructions apart). Writing the ex2 steps across the 8
    positions in the source gave the same order (a third compile, reverted).
  - PV: straight line, the V loads interleaved with the fma of the previous position.

3. WHAT IT COULD BE WORTH (*estimated*)
---------------------------------------
Per tile call, one warp's in-order issue over the emitted order, every instruction once, ops/sass_chain.py's
latencies (LDS 30, SHFL 30, ALU 4 to 6 cycles; assumptions, not measured on the L40S):
  tree, a12ipg32 guarded loop        2,900 cycles      a12ipg32ft loop head and full path   1,117 cycles (x0.385)
  4E, v9 guarded loop                2,936 cycles      v9ft loop head and full path         1,548 cycles (x0.527)
Prefill, 8k pass. a12ipg at 16 rows: attention 16.78 ms (*measured*, D1), split by the rows study into 10.1 ms of
per-row tile chain and 6.6 ms of bytes (*estimated* there). The chain part at x0.385 is 3.9 ms. Attention of a 16-row
pass at 8k: 6.6 ms if bytes and arithmetic overlap fully, 10.5 ms if they add; about 9 ms. A 32-row pass at 8k,
twice that: 13 to 21 ms against 32 to 34 ms for a12ipg32 (the rows study's x0.95 to x1.00 of twice 16.78). Over the
256 passes of a 8,191-token prompt the attention grows about linearly, so the first token through a12ipg32ft gains
about half the 8k saving a pass: 1.5 to 2.5 s off a12ipg32's 8.16 s (estimated in the rows study), 5.7 to 6.7 s.
4E at 8,192. The token's attention grows 1.985 ms from 5 to 8,192 positions (*computed* from 236.6 and 161.0 tok/s,
E1). The serial part of the tile in that growth: 256 jobs a layer over 142 CTAs (2 rounds), 8 steps a job, one call a
step for each of 8 task warps: 16 calls in series a layer, 36 layers, 576 calls x 2,936 cycles / 2.52 GHz = 0.67 ms.
At x0.527 that part saves 0.32 ms. If the whole growth scaled with the call, it would save 0.94 ms. Token at 8,192:
5.27 to 5.89 ms, 170 to 190 tok/s, against 6.21 ms and 161.0. At 5 tokens the tiles are partial and nothing moves,
unless the 30 new local operations cost.
What would make these wrong:
- the driver ptxas schedules the NVRTC PTX differently, in particular the score interleave or the spills;
- the latencies are assumed; a warp's issue order understates the hiding by the other warps of the SM;
- the 10.1 / 6.6 ms split of the prefill attention is a model, not a measurement;
- 4E's growth may be held by the cp.async ring's waits, the split fold or the barrier gaps rather than the tile, which
  the lower end of the interval assumes only in part;
- 4E v9ft's spills in the row phases (30 local operations a launch's thread) could cost more than the attention gains
  at short context.
