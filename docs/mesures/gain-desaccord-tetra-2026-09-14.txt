WHAT WAS ASKED AND WHAT WAS RUN INSTEAD
----------------------------------------
The go was for stage 0 on real GPTQ residues, which needs Qwen3-0.6B and the
wikitext-2 parquet. huggingface.co is refused by this session's egress policy
(403 on CONNECT, recorded by the proxy at 21:11:50Z). No model, no checkpoint
and no block dump exist in the container. The model-free half of stage 0 was run
instead: the angular geometry of the encoder, which is a codebook property and
needs no model. The trajectory half stays unmeasured and is named in LIMITS.

THE SETUP
----------
Blocks are Gaussian draws from the F1b seed 0x0f1b20260904 through
llvq_bench::gauss_block, the population the 88.89 % retention figure was read
on. 40 training rows and 400 evaluation rows, disjoint, 106 blocks per row,
which is the count of full blocks in a 2560-wide Qwen3-4B matrix. Centroids come
from llvq_quant::quantizer::fit_gain_centroids at k_bits = 1, called exactly as
calib.rs:840 calls it. Directions come from the production TetraShapeGain. One
gain bit, 47-bit label, 48-bit word: the rate, the format and the decoder are
identical in every arm below.

Rule A, served: the centroid nearest ||x|| / row_scale (quantizer.rs:777).
Rule B, Euclidean optimum over the same two levels: the centroid nearest
  <x,u> / row_scale, where u is the unit direction of the encoded point. With
  two admissible gains B is the oracle, so A's regret is exact and B's is zero.
Rule C, the Schur-conditioned score, is absent: it needs a real Hessian factor.

THE THREE CONTROLS
-------------------
1 passes. The algebraic cost model agrees with the production decoder
     reconstruct_shape_gain on all 84,800 candidate costs, worst gap 4.619e-14.
2 passes. A mutant that scales the point norm by 1.0001 moves that gap to
     8.502e-3 and trips the assertion. The check is not vacuous.
3 passes. Two consecutive runs are identical on every line except the clock.

THE RESULT
-----------
Disagreement A against B: 4,618 of 42,400 blocks, 10.892 %.
Direction: 4,618 go down a level, 0 go up. The shift is one sided, as the
  algebra requires, since <x,u> = ||x|| cos(theta) <= ||x||.
Gain occupancy at level 1: 45.901 % under A, 35.009 % under B.
Mean cos(x,u) = 0.960791, so the reachable magnitude sits 3.921 % below the
  block norm the served rule compares against.

The 2x2. Rule and centroids are separate mechanisms, so both are moved
separately and then together. Retention is at 2.000 b/dim and is a
within-protocol control, not the published 88.89 %, which uses the bench's own
frame and gain handling.

fit time and not an encoding pass over the matrix.

THE VERDICT AGAINST THE PLAN'S WRITTEN RULE
--------------------------------------------
The plan's stage 0 rule reads: under 1 % stop, 1 to 10 % continue, over 10 %
continue and raise stage 3 to first priority. The rate is 10.892 %, so the
third line fires. The family is not closed.

Both mechanisms are real and they are close to additive: -1.2224 % and
-0.8818 % separately, -1.9789 % together, so 0.95 of the sum where the file's
seven isolated arms ran 0.618 to 0.792 of sub-additivity.

The plan's claim that the centroids are first order and the selection rule
second order does not hold. The rule is the larger of the two here.

LIMITS
-------
The source is Gaussian, not GPTQ residues. Production hands the quantizer a
block already rewritten by the error feedback of every earlier column, against a
row scale fitted before that feedback ran. Rotated blocks of a real matrix have
kurtosis 3.01 (ROADMAP-QUALITY.md row 8), which justifies the Gaussian source for
the angular part and not for the trajectory part. The cos(x,u) distribution is a
codebook property and should carry; the ||x|| / row_scale distribution is not,
and it is what sets the width of the disagreement band.

Nothing here is a quality claim. Retention is Shannon retention at a fixed rate,
two transpositions away from perplexity (ROADMAP.md section 2.2 bis), and the
file holds three cases where a better local proxy composed worse (ETAT.md
section 7). The 21.5 % perplexity gap between leech0c13 and Tetra bought an MMLU
difference of 1.18 pp, CI95 [-1.54; +3.98].
