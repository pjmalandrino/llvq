1. THE CLOSED PROBLEM AND ITS NUMBER
------------------------------------
The problem: at 2.73 b/param for the whole model, the sealed 4B's four MMLU answer logits sit at SNR 1.5216 against
f16, and iid Gaussian noise of that amplitude alone predicts 64.65 against 70.14 for FP16.

The number: the answer-logit SNR against the f16 dump of the same size, beta sd(x) / sd(e) on the centred four option
logits, pooled over the questions (logit_snr.py, as R0 depot section 3a states it). The axis stays MMLU micro on the
full split; the SNR is the quantity the Gaussian model reads it through. Only the ratio sd(e) / beta moves the
Gaussian prediction: at the sealed 4B's slope with no noise the prediction is 70.27, and noise alone at sd / beta
gives almost all of the loss (*computed*, logit-snr-4b lines 164-168, on the older 2,280-q arms).
