WHAT IS SERVED
--------------
A config field, `metal_kernels`, optional, one closed name: "lutd+h+pq". It is the metalrun arm that measured the
kernels, read by one parser (`MetalTuning::parse_arm`), so the arm a journal timed and the config that serves it
cannot mean two things. "lutd+h+pq" is the Tetra matvec tv_tetra48_metal_lutd, the half-io twins, and the prefill
kernels of 8 rows a launch for the Tetra (tv_tetra48_metal_lutdr) and the int4 records (tv_q4_metal_rows): the bundle
arm lutd+h+grp+pq of metal-nuit-2026-11-06 (M2, M8, M10). `+r` (rot_tg) has no "retained" verdict in that journal and
is left out.
- The three new configs carry the five object fields of the expanded configs, `"attention": "grouped"` and
  `"metal_kernels": "lutd+h+pq"`.
- served.rs: a config with `metal_kernels` is refused off Metal and beside 4E or a batch. Metal accepts the grouped
  form only from such a config, so the card's grouped configs stay refused on Metal by name. The kernel form stays
  refused on Metal, with or without the field.
- `served::load_served` hands the config's kernels to the Metal loader. `Served::apply` checks on Metal that the
  loaded runtime launches the kernels the config names, so a door that loads through the older `load_resolved`
  (bin/mmlu, bin/gsm8k, bin/chat) is refused by name under a Metal config instead of serving the default kernels.
  The runtime engine loads through `load_served`.
- MetalTuning::default() is unchanged (pinned by a test), so Inkling's Metal stack and every bench keep their bits.
  LLVQ_CONFIG with one of the older configs serves the expanded form with the default kernels, as before.
- bin/metalrun gains `cfg:<config>` arms (another config's served door, whole, with no measurement fallback), a
  discarded-round count, the first token per arm and its ratio round by round, and a one-window ppl.
