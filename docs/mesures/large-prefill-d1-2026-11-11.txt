WHY THE FIRST-TOKEN PREDICTIONS MISSED (as far as the files show it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Attention time did not fall with the bytes.
   - The prereg computes 5.3 GB of cache reads a pass at 8,191 for a12ipg against 19.3 GB for a12ip, x0.27.
   - Attention time fell x0.49 (34.43 to 16.78 ms, *measured*).
   - Effective rate (*computed*, from computed bytes): a12ip 561 GB/s, a12ipg 316 GB/s.
   - 5.3 GB at the design note's 0.61 to 0.86 TB/s would take 6.2 to 8.7 ms (*computed*). About 8 to 11 ms of
     a12ipg's 16.8 ms are not accounted for by the bytes.
   - The stamps time attention as one phase; they do not split it. Candidates the design note names (the scalar tile
     work for 16 rows, the 2-stage ring, the balance between shared jobs and per-row jobs) were not measured. The
     cause is not established.
2. a12ipg costs more than a12ip at short contexts: about +0.45 ms a pass at 1,024 (*computed*, 0.029 s over 64
   passes). At pos0 below 256 a12ipg has no shared split and runs a12ip's jobs; the extra cost there was not isolated.
3. The predictions of the 8k pass (19 ms) and its attention (9.5 ms) sit at the low side; the measured values land
   at the upper edges of their intervals. The first-token predictions scaled from those central values and from a
   saving taken across the whole prompt; the saving is much smaller at short contexts (section above).
The design was signed at groups of 4 rows, not 16: the design note (large-prefill-contexte-2026-11-11.txt section 3)
already sets Rg = 4 with a 2-stage ring, for shared memory. The arm as built matches it (93,568 B a CTA).

VERDICT
-------
Decision rule of the prereg:
- Identity holds at 1,024 and 4,096 (and 8,191), 47 of 47 texts.
- First token at 8,191 x1.302 sooner, under the 1.5 gate. FAILS FOR SPEED.
The prereg's reading of the phase split when the speed rule fails:
- The pass at 8,191 is 26.49 ms, under 29.6 ms (44.4 / 1.5): the rest of the pass is not what holds the first token.
- Attention fell to 0.49 of a12ip's, not to about a quarter. The repeated reads were a cost, since attention halved
  with the bytes cut to 0.27; but the time did not follow the bytes. The design leaves about half of its attention
  time unexplained (WHY section, point 1).
- The first token gains less than the 8k pass because the saving shrinks with the context and turns negative at
  1,024.
What stands: a12ipg is a12ip's bits, at the same peak, x1.30 sooner at 8,191 and x1.10 at 4,096.

- The x3.61 / x4.52 against eager mix two jobs on the same card type and image family.
