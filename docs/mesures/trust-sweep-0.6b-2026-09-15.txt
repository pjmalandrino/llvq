WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE INSTRUMENT
------------------------------------
The claimed column is monotone and the measured one is not. The surrogate has no
idea where it stops being valid: it will keep promising more for any T it is
given, and at T = 0.20 it promises a quantized perplexity of 8.90 against the
f32 model's own 19.50, which is impossible.

So the domain is not derivable from the model. It is a measurement, and this
sweep is how it is taken. That is a property of the method and not a defect of
this map: a second-order expansion carries no information about its own radius
of convergence.

The arithmetic bound and the empirical one agree on which side to be. The claims
become physically absurd past T ~ 0.08 (a quantized perplexity below f32), and
the measured realization collapses to 0.107 at exactly that point.

LIMITS
-------
One model, one map, one corpus. The grid is five points and the maximum lies
between 0.04 and 0.05; nothing here resolves it finer, and refining it would be
fitting the grid to this one run.

Every T moves all 196 matrices at once, so this sweeps the amplitude axis at
fixed breadth. The breadth axis was swept in
errmap-heldout-0.6b-2026-09-15.txt, at fixed T.

The correction remains post-hoc scaling of quantized weights, not re-encoding.
