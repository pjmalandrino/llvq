SETUP
-----
Same text, prompts and tree as spec-arbre32-mac-2026-11-11: GSM8K rows 0-199 and the house set's 20 math prompts on
the card's own greedy text (SP8's gsm200.jsonl, decoder `pass`); house chat, code, rag on the MLX door's greedy text
(the arbre32 cache, copied). Target: sealed 4B through the MLX door; the drafter's embedding and tied head are the
target's int4 g64 table, as on the card (--vocab q4), in every arm. One target capture a prompt, read by every arm,
so the arms are paired prompt by prompt.
Reference ("ref"): the drafter checkpoint z-lab/Qwen3-4B-DFlash-b16 at b74e3a32, stored bf16, widened to f32. It
reproduces the arbre32 run's tree of 15 on 244 of 244 prompts, accepted and passes identical (*measured*).
Arithmetic: f32 in every arm. Weights: fake quantization, quantize then dequantize each matrix once.
Quantizer matched: llvq_llm::embedquant::quantize_affine, the function int4_record (the target's int4 g128 records,
int4swap) and embedq (the int4 g64 embedding) both call. Matrix narrowed to f16; per group of g consecutive weights
along the input dimension, scale = f16((max - min) / qmax), bias = f16(min), q = round((w - bias) / scale) clamped
to [0, qmax], w' = bias + q * scale; a group whose f16 scale is 0 keeps q = 0. Size b + 32/g bits a weight
(embedquant::bits_per_weight). int3 uses the same formula with qmax = 7; the Rust function refuses 3 bits.
Arms:
  ref          checkpoint bf16 -> f32
  f16          the 35 linear weights and the norms rounded to f16; fc and hidden_norm kept f32 (the card's form)
  int8g64      the 35 linear weights of the 5 layers (q, k, v, o, gate, up, down) int8 g64; the rest as ref
  int4g128     same, int4 g128
  int4g64      same, int4 g64
  int4g128+fc  int4 g128 on the linear weights and on fc
  int4g64+fc   int4 g64 on the linear weights and on fc
  int3g64      int3 g64 on the linear weights
Relative weight error, sum of squared error over sum of squares (*measured*, meta.json): f16 1.2e-18, int8g64
2.8e-5, int4g128 1.00e-2, int4g64 8.1e-3, int3g64 3.7e-2; fc 1.17e-2 at g128 and 8.9e-3 at g64.
fc: quantizing its weights is a storage question. The binary16 overflow of commit ce8fb7f is in fc's output (taps of
layer 34 up to 15,352 summed over 12,800 inputs), so the card must keep fc's arithmetic in f32 whatever the weights.
