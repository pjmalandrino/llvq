  error.
- G is conditional on beta and sd, which are fitted on the same questions (prereg §5). The interval does not carry
  the fit's own uncertainty.
- The instrument does not see a constant offset on one option letter (prereg C5 (c): -0.11 [-0.65, +0.43] for a

  resolved", never "zero".
- The 2,280-q figures of the logit-snr journal are not compared with these.
