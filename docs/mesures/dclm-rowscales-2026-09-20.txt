And then:

  MMLU, against f16's 70.14     **61.11**, nine points short

This repository already holds five dissociations where perplexity improved and
MMLU did not follow. **This one runs the other way and is larger than all of
them**: perplexity is essentially repaired while MMLU stays nine points down.

Two consequences.

**Perplexity is no longer a usable quality gate on this object.** Any decision
gated on it would now read this model as solved.

**It is expected, and the paper shows it too.** The training objective is KL
against the dense model on text, which is what perplexity measures; MMLU is a
different task. The paper's Table 6 carries the same effect at larger amplitude:
its fine-tuned sg-0bit reads Wiki 9.26 where its own FP16 baseline reads 12.41,
so its fine-tuned 2-bit models are *below* f16 perplexity while their MMLU
stays 7.4 points under. Ours is the weaker version of the same phenomenon, on
an object whose perplexity we can attribute to one lever.

What it does NOT license: reading the 1.0074 as "quality preserved". It is
preserved on the metric the lever was trained against.
