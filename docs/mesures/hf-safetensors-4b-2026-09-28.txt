THE GATE BITES, CHECKED THREE WAYS
----------------------------------
A gate that has never failed has not been read. Three mutants, each caught:

  one bit flipped in a codes tensor of the 4B, in place   MISMATCH on
      model.layers.17.mlp.gate_proj.weight.codes, exit 1; restored, green again
  the reader's word assembly reversed to little-endian    MISMATCH on
      model.layers.0.self_attn.q_proj.weight.indices, exit 1
  one bit flipped in a rotation table                     MISMATCH on the
      orthogonality of small, before any digest
  a record pointed at a rotation key with no table        MISMATCH before any
      digest. That is the one failure of this layout which would otherwise
      reconstruct plausible weights in the wrong basis

The f16 fields caught a real defect on the way: the reader first CAST f16 to u16
instead of reading its bytes, so a scale of 0.001 hashed as a zero. It failed on
the first int4 record and the cast is now refused by dtype.

VERDICT
-------
Row 1 of the decision rule: "every digest agrees and every control passes: stage
0 passes. Stage 1 is proposed to the operator with its own prereg."

The kill criterion of the plan did not fire. No field of the sealed file lacks a
faithful safetensors dtype. The one field that is not a tensor is the rotation
seed, a u64, which candle cannot hold and which config.json carries as a decimal
string beside the tables it generates.

reads this number, and it is an open decision for stage 1.
