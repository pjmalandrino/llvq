3. ANOMALY N1, EXACTLY (logit-snr-4b-2026-09-12, ops/logit_snr.py, self-test logit-snr-selftest-2026-09-12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3a. What the noise is, operationally (read from the code)
  - Data: the four option logits of each question, as mmlu writes them (DUMP_COLUMNS, mmlu.rs line 428). 2,280
    questions, 9,120 logits an arm, one f16 dump as reference (journal line 1; logit_snr.py lines 32-50).
  - Fit (fidelity, lines 105-119): centre the four logits of each question on their mean, for f16 (x) and the arm (y);
    pool all 9,120; regress y = beta x + e; sd_res = sd(e); SNR = beta sd(x) / sd(e).
  - Gaussian iid (simulate, lines 161-175; called at line 329): for every question, beta times the raw f16 logits plus
    an independent N(0, sd_res sqrt(4/3)) draw on each of the four options, argmax, stratified micro. 60 draws. The
    sqrt(4/3) undoes the variance centring removes (lines 106-107). Noise is iid across options and questions.
  - Own residuals (replay with bands = 1, lines 178-218, line 330): each question receives the centred residual vector
    of another question, drawn at random, added to beta times its centred f16 logits. 40 draws.
  - Banded replay (bands = 20, line 331): the same, the donor drawn inside the same twentieth of f16 top-two margin.
  - The "+-" is the sd over draws of the simulation. It is not a sampling interval on the gap. The journal forms no
    paired interval on measured minus predicted. Per-arm sampling SE on 2,280 q is about 1.41 pp (line 68).
  - Nothing is injected into weights, activations or the model. The script runs on dumps, no card (lines 5-22).
