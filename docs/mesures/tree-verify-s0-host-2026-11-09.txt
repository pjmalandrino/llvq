TREE VERIFY S0 ON THE HOST: pers_4et.cu AGAINST ITS THREE GATES, 2026-11-09
===========================================================================

Result: the three gates of stage S0 (docs/plan-verify-tree.md section 5) pass on the Mac (*measured*, $0, no card).
- Identity. Every row keeps its bits (argmax, binary16 logits, final residual, K and V at both layers) at R = 1 to 32,
  under slot permutations, scheduler seeds, two grid shapes, the 8B's parts and lengths past 256. A tree row equals
  the same token decoded later as the root of an R = 1 pass, once that pass's first phase moved its ancestors' K and V
  into the cache.
- Closeness. R = 1 within 1.09e-3 to 3.45e-3 relative RMS of 4E's logits, with 4E's argmax, in four configurations.
- Mutants. 10 of 10 killed, each by a named gate: row order 3, scale association 3, the tree mask 3, the fold order 1.
What this does not establish: the card. The tensor core's summation is modelled as a sequential f32 sum, NVRTC has not
compiled the device branches (cp.async, mma, cvt), and nothing here is a time. Identity on the card is S1's and S3's.

Code:    pers_4et.cu (llvq-cuda/kernels), commit 935cfc8 and the commit of this journal. Host: tests/host_mma.h (the
         MMA), tests/host_pers_tree.inc (command 'Y' of host_pers.cpp), tests/tree_verify_host.rs (the gates).
Command: cargo test -p llvq-cuda --test tree_verify_host -- --test-threads=1
Machine: Mac M3 Max, one core under nice 15, beside another session's MLX job. The five tests on the code of this
         journal: 1,741 s in one run (closeness 46 s, R = 1 to 32 about 540 s, the mutants about 13 min).
Prereg:  none, $0 host work. S1's draft (proofs/BROUILLON-preregistration-verify-tree-s1.md) stays unstamped.

1. THE PASS
-----------
  One cooperative launch, R <= 32 rows, row r the token toks[r] at position pos0 + depth[r], its ancestors' slots in
  path[r]. Waits: 1 + 9 a layer (+ 1 with a Tetra o) + 2.
  - Projections q, k, v, o, gate, up, down and the head: tv_batch_gemm.cu's arithmetic. A block decoded once into
    binary16 weights, scales folded (f16(v x ((gscale x invnorm) x rscale)), the Tetra tail as columns, int4 f16(s q +
    b)); binary16 activations; mma.m16n8k16 with f32 accumulation, k16 steps in column order. A CTA unit is one 16-row
    tile a warp and one d_in slice; 96 columns staged a chunk through two cp.async stages. Slices fixed per projection
    (here 3, 2, 1, 8, 2 for q/k/v, o, gate/up, down, head), folded in slice order by the warp that draws the tile's
    last ticket.
  - Prologues ln1, ln2, the down's input, the final norm, the Tetra o's rotation: row r on CTA r mod grid, 4E's scalar
    norm and rotation in f32, rounded to binary16.
  - Attention: 4E's job body and split fold; position p < pos0 from the cache, pos0 + d from slot path[r][d] of the
    tree buffer; length pos0 + depth[r] + 1.
  - K and V of each row go to the tree buffer [layer][slot]. The next launch's first phase copies slots mv[i] to cache
    position move_pos0 + i.

2. THE FIXTURE
--------------
  Command 'G''s model and draws (host_pers_4e.inc): two layers at the 4B's widths, a Tetra then an int4 down, int4 v and
  o on 4E v9's tiled words, a tied int4 g64 head of 1,024 rows; variant 16 adds the 8B's Tetra o on layer 1 and an
  untied head. Random caches. One change: the SiLU table is x / (1 + e^-x) of every binary16 pattern. With 'G''s
  random table the first closeness reading was 0.688: a one-ulp move of a gate output lands on an unrelated value.
  The reference is 4E v9's token symbol (pers_4e_tokens_f*_t512) for one token, its logits rebuilt from its final
  residual with its norm and pers_q4g64_row; the command fails unless their argmax is 4E's token.

  The mutants' gates: closeness at one configuration, identity on a 12-node tree (subsets of 1, 3 and 9 rows and the
  tree permuted), root identity on 2 nodes at depth 2 and 3.
  fold_arrival survived the first series. The units were dealt slice after slice, so a tile's last slice always
  arrived last and every fold ran in the same order. The units are now dealt block after block: a tile's slices run
  on neighbouring CTAs at once, their tickets arrive in no fixed order, and the mutant dies on the scheduler's seed.
  The arithmetic did not change: the other gates gave the same numbers on both dealings. Two mutants were rewritten
  because -Werror refused their first form (an unused parameter, an unused variable): a mutant that does not
  compile fails the test, it is not counted as killed.

6. WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
  - The card's tensor-core sum. It is not a sequential RN sum, so the host's logits are not the card's. Identity holds
    on the card if each output element depends only on its own column of B, as the PTX specifies; S1 checks it.
  - NVRTC: the host build compiles none of the asm (cp.async, mma.sync, cvt.rn.f16x2.f32) nor the register budget.
  - Cost: no c_R. S1 measures it, and needs the Rust mirror of Pers4tLaunch, the module install and its bench, none
    written.
  - The EAGLE states (each row's residual at three layer inputs) and the head on the card: S3.
  - Quality on a real model: S2.
