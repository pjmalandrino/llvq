WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------
No attribution between the int4 embedding and the int4 reallocation. One
`LLVQ_EMBED` arm on an unchanged sealed file would separate them and has not
been run.
Nothing about the device on a **sealed** file. The device effect is measured on
a base, which carries no int4 embedding and fewer int4 records.
Nothing through the served kernel. `ppl` does not read `LLVQ_CONFIG`.
Nothing about MMLU. These are perplexities, and this file holds seven cases
where the two metrics disagree.
