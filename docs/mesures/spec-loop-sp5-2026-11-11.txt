DECISION (the prereg's rule)
--------
VALID: the self-consistency gate holds on every run. The aggregates are read against their predictions above: the
acceptance, the head stage, the null drafter and the VRAM hold; the drafter round and the speeds (but rag on q4) fail.

READING (not part of the decision; *computed*)
-------
- The q4 vocabulary changes nothing the pass sees: the same tokens and the same accepted counts as dense, prompt by
  prompt, within one or two rounds in the histogram. It takes 0.38 ms off the head stage a round and 777.9 MB of VRAM.
- The drafter round does not follow the halved device operations: 5.67 ms a round in the loop (dense), against SP3's
  4.9 ms. The readout (1.95 ms) and the block (1.90 ms) are the larger stages; dflashcheck's loop-like lines put one
  round at 5.4 to 6.0 ms with one synchronisation, 5.4 ms with a synchronisation between stages.
- Against SP4 (the older drafter, tree x1.470 over all prompts, 36 % of the wall), the tree on the dense vocabulary reads
  x1.395 at 40 % of the wall: the same acceptance, a dearer round.
