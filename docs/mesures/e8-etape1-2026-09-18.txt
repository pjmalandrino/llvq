WHAT IS VOID, AND WHY IT IS STATED FIRST
-----------------------------------------
The prereg compares arm A, `Tetra` as the dump recorded it, against arm B, a
product of three E8 codes. Arm A is a **sequential GPTQ encode**: `diagnose_row`
takes a `GptqFactor` and carries a `working` vector, so its error is fed forward
through the Schur factor of the cell's Hessian. Arm B, as section 2 of the
prereg specifies it, is a plain per-block nearest neighbour with no
compensation.

The arms therefore differ in the codebook AND in whether they know H. The
primary ratio measures the second at least as much as the first, so **no
statement about E8 against Leech follows from this run**. That is the same
confound the arbitration dossier criticized in the paper's own E8 row,
reproduced here in my own protocol, and found by a control that was not in it
(E6).

THE BY-PRODUCT, AND IT IS THE RESULT WORTH KEEPING
---------------------------------------------------
The isotropic column is what makes the pair readable. For a residual of the
same energy in a random direction, `E[e'He] = |e|^2 tr(H)/n` exactly, so the
ratio to that value separates how much error an arm makes from where it puts
it.

  Arm B sits at **0.996 of isotropic**. A plain nearest-neighbour encode puts
  its residual nowhere in particular, which is what one would expect and is
  now measured.

  Arm A sits at **0.148 of isotropic**, from 0.097 on `o_proj` to 0.256 on
  `gate_proj`. The served pipeline's residual costs **6.8 times less** than a
  random residual of its own energy.

So the compensation buys a factor of 6.8 in the H-weighted cost, and pays for it
with 40 % more unweighted energy than a plain encode at fewer bits. Both halves
of that trade are measured here on real rotated blocks, which the record did not
have.

WHAT THIS CHANGES FOR THE ARBITRATION
--------------------------------------
The dossier asked whether the 24-dimensional block needs the Leech lattice, and
bounded the lattice difference at 9.0 % of MSE. This run cannot answer it, and
it also shows the question is smaller than the one beside it: a factor of 6.8
sits in where the error goes, against a bound of 1.09 on how much error the
lattice makes.

Stage 1 proper needs arm B **inside the same GPTQ loop**, sharing the Schur
factor, the gain levels and the row scale, so the only difference left is the
direction codebook. That is a new prereg. Its signed prediction follows from
this file: if compensation is what the 6.8 buys, arm B's `J_A/isotropic` should
fall from 0.996 toward 0.148, and whatever gap remains is the lattice.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
- Nothing about E8 against Leech, by E3.
- Nothing about quality. `J_local` is a local activation metric over one
  projection, not the model's loss, not perplexity, not MMLU.
- The 0.148 is measured on `Tetra` with GPTQ against `Tetra` with nothing. It
  is not attributed to the lattice, to the trellis or to the gain rule, and
  this file separates none of those.
- `down_proj` carries 1.7 calibration samples per dimension even on the v64
  arm, so its Hessian is rank-deficient and its J column is the weakest of the
  four.
- Three layers of 36, four rows of thousands, one model, one calibration seed.
