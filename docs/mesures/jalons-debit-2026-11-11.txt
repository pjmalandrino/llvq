Throughput work on the sealed Qwen3-4B Tetra object, L40S: the milestones.

Rule of the work (operator, 2026-11-11): raise speed while quality, whole-model b/param and VRAM stay where the served
object has them. The scope is open, the served 4E kernel included. A milestone is one question settled by a
measurement. Each one closes with the table of the fundamental criteria against base Tetra and what is still owed.
Every figure is measured unless it says computed or estimated.

J2, 2026-11-11 morning. What does the speculative mode cost in memory, and can it be had at 2.73 b/param?
  Not usefully. Reading the weights in place gives back 1.28 GB and costs 15 % of speed (house set 340 to 289 tok/s,
  chat 240 to 200, below base). An int4 drafter keeps acceptance within 2 % at 0.29 GB, whole model 3.30 b/param
  (Mac, not built on the card). A drafter with no weights accepts 1.27 tokens a pass on GSM8K and 0.93 on the house
  set: +12 % and -6 % (estimated). On MT-Bench's 80 first turns conversational chat gains 2 to 22 % at best and some
  prompts lose under every gate tried. At 8k the speculative pass is 3 times slower than base. Operator ruling: two
  profiles, the default at 2.73 b/param, DFlash kept for deployments with memory to spare.
  Journals: spec-memoire, spec-arbre32-mac, spec-brouillon-int4-mac, spec-brouillon-sans-poids-mac, spec-chat-mac,
  spec-mem-m3, spec-cout-fixe-audit, spec-passe-4e-r-etude.
