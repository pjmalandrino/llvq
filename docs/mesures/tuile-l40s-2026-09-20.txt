THE MECHANISM, WHICH IS THE REAL RESULT
----------------------------------------
  amplitude across the three tiles
  Planes14   **1.5 %**
  Tetra     **19.1 %**

The prereg named this as its fourth refutation line: "Tetra moves and Planes14
does not: the effect is the decoder table's residency and not a global
occupancy change, which is the mechanism tile.rs claims."

Planes14 is flat and Tetra swings 19 %. The tile is not changing occupancy for
everyone; it is taking L1 from a table that only Tetra has. `tile.rs` claimed
that mechanism from a synthetic bench and from a two-card discrepancy. It is now
measured on the served kernel, on one card, with the other arm as the control.

THE PREDICTION WAS WRONG, AND IN THE FAVOURABLE DIRECTION
----------------------------------------------------------
  Tetra ms at tile 64   predicted 3.77 [3.5, 4.1]   measured **3.423**  OUTSIDE
  gain on the total     predicted +8.3 % [+0, +15]  measured **+16.1 %** OUTSIDE

Both fall below the floor of their intervals. The floor was set at zero gain to
cover the case where the synthetic row did not transport at all. The case that
happened is the opposite: the served path shows a LARGER effect than the
synthetic bench did.

  R at 128 / 64 / 32
  synthetic, f1rankfloor   0.5784   0.4668   0.4704
  served path, here        0.6455   0.4193   0.4386

So `f1rankfloor` understated the penalty of tile 128 on sm_89, and the module's
caution about promoting its optimum was right for the wrong reason: the
synthetic bench was not optimistic, it was conservative.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE CODE
-------------------------
`resolve_with`'s `Served` arm now reads `TILE_BY_SM` and falls back to
`TILE_BLOCKS` = 128 only where a card has no row. Both rows cite a served-path
journal: `f1d-2026-09-10` for sm_120, this one for sm_89.

The provenance semantics moved with the policy. The warning marked a departure
from the served constant; the served policy is now the table, so a measured row
no longer warns and only an explicit `LLVQ_TILE_BLOCKS` override does. Every arm
still names the card, because a default that varies by architecture is
irreproducible without it.

24 tests, 4 mutants, 4 killed: Served reading another card's row, sm_89 back to
128, the override no longer warning, and the provenance dropping the card name.

**Every figure published before 2026-09-20 was measured at 128.** The bench of
the same morning, `banc-tetra-2026-09-20`, is one of them: its 4.107 ms is a
tile-128 number, and the same arm at the new default reads 3.423.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
- Nothing about sm_80 or any card absent from the table. They keep 128 and say so.
- Nothing about tiles other than 128, 64, 32. `TILE_MIN` is 32 and the parser
  wants a power of two, so 64 is the optimum among the reachable values here.
- Nothing in tokens a second. This is the projection bench; the floor is now
  2.288 of Tetra's 3.423 ms, so 67 % of what is left is launch overhead.
- Nothing about the 8B or any other model.
