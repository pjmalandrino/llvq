WHAT THE RUN DOES NOT SAY
-------------------------
- It does not say the blocks after the write matter less to a compressed 8B or 14B. On the 4B a restore agrees with
  the reading (depth-restore-4b-2026-11-14.txt). On these two sizes nothing was restored.
- The write is the moment the answer enters the directions of the four answer tokens. The model may hold the answer
  earlier in other directions; the lens does not see it there.
- One task, MMLU, read at one position. A model may write a generated token later or elsewhere.
- Three sizes of one family.
