M1, 2026-11-11: code written and checked on the Mac, not run on a card
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Result: arm a12ip holds the weights once. It is a12 reading the words 4E and the eager path already hold, in place,
so the install builds none of the 253 tree-tiled copies: 1,307,547,648 B = 1.308 GB less on the card (computed from
pers::p4t_tiled_words over the 4B's shapes; equal to the loader's 1.308 of spec-loop-sp8-2026-11-11-brut/
spec-house.txt:33). Same tokens by construction, held on the host twin bit for bit; the card gate is open.

Design. The served readers were not taught the tree order; the tree pass was taught the served order.
- 4E's row loop (pers_4e.cu), the eager prefill (tv_tetra48_h, tv_q4_hht), the embedding gather and the drafter's q4
  vocabulary read a row's words contiguous. In the tree order a row's words are 12 B every 192 B (Tetra) and 24 B
  every 384 B (int4). Teaching them means rewriting the served module's inner loops and the eager kernels: refused,
  the served path must not move.
- The tree pass's stream unit already reads, a lane, its own row's words a sub-step (two Tetra, three int4). The copy
  only puts a tile's 16 rows of a sub-step side by side. So one order serves every reader: the served one.
- pers_4et_a10rc.cu: p4t_unit_rc and p4t_phase_rc take a template flag IP (default false). Under IP a lane reads
  logical words 3 sa + h and + 1 (Tetra) or 6 sa + 3 h + k (int4) of its row, zero past the row's end, an int4 word at
  its 4E v9 place when the segment says so: p4t_tile_word's rule, word for word.
- pers_4et_a12.cu: the a12 body takes IP; a module under P4T_A12IP 1 emits pers_4et_a12ip_f{0,1,2}_t384 and the load
  check p4t_ip_check, not the a12 symbols. pers.rs: p4t_a12ip_unit_text, p4t_a12ip_symbol, p4t_served_words.
- persist4e_cuda.rs: TreeForm::A12ip launches on 4E's own images (as the rows arm does). At install, projection by
  projection, the copy is built, p4t_ip_check compares every lane's words read in place with it (every word of the
  copy is some lane's), the copy is freed; a nonzero count refuses the install. The v9 load check's pattern.
- specbench: arm `a12ip` (taps on), and a cuMemGetInfo line after the verify pass's install, before the drafter.
- Selected by the arm name only. No config changes; a12 and every served path are as before.

What cannot be freed: nothing of the 1.308 GB on the 4B. What stays is the served object (projections 1.16 GB,
carried 0.22 GB), the tree's row buffers 0.153 GB, the drafter 1.193 GB and its cache. A Tetra o_proj (the 8B) is
refused by a12ip as by a12.
