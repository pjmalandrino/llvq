  Reading. On the L4 the dense attention products are 11.1 ms of 30.0: 4,608 bytes a row of BF16 weights that the

  The glue kernels above the floor are those with a chain of dependent adds over the 2,304 channels in one warp
  (`glue-gate-down`, `glue-router`) or several block sums in a row (`glue-post-attn`, `glue-pre`).
  What it means for the next gain, *estimated*: widening those chains could take 1 to 2 ms on the L40S, and would
  change sums that are class B, so the perplexity band would be owed again. Not done. A larger gain needs fewer
  launches a visit, which is a rewrite of the visit as one kernel, not a tuning.
