THE ANSWER
-----------
Two candidates are easy, under $5 and a day: the int4 reallocation at 4B (row 1) and
MSE clipping of the int4 records (row 3). Neither has a measured gain at 4B. The largest
expected gains, held-out draw selection and YAQA rounding, are not easy: $11 to $14 and
two days, and 5 to 10 days. No candidate here crosses the f16 gap. The best of them closes
1 to 2 of the 6.77 MMLU points the 4B sits below f16 (ETAT §3).

          GSM8K unknown.
   Moves: b/param equal or lower by construction. VRAM equal or lower. Decode speed and
          prefill are NOT guaranteed: the same bytes pass through a different kernel mix,
          and arm A of the 8B was never timed. Unmeasured, +-3 % (*estimated*). The Metal

   Cost:  4B: build on the Mac $0, MMLU $0.70, GSM8K $2.13, speed check about $0.30

   What:  the int4 g128 records (v_proj, o_proj, down_proj@12-23 at 4B, about 770 M

   Gain:  +0.1 to +0.5 pp MMLU (*estimated*; L33 put +0.15 [-0.1 ; +0.4] on v_proj alone,

   What:  GPTQ today minimises tr(E H_in E') row by row. YAQA adds a dense output factor
