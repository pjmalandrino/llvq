The attention kernel's context threshold, on the host, 2026-11-04

Result: the "kernel" attention form now sends a decode or verify row to the kernel only from position 1,280 on
(llvq_llm::kvq::KERNEL_FROM, a constant of the form); every row below takes the grouped path and is the grouped
form's, bit for bit (*measured* on the CPU replay). A call that straddles 1,280 is split by row, so a row's path
depends on its position alone. Nothing ran on a card; $0 spent. The configs that said "kernel" in the two attention
benches (d74ea29a..., r1 job 6aeb53b2 and r2 job 6aeb90e3) ran with no threshold: the served line now names it,

1. The threshold, *computed* from the served 4B on the L40S (attention r2, job 6aeb90e3, *measured*,
   docs/mesures/lot1-4b-2026-11-04.txt on integration/lot1). ms a decode token = 1,000 / median tok/s.
     prompt   kernel ms   grouped ms   grouped slope to the next length, us a position
          5     7.868       7.402        0.693
        256     8.137       7.576        0.642
       2048     8.264       8.726        0.691
       4096     8.475      10.142        0.666
       8192     9.597      12.870
   The grouped form is linear in the position, 0.64 to 0.69 us each over the whole range. The kernel is flat from 256
   to 2,048 (0.07 us a position): its fixed cost dominates there.
   - Bracket. If the kernel's cost does not fall as the position grows, it lies between 8.137 ms (its 256 median) and
     8.299 ms (the slow end of its 2,048 range, 120.5 tok/s) at any position in between. The grouped line,
     7.576 + 0.642 us x (L - 256), crosses those two values at L = 1,130 and L = 1,382. The forms cross in [1,130,
     1,382].
   - Point. The two straight lines (grouped and kernel, 256 to 2,048) cross at 1,239.
   - 1,024 is excluded: the grouped form costs 8.069 ms there, under the kernel's floor of 8.137.
   - 1,280 = 5 splits of 256, the multiple of the split nearest 1,239, inside the bracket. The error is asymmetric,
     so the threshold sits on the high side: one too high costs speed only, at most 1.2 % a token at 1,280 if the
     true crossing is 1,130; one too low costs speed (at most 0.8 % a token at 1,280 if the crossing is 1,382) AND
     gives more positions to arithmetic that is not the grouped form's.
   - Caveat. Between 256 and 2,048 nothing was measured: the bracket rests on the kernel's cost not falling with the
     position (more positions, never less work) and on the grouped form's linearity (five lengths). The kernel at
     256 takes the one-split path (no partials, no ticket); at 512 and above it folds splits, so its cost may step up
     past 256, which moves the crossing down, never up. Sweep (a) reads the kernel from 1,280 directly.

2. What the code does
   llvq-llm/src/kvq.rs          KERNEL_FROM = 1280, with the derivation above.
   llvq-llm/src/model.rs        kernel_rows(): rows at positions max(from, n - l) .. n go to the kernel, the rows
                                below to grouped_attention over the history they see (k, v and the mask narrowed),
                                then the two parts are joined in row order. A call wholly below is one grouped call,
                                wholly past is one kernel call. Block::kernel_from = KERNEL_FROM; only cfg(test)
                                writes it. A decode or verify call under the kernel form in any dtype but f16 is
                                refused at once, below the threshold too, instead of at position 1,280.
                                Qwen3::kernel_from(); decode_nll_split(tokens, arms, split): the steps below `split`
                                compared bit for bit (below_differ), the steps from it scored apart (tail_nll,
                                tail_count, tail_top1_agree). decode_nll_paired = split 0, unchanged.
   llvq-llm/src/served.rs       the served line names the threshold.
   llvq-llm/src/bin/fusedrun.rs LLVQ_GQA_PPL kernel mode: refuses a window that does not reach position 1,281;
                                stops on any step below 1,280 that differs between the arms (a wiring defect);
                                prints, per window, the nll from 1,280 beside the whole window's, and in the summary
                                the paired interval over positions 1,280 and later beside the whole-window one.
                                LLVQ_CONTEXTS prints, below its table, the tokens each prompt produced, with their
                                fingerprint: two configs' sweeps compare token for token after the job.
