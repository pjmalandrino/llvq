4. THE ENCODER AND QUIET DIRECTIONS
-----------------------------------
Facts from the code.
- The layer objective is the H-weighted output error. `proxy_loss` docstring: "Tr(ΔW H ΔWᵀ), the expected output MSE
  of the layer under the calibration distribution" (llvq-quant/src/gptq.rs lines 554-559).
- H is the uncentred second moment of the layer input, AᵀA/N, every calibration row weighted 1/N, no mask, no clip, no
  position weight (llvq-llm/src/calib.rs lines 17-22; `accumulate`, lines 89-92, `* self.scale` with scale = 1/N at
  line 64). The sealed recipe draws 64 windows of 2,048 DCLM-edu tokens, 131,072 rows (CERVEAU Q24 line 357; smoke.rs
  lines 630-631 for the positionals).
- The inputs of block t come from the already quantized prefix; inside a block every activation is captured with the
  original weights on the published path (calib.rs lines 1-13; lines 1180-1186 comment; `sequential_block` false,
  lines 607-621).
- Damping adds 1e-2 times mean(diag H) to every diagonal entry (llvq-quant/src/linalg.rs lines 51-63; default 1e-2,
  smoke.rs lines 735-754). It is applied to the rotated H (calib.rs lines 1290-1294). The trace, hence mean(diag H),
  is the same in either basis.
- The off-diagonal shrink is skipped on the published path (calib.rs lines 547-566).
- The input rotation is orthogonal, built from a Walsh-Hadamard factor, a small dense factor and random signs, one per
  (block, activation) (llvq-quant/src/rotation.rs lines 1-32; calib.rs lines 600-606). H moves as Q H Qᵀ (calib.rs
  lines 1286-1292).
- The block quantizer sees only the 24 weights of a row block, `quant.quantize(v, ...)` (gptq.rs line 318). H enters
  only through the error feedback onto later columns, by the Cholesky factor of H⁻¹ (gptq.rs lines 375-393).
- Gain levels are fitted per matrix by Lloyd-Max on relative block gains (quantizer.rs lines 890-896), the row scale
  is fixed before the loop (gptq.rs lines 283-291), and the published `gain_scale` is 1 (calib.rs line 583 doc).
- int4 types skip GPTQ, the rotation and H entirely: group-affine RTN of W (calib.rs lines 1336-1350 comment and
  branch).
- Row scales trained afterwards act per output row (dclm-rowscales lines 20-24: 1,069,056 row_scales change, nothing
  else). They cannot reweight an input direction.

Inference, not measured.
- In the eigenbasis of H, the objective prices error along input direction u at lambda_u plus 1 % of the mean
  eigenvalue. A direction whose calibration energy is under that floor is priced at the floor; one above it at its own
  energy. Nothing else in the objective raises the price of a direction that is quiet on average.
- GPTQ's feedback moves error toward cheap directions; the literature states the same property for LDLQ (ExLlamaV3,
  r0-litterature-2026-11-14 lines 79-81). So the encoder is expected to leave its largest relative output error on
  inputs that live in quiet directions of H. The rotation does not change H's eigenvalues, so it does not protect
  them.
- H averages all calibration positions of running text. A position whose input has a distribution unlike the average
  token, such as the end of a structured prompt, is priced by the average. The answer position's stream is hit about
  four times harder than the average position (contrast-map line 17); the code is consistent with this and does not
  prove it.
- down_proj has 9,728 inputs for 131,072 rows, 13.5 rows a dimension (calib.rs line 554 comment). Its small
  eigenvalues are estimated from few effective samples; the 1 % floor is then the only thing that sets their price.
- Where one token or a few directions hold most of the trace, the floor (1 % of the mean) rises with them, and every
  other direction is priced more evenly. The pooled errors near 0.001 of gate_proj layers 1 to 3 and down_proj layer 6
  (section 1e) are what a trace held by a few outputs would look like; they are not shown to be that.
- The int4 records see no H at all and read coherent gains near 1 (section 1e); the lattice records read 0.96 to 0.99.
  That the shrink comes from the gain code or the feedback, not from H's weighting, is not separated.

5. RIVAL HYPOTHESES FOR N6
--------------------------
Common target: why, at the answer position, the sealed 4B keeps 0.58 of FP16's answer contrast (0.81 at the write,
then a rescale), with the quiet band at slope 0.47 by depth 12. One card run of the contrast-map kind can carry the
kills of
H6-A to H6-D at once; its cost is *estimated* from contrast-map-4b (job 6af86d01, l40sx1, 918 s billed, $0.46, loop
705 s for 570 questions with both models resident; lines 31-33). Patching and per-position pools need new code in
ops/contrast_map.py, which has `stream_hook` and `proj_hook` taps that read but do not write (lines 302-342).

H6-A, the dull one: the quiet-band loss is a symptom. The layer objective leaves noise in quiet directions at every
position, it is larger at the answer position only because that position's band signal differs, and the band does not
carry the answer. The MMLU deficit sits in the louder bands and at the write block.
- Predicts, alone: replacing the sealed answer-position stream's band [513, 2560] by FP16's at depth 12, then running
  the sealed blocks on, recovers little of the gap at the output; the same patch on band [9, 64] or at depth 24
  recovers more.
- Kill: the depth-12 quiet-band patch recovers at least half of the output gap on the 570 questions (73.51 against
  66.32 in the script, contrast-map line 48-49). Card, *estimated* $0.5 as above.
- $0 hint already on disk, not a kill: down_proj at int4 on layers 0 to 11 of the C4 base, where the band loss forms,
  moved MMLU +0.48 [-0.17, +1.10] (downproj-slices line 20). Older base, int4 not f16, one draw.

H6-B, a position mismatch of the calibration statistics: the encoder prices directions by their energy in running text
(section 4), and the answer position uses directions that running text leaves quiet, so the matrices themselves commit
their largest relative error on answer-position inputs, mostly in layers 0 to 11.
- Predicts, alone: the relative error of the 252 matrices measured on answer-position inputs is several times the
  all-position value of matrices.tsv, with the excess in the layers before depth 12; the question-text positions of
  the same prompts read like the pool.
- Kill: the answer-position relative error of every type is within a factor 1.5 of its all-position value
  (contrast-map lines 99-105). Then the 4-times-harder hit is made by propagation, not by the matrices. Same card run.

H6-C, massive activations in H: in some matrices the trace of H is held by one token or a few directions (the sink),
the encoder fits those, and ordinary tokens, the answer position among them, see a relative error far above the pooled
figure.
- Predicts, alone: in the matrices with pooled errors near 0.001 (gate_proj layers 1 to 3, down_proj layer 6;
  matrices.tsv lines 15, 52) the error pooled without the first token, or without tokens above a norm threshold, is
  many times larger; the quiet band starts to fall between depth 4 and 8, where the all-position direction-1 slope
  jumps to 1.188 (report.txt lines 59-60).
- Kill: excluding those tokens changes the pooled error of those matrices by less than a factor 2. Same card run, or
  the Mac capture tool `hcapture` with `hstats` (ROADMAP-QUALITY row L36, line 151), which loads the model on the work
  machine and needs the operator's word.

H6-D, single attention heads lose their focus: aggregate dilution is dead, but heads at layers 7 to 13 lose most of
their top weight at the answer position (section 1f), in the depths where the band falls.
- Predicts, alone: writing FP16's attention probabilities into the sealed run at layers 7 to 13, answer position only,
  restores the band slope at depth 12 and the lens accuracy at depth 25.
- Kill: that patch recovers under a fifth of the band deficit at depth 12 (0.47 to 1). Same card run. The forward
  calls scaled_dot_product_attention (ops/contrast_map.py line 332), so writing probabilities needs an explicit path.
- Against it on disk: restoring q, k and v raises the output slope to 0.6994 and not the noise, for +0.55 MMLU
  (r0-contraste lines 63-64).
