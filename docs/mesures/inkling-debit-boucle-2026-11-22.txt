INKLING 2 MINI, DECODE SPEED ON THE MAC, THE LOOP OF 2026-11-22

RESULT SO FAR
  Decode went from 0.526 s a token in the morning to 0.0361 s in the night, 1.9 to 27.70 tok/s (*measured*), with
  the 24 layers of experts resident in lattice codes. Device memory after load went from 11.37 to 8.03 GB.
  With 600 tokens of context the same setting reads 27.4 tok/s, with 2,000 tokens 26.2. With no switch at all,
  every step bit for bit, the engine reads 5.88 tok/s. The file, its bits and its values did not change: every
  step below is bit for bit, or class B with its gate, as `docs/plan-debit-inkling2-mini.md` defines them.

WHAT IS A DEFAULT AND WHAT IS NOT
  Default, bit for bit: the pool fix, the one-row visit, the cache in place, the experts in four launches, the
  8-row prompt path.
  Behind a switch: the fused glue (`--glue fused`), class B. Its gates on one window: equal greedy tokens on the
  32- and 600-token prompts, 0 and 6 routing flips of 768, perplexity +0.035 % +- 0.100 %. Two more windows are
  being read before it becomes the default.
  Behind a switch: the int4 head (`--head int4`), the file's own values.
  Behind a switch: the attention's products in three launches a visit (`--attn fused`), class B. The seven
  attention matrices of a layer are then held once on the device, in BF16 as the file stores them, and a prompt
  widens one layer's matrices for the duration of its call. Its gates (`attn-wiring/` beside the raw table):
    `--attn candle` gives the logits of the head before it, bit for bit, on the 32- and 600-token prompts, both glues;
    `--attn fused` gives equal greedy tokens on those four runs of 17 rows; 1, 0, 0 and 6 routing flips of 768,
    starting at margins of 1e-5; logit gaps of 3e-3 where no flip happened, 0.12 at most after one;
    one WikiText-2 window, 2,047 teacher-forced positions, fused against candle: -0.0006 % +- 0.102 % (95 %);
    five wiring mutants, each seen red by `tests/inkling_wave3_attn.rs`.
  Behind the same switch, third value: the scores of the row over the cache in one launch
  (`--attn fused+scores`), class B, reading the keys and values where the cache holds them. Its gates
  (`scores-wiring/`): equal greedy tokens on three runs of four; on the 600-token prompt with the fused glue the
  tokens part at row 15 on a near-tie (top-2 margins 0.0020 and 0.0011) after one routing flip at a margin of
  2.5e-5; one window of 2,047 positions, -0.052 % +- 0.124 % against candle: the point is inside the band of
  +-0.12 %, the interval is as wide as the band, so one window does not settle it. Three wiring mutants seen red.
  Its gain grows with the context: at 600 tokens held the step goes from 0.139 to 0.095 s.
  A default with the fused glue, bit for bit: the routing of a visit goes to the experts on the device, and the
  host no longer waits on the device 48 times a token. `--routing host` is the path before it and its reference:
  same logits on four runs of 17 rows (`routing-on-device/`), two mutants of the hand-off kernel seen red.
  With the fused glue, class B as the glue is: the four message inputs of a loop visit in one launch
  (`messages-kernel/`). With the kernel against without it, the other switches equal: equal greedy tokens on the
  32- and 600-token prompts, no routing flip of 768, one window -0.029 % +- 0.061 %. Two mutants seen red. It is
  worth 7 % (0.0472 to 0.0442 s).
  A default of a resident layer, bit for bit: the activation between the experts' launches in one launch instead
  of eleven (`activation-kernel/`). Each of its three factors equals candle's on every one of the 2^32 f32 values,
  the whole activation on 834,849 pairs, and the real model's logits are those of candle's operations; the default
  path still gives the logits of commit a76a44b. Five mutants seen red. It is worth 2 % (0.0442 to 0.0433 s).
  With the fused glue, bit for bit against it: the message gates and the router's scores spread over
  threadgroups (`gate-split/`). One threadgroup read a gate's 2.4 MB of weights at about a tenth of the device's
  bandwidth; the gate is now three launches whose sums keep their lanes and their order. The two paths give the
  same bits on every kind of visit; on the real model the logits are those of the head before it, and the default
  path still gives those of a76a44b. Six mutants seen red. Glue a synthetic visit: 443 to 221 us with messages,
  113 to 88 without. It is worth 20 % (0.0433 to 0.0361 s). The perplexity pair below was read before it and
  holds for it: the logits are the same bits.
  Memory with `--attn fused` (*measured*): 5.14 to 2.49 GB after the dense load, 11.34 to 8.70 GB after the experts,
  peak of a 600-token prompt 13.63 to 10.46 GB, prompt time unchanged (6.2 s for 600 tokens).

WHAT IS LEFT, head f44896e (*measured* profile `profile_gate_split.json`, *estimated* split)
  A profiled token takes 0.129 s for 0.036 untimed: the profile drains the device at every boundary, about
  0.2 ms each, so its shares are inflated by the count of their boundaries. With the drains taken out
  (*estimated*): the experts about 13 ms a token, the attention's products about 10 ms, the fused glue about
  9 ms, scores 2 ms, routing 2 ms, head 1 ms.
  Two lessons of the night. Before the routing stayed on the device, a small launch cost the host a wait; since
  then the host encodes ahead of the device and removing small launches buys little: ten launches fewer a call
  of the experts moved the token by 2 %. And a kernel that reads weights from one threadgroup runs at a tenth of
  the device's bandwidth: spreading the message gates gave 20 % with no bit changed.
  What bounds the rest: the attention's products read 54 MB a visit, 8 ms a token at this Mac's bandwidth, so
  they are near their floor; the experts' four launches a call read 16 MB of codes in about 0.28 ms, about 1.5
  times the time the served Qwen3 kernel would take for as many bytes (*computed* from 65.5 tok/s on 1.38 GB).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT READ

  machine before midnight: agents were building and measuring beside every round until 22:37. Any of these
  kernels on CUDA: they are Metal only. The card's last reading is the GSM8K run, 0.157 s a token on the eager
  path of commit d4c60ca (`inkling-gsm8k-sealed-2026-11-22.txt`).
