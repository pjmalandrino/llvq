Decision rules (§7):
- A, identity: every arm that owes the bits keeps them.
- B, stamps: v2's balance at 4,096 and 9,728 (0.626, 0.637) lies outside 0.563 +- 0.05: rows slow each other unevenly
  inside a CTA, and cycles a step are read per class with that caveat.
- C, the bound: l1w x1.214, v2_o2 x1.076, xbcast x0.995, bcast x0.987, ilp2 x0.928. None reaches 1.30, so none is a
  lever by the rule. l1w lies between 1.15 and 1.30: the rule's "none at 1.15 or more" branch (a profiler next) does
  not apply either. It is reported as the strongest single suspect, under the lever bar.
- D, calibration: the next 4E estimate is built from these cycles, balances and clocks; its backward check against
  4E v3's MLP mean work (2,975 us) is the session's, at $0, before any forward number.
- E, Λ-transposed read: bcast x xbcast = 0.98 and ilp2 = 0.93, both under 2: its claimed 2-3x does not stand on these
  mechanisms.
- F, v3's MLP: v3 over v2 = 0.849 <= 0.97: the stream loop's own cost explains it.
- G, refuted terms of §6: v2 GB/s (379 against [190, 330], faster); v2 cycles a step at 2,560 (1,616 against
  [2,200, 4,500]); v3 over v2 (0.849 against [0.85, 1.03]); ilp2 over v2 (0.928 against [0.95, 1.70]); v2's balance at
  4,096 and 9,728 (0.626, 0.637 against [0.51, 0.62]). Inside: identity, no local memory, the clock, the balance at
  2,560, l1w, bcast, xbcast, v2_o2, tpersall.
