WHAT THE TABLE SHOWS
--------------------
1. With every projection quantized the noise stays in a band, 1.20 to 1.77, and the slope moves from 0.22 to 0.58.
   Only restores to f16 take the slope above 0.6.
2. What each correction did to the pair:
   - Trained row scales: on the DCLM base the noise falls by 20 % (1.6895 to 1.3440) and the slope by 4 % (0.5354 to
     0.5132); on bare Tetra the noise falls by 14 % and the slope rises by 5 %. They work on the noise.
   - The error-map correction, the case of CERVEAU Q5 (perplexity -17.4 %, MMLU -3.06): the slope falls from 0.3798
     to 0.2235, the noise from 1.5835 to 1.2000.
   - Four times the calibration volume, the case of Q14 (-1.20): the slope falls from 0.5354 to 0.4422, the noise

3. Two of the repository's cases where perplexity improved and MMLU fell (Q5, Q14) share one signature here: the
   slope fell more than the noise.

RESERVES
--------
- Only the ratio decides an argmax. logit-snr-4b-2026-09-12.txt section 9 states it: "Attenuation costs no MMLU point.
  Only the ratio does." A slope of 0.58 with a noise of 1.42 picks as a slope of 1 with a noise of 2.44. The slope is
  still a physical quantity: the head is the same linear map in every arm, so 0.58 says the final hidden state carries
  58 % of FP16's component along the answer contrast. Whether that component can be raised without raising the noise
  as much is not known. It is the phase's question.
- Slope and noise are fitted on the questions they are read on.
- The replay misses single arms by 0.3 to 4.5 points (N1 journal, secondary arms).
- One calibration draw a file.

RIVAL HYPOTHESES FOR THE PHASE (R3 DRAFT)
-----------------------------------------
- Ha, the dull one. Slope and noise are one variable: internal noise, then normalisation, shrink every direction of
  the residual stream by the same factor. Predicts an attenuation that is uniform across directions and equal to the
  norm ratio.
- Hb, local shrink that compounds. Each quantized matrix passes less than the full coherent signal in the quiet
  directions of its input, and the loss multiplies along the depth while the noise adds. Predicts per-matrix coherent
  gains under 1 on quiet directions, a slope of the stream that falls with depth, loud directions near 1.
- Hc, attention dilution. Noise in q and k flattens the attention patterns, and the retrieved signal is diluted.
  Predicts higher attention entropy in the sealed model and a slope that falls at attention writes; restoring q, k
  and v raises the slope by 0.12 (table above).

The cheapest experiment that separates them reads, on the sealed 4B and FP16 side by side, on MMLU prompts: the slope
of the residual stream by depth and by variance band, the coherent gain of each matrix, and the attention entropy by
head. No encode. Its design, cost and kill rules go into a prereg.
