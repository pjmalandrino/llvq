OLALA V1.0 MINI 7A1B, THE NOISE PROXY AGAINST A REAL ENCODE ON THREE LAYERS: WHAT WILL BE RUN
Written on 2026-11-26 before any of its numbers was read. Not a prereg: it carries no OpenTimestamps stamp, and the
commit that adds this file is its only date. The operator's go: "Tu peux sur cette machine ? Si oui go."

QUESTIONS
  Q1. Does the isotropic noise proxy of `olala-reconnaissance-2026-11-25.txt` read the same KL as a real Tetra
      encode of the same matrices, for one word and for two?
  Q2. In the model, does a second Tetra word (4.06 b/weight) beat int4 g128 (4.25 b/weight), as it does in squared
      error (0.0074 against 0.0100, `olala-second-mot-2026-11-26.txt`)?
  It does not answer what GPTQ against real Hessians adds: every encode here is data-free, H = I.

OBJECT AND SETTING
  The weights, the forward, the container and the text of the reconnaissance: sha256 99a208e1...9356442, OVH's
  `modeling_olala.py` on a CPU, each block cast to float32, $0. Four windows of 4 x 512 tokens at offsets 0, 2,048,
  4,096 and 6,144 of the same text, 8,192 positions.
  Only the routed experts of layers 6, 17 and 28 change, both matrices of all 256 experts: 453 M parameters, a
  twelfth of the routed experts. Everything else is the bf16 model.

ARMS
  T1   one Tetra word, the repository's encoder as `extq::encode_matrix` uses it, H = I: one rotation a matrix
       (seed 0x110FEED ^ L<<32 ^ e<<20 ^ g<<16), frozen row scales, two gain centroids a matrix, retraction.
  T2   T1 plus a second word on its residual in the rotated basis, same pieces.
  Q4   int4 g128 by the arithmetic of `embedquant::quantize_affine`: min and max of a group of 128, scale and bias
       rounded to f16.
  N1   the proxy at r = 0.085.      N2   the proxy at r = 0.0074.      N4   the proxy at r = 0.0100.
  One noise draw an arm.

READINGS
  KL(bf16 || arm) on the next-token distribution over the whole vocabulary, at every position; its mean, with a
  standard error over positions. Top-1 agreement. dNLL on the true token.
  Q1: the ratios KL(T1)/KL(N1) and KL(T2)/KL(N2). The proxy is called faithful inside [0.7, 1.3], pessimistic
      under 0.7, optimistic over 1.3.
  Q2: the paired difference KL(T2) - KL(Q4), position by position. Two words are called better if its mean is
      negative and the mean plus 1.96 standard errors is still negative; worse if the mirror holds; else not
      separated.
  Positions of one window are not independent, so the standard errors are too narrow; the four windows are also
  reported one by one.

EXPECTED, BEFORE THE READS
  If the 36 layers weighed the same and added up, three layers would read about a twelfth of the proxy's pooled
  figures: KL near 0.010 to 0.013 for N1, near 0.0013 for N2. Both ifs are unverified.
  A data-free code after a rotation should look like isotropic noise, so both Q1 ratios are expected inside
  [0.7, 1.3], and T2 is expected under Q4.
