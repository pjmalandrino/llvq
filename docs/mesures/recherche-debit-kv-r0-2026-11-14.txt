Decode-speed research phase at long context (CERVEAU O13), step R0: what the repository and the literature hold on
reading part of the KV cache at decode, 2026-11-14

Nothing was measured. Two read-only agents, $0. Figures are quoted with the label of their source.

1. WHAT 4E DOES TODAY (code read, llvq-cuda/kernels/llvq_pers.cuh section 10, pers_4e.cu 333-351)
  K and V are separate binary16 buffers a layer, [n_kv][cap][128], contiguous by position, grown by 256 positions.
  K is stored after RoPE; nothing is rotated at read time. A job is one (split of 256 positions, KV head); a split
  is 4 units of 64; a step stages 32 positions, K then V. One staged tile serves the 4 query heads of its KV head
  (5 at 14B). The softmax is one pass, online: running max, running sum, f32. Splits are folded left to right.
  Every key and value of the context is read by every token.

2. THE NUMBERS ON RECORD (sealed 4B, 4E v9, L40S)
  Attention wall a token (*measured*, large-4e-hors-poids-etude): 599 us at 5 tokens, 1,108 at 1,024, 1,562 at
  4,096, 2,646 at 8,192, of a timed token of 4,227 / 4,611 / 5,080 / 6,211 us.
  KV read: 147,456 B a cached position; 151 / 604 / 1,208 MB at 1,024 / 4,096 / 8,192 (*computed*).
  P3 (conjecture): from 1,024 up the phase sits at the DRAM time of its bytes plus the prefetch.
  KV levers on record: int8 KV fails the +-0.12 % band (P4); the grouped and kernel forms are served under it;
  v9ft, the prefetch arms and the v8 ring are closed. Reading part of the cache: never proposed, studied or
  measured here.

3. THE TOOLS THAT EXIST
  LLVQ_GQA_PPL scores an attention form against the served one, paired window by window, teacher-forced, on
  WikiText-2, and accepts 8,192 (6 windows in 940 s, *measured*, attn-t8-r2). It runs the eager kernel form, which
  4E equals bit for bit. No tool dumps the decode query, the scores or the weights; the cheapest place is a
  capture hook in model.rs::attention_ctx, with the weights recomputed on the host by attn_ref.

4. THE LITERATURE (primary sources read by the agent; its own list of unverified claims is kept in the session)
  Quest (2406.10774): per-page min and max of the keys a channel, U = sum_i max(q_i min_i, q_i max_i), a true upper
  bound on every score of the page in exact arithmetic; keys and query after RoPE in its code; used for a top-K of
  pages, no rule on mass. 512 B a page a KV head at head dim 128: 6.25 % of the KV bytes at a page of 16.
  BLASST (2512.12087): a deterministic bound on skipped mass, from the exact block max, so every K is still read.
  Certified Sparse Attention (2610.24991): the only perplexity loss under 0.1 % found (-0.02 to +0.07 % on PG-19,
  32K to 128K), probabilistic, a 4-bit scan of every key, CPU only.
  Twilight, Loki, Double Sparsity, SparQ, RocketKV: +0.19 % to several percent of perplexity, or no perplexity.
  Mass statistics are thin: the top 1 % of keys holds 89 % of the mass at 100K on Llama-3-8B (RetrievalAttention);
  the first two layers are broad (Quest, InfiniGen). SparQ and Sparse Frontier report little gain at batch 1 on GPU.
  Not found as published: a page bound, the exact softmax on the pages that can matter, and a skip under a bound
  on total skipped mass. Not found is not proof that it is new.

5. WHAT R0 LEAVES FOR R1 AND R2
  The closed problem: the fraction of KV bytes a token must read at 8,192 so that the skipped mass stays under a
  budget, head by head, on the sealed 4B. Its first experiment is the one that can kill the phase: with the exact
  page maxima (the best case of any summary), if the bytes to read at a skipped mass of 1e-3 stay above about half,
  no summary can do better and the idea is dead. Unknown and unpublished: how a mass budget a layer becomes
  perplexity over 36 layers.
  Constraint to settle with the operator: a page index is 38 MB (page 32) to 76 MB (page 16) at 4B (*computed*);
  the served peak at 8k is reached in the prefill, and the only tolerance on record for it is 0.02 GB.
