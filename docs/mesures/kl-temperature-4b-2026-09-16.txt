ONE TEMPERATURE BUYS 2.5 % OF THE GAP TO THE DENSE MODEL (2026-09-16)
======================================================================
Code: kltemp and llvq_llm::tempfit at d49bbac, bytes identical to what ran.
  Cost: $0, about 5 min of Mac, Metal, f32. Artifact ~/llvq-4b-tetra.llvq
  (0.981 GB, 252 matrices, Tetra, no correction applied).
Data: raw logs in the -brut directory.

THE RESULT
-----------
Qwen3-4B against its own dense checkpoint. KL(dense || quantized) in nats per
position, full vocabulary (151,936), exact — no top-k. The temperature is
fitted on 4,092 positions of wikitext-2 train and scored on 4,092 positions the
fit never saw.

  validation split   at T = 1    at T*      removed by one temperature
  wikitext-2          0.627775   0.612121    2.49 %
  C4                  0.464936   0.460625    0.93 %

  fit split           0.381028   0.372136    2.33 %
  T* = 1.07216 (beta 0.93270), one number for the whole model.

So 97.5 % of the divergence on held-out text of the same domain, and 99.1 % on
another domain, is NOT a temperature. The quantized model is slightly
over-confident relative to the dense one, and flattening it by 7 % is worth
almost nothing.

WHAT THIS REFUTES, AND IT IS MINE
----------------------------------
On 2026-09-16 I argued against fitting gains on a KL objective, on the ground
that "the KL is dominated by the scale, so the optimizer will spend its freedom
on a direction the argmax cannot see". That was an extrapolation from four MMLU
option logits to a vocabulary of 151,936, and it is wrong by the measurement
above: the scale direction is worth 0.93 to 2.49 % of the gap, not most of it.

The reviewer who refused the claim until it was measured was right to refuse it.

WHAT SURVIVES
--------------
Only the part that was true by construction: any fraction of a KL improvement
obtained by a positive rescaling of logits is neutral for accuracy. That
fraction is now measured, and it is small. The objection therefore does not
block the pilot — it sizes a term in it.

THE CONTROL
------------
Top-1 agreement with the dense model is 74.4501 % (wikitext) and 73.1549 % (C4),
and it is bit-identical at T = 1 and at T*: 6093/8184 both times. It must be,
since a positive scaling preserves an argmax, so this is a check on the harness
rather than a result. The harness agrees with its own claim.

Two facts worth keeping beside it. The dense and quantized models already
disagree on the top token at a quarter of all positions. And the same
temperature fitted on wikitext transfers its FRACTION to C4 (2.33 % -> 0.93 %
of a smaller gap) while the absolute divergences differ by 35 %, so the
divergence level is a property of the text and the fraction is a property of
the model.

DEVIATION FROM RULE 2
----------------------
No prereg was stamped before this measurement. Rule 2 of METHODE is
unconditional -- "a written and timestamped prereg comes before the first
millisecond" -- and it was not followed. Stating it here rather than
retro-stamping, which would be worse.

What limits the damage, and does not excuse it: this stage carries a
measurement and not a gate. KL is none of the four axes nor of the three
feasibility quantities, no kill or adoption follows from it, and the number
informs whether to BUILD the pilot rather than deciding anything about the
served object. Anything decisional that rests on it must be re-measured under a
stamped prereg with its prediction written first.

SHORTCUTS
----------
One model, one artifact, one calibration corpus, one seed. 4,092 positions per
split, chosen so both models' full-vocabulary logits stay resident; the
sampling error on the fraction has not been measured. The fit and validation
splits both start past 131,072 tokens of the training corpus, so neither
overlaps the 64 x 2048 windows GPTQ calibrates on -- but the artifact's own
calibration was not re-read from the file to confirm that offset, it is the
`smoke` default assumed to hold.

The artifact is the uncorrected Tetra 4B, deliberately: the reference has to be
frozen before anything is fitted against it, and the corrected arm is the one
whose MMLU loss started this.
