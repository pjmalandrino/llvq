1. WHICH TOOL GIVES WHICH COLUMN
-------------------------------
1a. The objects swap cleanly, one matrix at a time.
- Rust: the dense path decodes each record to its own natural-basis matrix, row scales folded and rotation undone
  per record (llvq-artifact/src/format.rs 1500-1531), then narrows to the run dtype (llvq-llm/src/sealed.rs 531).
  No rotation couples two matrices on this path; rot_share is a kernel setting (CLAUDE.md, LLVQ_ROT_SHARE).
- Rust: a block's projection is reachable by name and replaceable in place (llvq-llm/src/model.rs 2057-2059,
  `linear_mut`). errmap already loops over matrices in one process by this means (errmap.rs 133-141, 398-455).
- Python: llvq-tetra materializes a dense weight per module, the rotation applied at load for that record only
  (llvq-tetra/llvq_tetra/modules.py 74-95); a one-module swap is not broken by the shared rotation tables
  (modules.py 137-152), which are inputs of the materialization, not of the forward.
- Python: `export` writes the sealed file as an f16 safetensors checkpoint, "398 tensors identical bit for bit" to
  its decode (sealed-rownorms-2026-09-23-brut/export.txt lines 14-15). It is the object llvqtune trains on.

2. ONE PROCESS OR ONE PROCESS PER MATRIX
---------------------------------------
One process can loop: both weight sets fit on an L40S. FP16 model 8.04 GB, the 252 sealed projections at f16 7.27 GB,
one window's teacher log-probabilities at 4,096 x 151,936 x 4 B 2.49 GB (*computed*); about 25 GB with the student's
logits and temporaries against 46 GB (*estimated*). The window-outer order computes the teacher once per window.
Reloading per matrix is what exists today, and it is the dear way: one `mmlu` process spends 76 to 79 s outside its
question loop, 170 s on the first checkpoint fetch (*computed*, ablation-brut/summary.tsv against the last
elapsed line of out-control, out-gateup, out-all, out-qkv). `ppl` at 12 windows adds 12.5 s (ppl-scelles-brut
ppl-4b.txt line 16). 252 mirror arms by reload: 252 x 88.5 s = 6.2 h, $11.15 (*computed* at $1.80/h, run.py 105),
over the $10, in NLL only, and the direct form refused (section 1b).

4. HOW MUCH TEXT THE KL NEEDS
-----------------------------
Sizes to separate. The sealed 4B's NLL gap to f16 is ln(12.5834 / 12.2361) = 0.0280 nats a token (*computed*, ETAT
line 32, jobs.csv line 172), 0.000111 a matrix if it split evenly. Its KL to FP16 on DCLM read 0.2622 at the first
step of a 2,048-token batch (sealed-rownorms line 50), 0.00104 a matrix if additive (*computed*). Neither split is
known; the restores do not add (ablation line 118).
Noise known. One perplexity pair, KV q8 against f16 on one file, 49,140 tokens: 95 % half-width 0.12 %, 0.0012 nats
(kvq8-4b lines 52-53; CERVEAU Q3). That is about 11 times the even per-matrix NLL share (*computed*): NLL cannot
rank single matrices at 12 windows, and 73 windows narrow it by about 2.5 (*computed*, sqrt of 299,008 / 49,140,
assuming windows independent). Group shares of 0.003 to 0.015 nats would clear it (*estimated*, unmeasured).
KL spread per 2,048-token batch: sd 0.054 around 0.204 over the first 30 logged steps of the sealed 4B's second
training, 0.054 around 0.211 over the last 200 (*computed* on sealed-rownorms-brut/journal.jsonl, one loss every ten
steps). The model moves under training, so this is an upper bound on sampling spread. As a coefficient of
variation, 26 % a batch, about 5.3 % at 49,152 tokens and 2.2 % at 299,008 if batches are independent (*computed*).
What follows: a direct-form KL is its own quantity, so two matrices separate when they differ by more than about
15 % at 49,152 tokens before pairing (*computed*, 2 x sqrt 2 x 5.3 %). A mirror-form change is about 0.4 % of
0.26 per matrix if even, far under the unpaired spread; it rests entirely on how tightly two evals on the same
positions correlate.
Not known: the paired sd of a KL difference between two swaps on the same positions; whether WikiText's KL level
and spread match DCLM's (the Mac read 0.63 on WikiText and 0.38 on the fit split for bare Tetra, kl-temperature
lines 15-19); the paired KL noise of any arm; whether the per-position KL tail breaks the sqrt scaling.
