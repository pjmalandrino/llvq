pic-vram-8k, 2026-11-08. Where the card memory goes after an 8,192-token prompt, and what the branch cuts.

Result: the excess of the duel's peak is the grouped prefill's attention, not a storage cost. It kept three f16 score
tensors of heads x 4,096 x 8,192 alive at once (6.44 GB on the 4B), and the stream-ordered pool kept the first
chunk's (1.07 GB) under them (*computed*; the pool term *estimated*). That model lands on all three measured peaks.
The branch removes two of the three tensors and the pool's leftovers with no bit moved, which brings the computed
4B peak from 11.48 to 5.65 GB. A kernel prefill, not served, removes the last one: 3.67 GB against llama.cpp's 3.52, its
peak then at the MLP.
Nothing here ran on a card; the card gate is written and not launched.

1. HOW THE DUEL READ THE PEAK
-----------------------------
One process per size and length: `fusedrun <sealed> 256` under the served v9 config with LLVQ_CONTEXTS, and
`nvidia-smi --query-gpu=memory.used -lms 100` beside it; peak minus idle (idle 0 MiB). The process loads, installs
4E, runs 1 + 5 rounds (each: prefill in chunks of 4,096 through the eager path, then 4E decodes 256 tokens), then one
eager generation to check 4E's tokens. Measured: 1,921 / 10,951 MiB at 4B after 5 / 8,192 tokens, 3,265 / 12,071 at
8B, 5,441 / 16,295 at 14B.

What a `llvq serve` user pays at 8,192 tokens: the same. Session::feed prefills in chunks of prefill_rows() = 4,096
through the same hidden_cached, and 4E decodes after it (*computed* from the code path). The eager check adds
nothing: it runs after the rounds, on the same prefill, and each generation's caches are freed and the stream
synchronised (the token read) before the next. The harness costs no memory a server does not.

2. THE DECOMPOSITION, 4B (GB = 1e9 B), second prefill chunk: 4,096 rows over 8,192 keys
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  item                                                     GB     label     where
  context, weights, 4E, fixed buffers (peak at 5 tokens)  2.01   measured  duel vram.txt
  KV cache, 8,192 positions, 36 x 2 x 8 x 128 x 2 B       1.21   computed  KvCache, Grow(256)
  score tensors: scaled, masked, softmaxed, all alive     6.44   computed  grouped_attention, model.rs (base)
    one tensor S = 32 heads x 4,096 x 8,192 x 2 B         2.15
  chunk 1's score block left in the pool                  1.07   estimated cuMemAllocAsync, no sync in a prefill
  activations alive at the attention (x, ln1, q, k, v,    0.18   computed  forward_cached
    q and k after norms and RoPE)
  causal mask f16                                          0.07   computed  build_causal_mask
    its f32 copy, uploaded then cast, freed to the pool   0.13   computed
  chunk 1's cache buffers dropped at the growth, up to    0.60   estimated append_grow
  repeat_kv copies                                         0      computed  the grouped and kernel forms have none
  eager check                                              0      computed  sequential, same path
  model                                                   10.99-11.59         measured 11.48

The same model at 8B: 12.42-13.03 against 12.66 measured; at 14B (40 heads, S = 2.68 GB): 16.76-17.43 against 17.09.
Without the pool term the model falls 1.3 to 1.7 GB short at every size: the leftovers are needed to explain the
measure. On the cards the pool is candle's stream-ordered allocator (cudarc 0.19.8, malloc_async), whose default
release threshold is 0: freed memory returns to the driver only at a stream, event or device synchronisation, and a
prefill had none between its chunks.

Why the score tensors were three: candle's ops take &self, so `let scores = ...; let scores = match mask {...}` keeps
the first binding alive to the end of the function, and the softmax allocates a third. The 6.44 GB was already
computed in the ML audit of 2026-11-02 (docs/archive/debit-vague5-2026-11-02/prefill.md §3.1, "U5": 9.6 GB estimated
at 4B as 1.38 + 1.21 + 6.44 + ~0.6). The gap from 9.6 to 11.48 is the CUDA context and fixed buffers (2.01 measured
against the 1.38 of weights) and the pool's leftovers, which the audit did not count.

3. THE LEVERS, GAINS COMPUTED BEFORE THE CODE (4B, second chunk)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  lever                                            gain GB   bits
  softmax in place, blocks of 1,024 rows           -4.26     none move (argument below, tests §4)
  sync before each prefill chunk                    -1.07     none (ordering already holds)
  sync after each cache growth                      up to -0.60, and 4E's growth past the prompt stops holding
                                                    the cache twice (+1.21 transient, 2.42 at the loop's end)
  mask built in f16 on the host                     -0.13 transient
  kernel prefill (8 rows a call through attn_dec)  -1.98 more: no score tensor at all, the peak moves to the
                                                    MLP (0.38 GB at 4B); NOT bit for bit with the grouped prefill
  smaller prefill chunks                            not taken: they change the matmul shapes cuBLAS sees, so the
                                                    bits (gqa-ab-2026-11-01: 2 of 6 reshapes kept them)

The in-place softmax keeps every bit: the QK^T and PV matmuls keep their operands, shapes and strides; between them
each element meets the affine x*scale+0, then x+m with m = +0 or -inf, then a softmax that reduces one row at a time
(one warp a row, candle-kernels reduce.cu). A block of whole rows is contiguous, so each kernel takes the branch the
whole tensor took; the broadcast mask add becomes an add of equal shapes, the same x+y. The copy back is a copy.

4. WHAT RAN ON THE MAC (measured)
---------------------------------
Host tests, llvq-llm (CPU):
- in_place_softmax_matches_the_whole_tensor: grouped and expanded bodies, f16 and f32, groups of 4 and 5, batch 2
  under a one-row mask, chunks at an offset, blocks of 1, 3, l and 4l rows: bit for bit. Same test on Metal
  (in_place_softmax_matches_the_whole_tensor_on_metal): bit for bit.
- the_in_place_softmax_moves_no_logit: the whole tiny model, in place forced on every masked call in blocks of 3,
  against the default, on expanded/Cat, expanded/Prealloc, grouped/Grow(256), grouped/Grow(4), kernel/Grow(256):
  the same logits through a chunked prefill, 12 decode steps and the full window.
- the_causal_mask_is_built_in_its_dtype: f16, bf16, f32 equal to the f32 mask cast.
- Four mutants of softmax_in_place (wrong mask rows, no copy back, scale off by 1e-4, first block only), each
  applied (non-empty diff), each killed.
- the_kernel_prefill_does_not_depend_on_the_chunking: under prefill_attention kernel, chunkings 19, 16+3, 11+8,
  8+8+3, 4x4+3 give the same logits bit for bit; the grouped prefill moves with the chunking; kernel against grouped
  max |dlogit| 3.7e-4. A chunking that leaves a one-row piece moves the tiny model's dense projections under every
  form (candle's CPU gemm rounds a one-row product apart), so it is left out; on the card that is the rows kernels'
  identity, which the gate reads. kernel_prefill_rows_are_the_kernel_rows: each row equals the kernel called alone.

Metal, dense Qwen3-4B f16, 8,192 tokens in chunks of 4,096, `llvq-llm/examples/prefill_peak.rs`, base 1997edc
against this branch, M3 Max 64 GB, peak memory footprint by `/usr/bin/time -l` (counts the Metal buffers):
  form       code      time    first token   last-position logits fingerprint   peak footprint
  grouped    1997edc   33.0 s  1874          f92a4ed4b7f432c2                   56.65 GB
  grouped    branch    21.7 s  1874          f92a4ed4b7f432c2                   14.94 GB
  grouped    40c405f+  17.4 s  1874          f92a4ed4b7f432c2                   14.79 GB   (locals freed early)
  expanded   1997edc   29.2 s  1874          f92a4ed4b7f432c2                   62.99 GB

The same bits at the real shape, and 41.7 GB less on the grouped form. Metal's buffer cache is not the CUDA pool
(candle keeps freed Metal buffers by size and a sync does not return them), so these GB are not a card prediction;
the old runs sat near the machine's 64 GB, which is likely where their extra time went (*estimated*). The expanded
form keeps 12 GB more than grouped on Metal on the branch; it is not served and its cause is not traced.

The served line keeps one score tensor (2.15 / 2.15 / 2.68 GB). The kprefill line keeps none, and its peak moves to
the chunk's MLP: gate, up, act(gate) and their product alive together, f16, with the residual in and out and ln2,
0.38 / 0.50 / 0.70 GB (*computed*, mlp_residual). A first version of this table counted the attention moment only
(3.47 / 4.91 / 7.37); the bug audit of 2026-11-08 flagged it, recounting +0.44 / +0.56 / +0.77. The audit's
extra 0.24 / 0.27 / 0.33 GB was real: the attention's locals (ln1, q, k, v before and after norms and RoPE, the
context and its flat copy) stayed alive through the MLP, because `finish` ran inside `forward_cached`'s scope, and
the prefill driver kept the last chunk's hidden states until the next returned. The code now frees them (the
attention in its own function, `ctx` consumed after o_proj, ln2 and gate/up dropped before down_proj, the last h
dropped before each chunk), which leaves the table's count. What
remains against llama.cpp is the base at 5 tokens (2.01 against 2.17 at 4B, 3.42 against 3.35 at 8B, 5.71 against
5.40 at 14B) and the 4,096-row MLP, where llama.cpp prefills 512 rows at a time.

6. OPEN
-------
- The card gate (prereg above, ops/jobs/pic-vram-8k.sh): $1.75 expected, $2.40 at worst. It reads the three peaks,
  the in-place logits against the whole-tensor ones in a second process (LLVQ_SOFTMAX_WHOLE=1), and the kprefill
  peaks, time to first token and chunking identity. Waiting on the operator's go; the Space carries another branch.
- Serving the kprefill configs: the operator's decision after the gate.
- The time to first token: the in-place pass and the syncs cost an estimated +0.2 to +0.6 s of 34.5 at 4B; the kernel
  prefill's attention against cuBLAS's is estimated 0.9 to 1.1 times. Neither is measured on a card.
- With the kernel prefill, the causal mask (0.07 GB) is built and not read, and the prefill chunk could shrink for
  its MLP (0.38 / 0.50 / 0.70 GB at 4,096 rows, a quarter at 1,024) without moving a bit if the rows kernels are
  chunk-invariant (the gate's probe says).
