THE TWO GATES
-------------
  gate A   253 of 253 digests identical, in 166.7 s
             252 records (168 Tetra, 84 Int4G128) plus the int4 embedding
  gate B   4 prompts, 64 greedy ids each, 256 of 256 identical
             from_pretrained reports no missing and no unexpected key

Exactness and not closeness. Every step is reproduced operation by operation: the
reconstruction is one multiply, one division and one multiply per coordinate, the
Walsh-Hadamard transform is a butterfly of single adds in a fixed pairing, and the
k by k mix accumulates over t from zero rather than through a matrix product.

CONTROLS
--------
1. A foreign Tetra fingerprint is refused before an index is read: passes
   (llvq-hf/tests/test_quantizer.py, and the reader's own check).
2. 100,000 labels drawn at seed 0xfe0001, decoded in Rust and in Python: 0 differ.
   100,000 distinct points, norm²/16 from 4 to 24, max |coordinate| 10, which is
   the codebook's own maximum. The origin word decodes to the origin.
3. Mutants. Four on gate A, each caught on the first record: sections swapped in
   the decode, the mix transposed, the gain bit ignored, the butterfly's stages
   reversed. One on gate B, caught at token 0 of all four prompts: gate_proj's
   weight and up_proj's exchanged after materialization, so the MLP computes
   act(up(x))·gate(x) instead of act(gate(x))·up(x).
   A first gate B mutant, the two descriptors swapped in the record table, changed
   nothing and proved nothing. It is recorded because what it found is a property
   of the design: a module's buffers are named relative to its own path, so the
   file's keys decide which module gets which weights and the record table cannot
   misroute them. The descriptors of gate_proj and up_proj are identical at every
   layer of the 4B, so swapping them is a no-op.
4. cargo clippy --all-targets silent, cargo test green in the fast loop
   (181 suites, 957 tests, 0 failed, 81 ignored), pytest 24 green.
5. The stage 0 gate still passes on the same directory: 1602 fields, unchanged.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------
Nothing about memory at inference: the model is dense once loaded, by decision.
Nothing about speed, here or at any stage of this plan.
Nothing about the rotation under a kernel, which is stages 2 and 4. Stage 1 folds
  the un-rotation into the dequantization, so the forward pass never rotates.
Nothing about the 8B and the 14B, same format, not loaded here.
Nothing about quality. No MMLU, no perplexity, no GSM8K.
Nothing about save_pretrained: is_serializable returns false, by design.
