LARGE-4E BUDGET: ONE 4E v9 TOKEN, PHASE BY PHASE, AGAINST THE BANDWIDTH FLOOR, SEALED 4B, L40S, 2026-11-11. $0.
==========================================================================================================
Result: a 4E v9 token reads 1.378 GB (*computed*) and costs 4,467 us timed, 4,677 us stamped (*measured*, N11). At
85 % of the card's 864 GB/s those bytes take 1,876 us (*computed*): the token is 2.4 times its byte cost. The row
phases take 2,920 us of mean CTA work against 1,877 us at 85 % (+1,043 us); the phases that read nothing take
1,757 us (*measured*). Gate/up rows sit furthest above their floor (2.04x of 85 %); down rows, o and the head sit at
1.13 to 1.25x. One new reading: 4E's attention phase carries the L2 prefetch of the next o and gate/up rows, 670 MB a
token, which at 864 GB/s is 776 us, the size of the phase's 791 us wall. Moving that prefetch to the gate/up phase,
where the DRAM idles, is the largest cut found that keeps the logits bit for bit: -0.20 ms short [-0.10, -0.29],
-0.65 ms at 8,192 [-0.30, -0.90] (*estimated*). Three experiments below, one job, about 14 min of L40S.
Scope: read only. No build, no run, no job, no model load. Sources: llvq-cuda/kernels/pers_4e.cu, llvq_pers.cuh,
pers_segment.cu at 172e417; the journals named on each line.

THE RULE
--------
tok/s must rise while quality, b/param (2.73, whole model) and card memory (2.01 GB at a short context, peak 2.014,
spec-mem-m3) stay where they are. Here every cut keeps the output token; each line says whether the logits stay
bit for bit or only the argmax.

Reading.
- At or near the floor: down rows (1.13x of 85 %), the head (1.23x), o (1.25x). Together 1,195 us against 1,000 at
  85 %: 195 us to win, at most.
- Far above: gate/up rows (+666 us over 85 %) and q/k/v rows (+183). Both are Tetra rows; the record places their
  excess in decode instructions, not bytes: words served from L1 change nothing (l1w x0.99, pers-rowloop), 237 SASS
  instructions a block run at 53 % of issue (recherche-debit-maths §2-3).
- Byte-free time: ln1 233.7, norm/RoPE 125.9, attention's own share (595 to 791, 1b), ln2 141.7, inner wait 181.8,
  down input 149.7, imbalance 130.7, embedding 3.0: 1,561 to 1,757 us, 33 to 38 % of the stamped token.
- Every phase at 85 %, the byte-free phases as measured: 1,877 + 1,757 = 3,634 us stamped, 3,471 timed at N11's
  stamp ratio 0.955, 288 tok/s (*computed*). Byte-free phases at zero as well: 1,889 us with the KV, 529 tok/s. That
  is the bandwidth ceiling at the bit, and it needs the Tetra rows at 734 GB/s, which today's decode cannot reach.

1b. The attention phase carries the prefetch (new reading, cause *estimated*)
- pers_4e.cu prefetch_next: every CTA asks L2 for its o slice and its gate/up slice during attention, 18.6 MB a
  layer, 670.3 MB a token (*computed*). At 864 GB/s that is 776 us; the phase's wall is 790.9 (*measured*).
- At context 62 attention is 8 jobs on 8 CTAs (one split per KV head). Their K/V loads miss L2 behind 134 CTAs'
  prefetches. The comment in pers_4e.cu orders the prefetch after the attention CTA's own job, but the other CTAs'
  traffic is already queued.
- The tree pass of N11 runs the same attention without any prefetch: 594.9 us, 196 us under 4E's 790.9 (*measured*,
  one job). spec-cout-fixe-audit line 3 put the cause on this prefetch, *estimated*.
- At 8,192 attention reads 1,208 MB of KV beside the same 670 MB: 1.878 GB in about 2.56 ms (*estimated*: 790.9 us
  plus the door sweep's +1.77 ms from 5 to 8,192, served-v9), 733 GB/s, 85 % of the peak. The phase is DRAM bound
  there, and the prefetch is a third of its bytes.
- The prefetched gate/up rows gain little from L2: the Tetra loop is issue bound (l1w x0.99; the one-launch pass
  with an L2 prefetch read x1.023 over the same pass without, pers-4e-v2). The gate/up phase reads 360 GB/s and leaves
  about 374 GB/s of the 85 % idle for 1.3 ms a token (*computed*): 488 MB of room.

1c. ln1 costs more than ln2 for the same work (cause *estimated*)
- Both are an RMS norm and a rotation at width 2,560 in every CTA. ln1 takes 233.7 us mean, ln2 141.7 (*measured*).
- ln1 also writes the unrotated vector for v, one store an element. And ln1 starts with the q/k/v prefetch in flight,
  173.8 MB a token, while its first load (x, 5 KB from L2) is on the critical path. ln2 starts with none.

1, P. Each CTA asks L2 for qkv(l+1) and o(l+1), 10.4 MB a layer, during its gate/up rows of layer l, where the DRAM
   idles (1b). gate/up rows are no longer prefetched: they stream themselves. ln1's x load issues before any
   prefetch. Attention runs with no foreign traffic. Bytes do not change; prefetch.global.L2 writes no register, so no
   value can move. Expected: attention 791 to about 600 us, ln1 toward ln2 (0 to -90 us), gate/up 0 to +30 us
   (x1.023 lost). At 8,192 attention sheds 670 MB of its 1.88 GB. Risk: L2 must keep qkv and o of the next layer
   through attention; at 8,192 a layer streams 33.5 MB of KV past them (L2 96 MB). A KV load hint (evict-first)
   protects them and moves no value.
2, B2. Each block's 48 bits relabeled at load (a permutation, same bytes), two Golay tables by linearity, lane
   rotation of idle items: 237 to 207 instructions a block, items 0.855 of today's (*computed*, recherche). The f32
   chain and each lane's order stay. Already ranked on the record; listed for completeness. 2 weeks.
3, W. One global counter a layer hands gate/up pairs to warps as they finish, instead of the host's static CTA
   slices. Each row stays one warp, one lane order: same bits whatever warp computes it. The slowest gate/up CTA is
   CTA 114 in only 8 % of the layers (vtree-4b.txt), so the excess is jitter, which claiming absorbs. It
   is not v5 (closed: v5 spread a CTA's last rows over its own warps). Claims of 4 pairs with the next claim in flight
   keep the atomic off the critical path. Works only with P (no static gate/up prefetch to honour).
4, F. The 8 attention jobs (all 256 at 8,192) each compute q-norm and RoPE of their 4 query heads from the q rows,
   and the job holding the last position appends that position's k and v. Only that job reads the new position, so
   nothing races. Same functions, same bits. Removes one phase and 36 waits.
5. ln1 6.5 us a layer, ln2 3.9, down input 6.2 per Tetra layer (*measured* means over 36 or 24). An L2 round trip,
   one reduction and a 12-stage butterfly fit in about 3 us (*estimated*). K2 already took ln1 from 320 to 227 us.
   Keeping bits asks the same order of every float operation.
6. Head. Pass 1 reads 3 of the 4 nibble planes and the scales (170 MB of 219) and bounds each row: the missing bit
   adds s_g x q0 x_i, q0 in {0, 1}, so the row lies within the partial sum plus sum over groups of s_g P_g or s_g N_g
   (P_g, N_g: the sums of positive and negative x in group g, 80 numbers a token) and an FMA rounding margin. A grid max
   of lower bounds sets T; rows whose upper bound rounds to an f16 under f16(T) are dropped, the rest re-read and
   scored in today's order. Surviving logits are bit for bit; the dropped ones are never formed: argmax only. No
   per-row table is needed, unlike plan-300's K5 (+0.6 MB). Saves at most 22 % of the head's bytes, 81 us at 595
   GB/s; the prune rate is unknown. The table is tied: the embedding gather and the eager head must read the planes
   too (one copy, as v9 did for int4). 1-2 weeks. Norm and cluster bounds (Cauchy-Schwarz, centroids) are worse: in
   2,560 dimensions their slack is of the order of the whole logit spread, so they drop nothing (*estimated*).
7. o and down read 589 and 648 GB/s; K11 (v9) already moved them. o runs 18 or 19 rows on 16 warps a CTA, two
   rounds for 2,560 rows on 2,272 warps; splitting a row over warps changes its sum order, so the round stays.

Not ranked: attention's own latency at a short prompt after P (about 600 us against a floor near 130, plan-300 K3a).
Its critical path is one unit's tiles in order, which the kernel form's arithmetic fixes; more jobs a KV head changes
the split and the bits.

3. NOT REACHABLE UNDER THE RULE
-------------------------------
- Fewer bytes a weight. Q1 (int4 records re-encoded in Tetra), a smaller head format, a re-encode for QTIP-style
  codes: each changes the stored values, so quality and b/param move.
- B1's integer dot (dp2a on int16 activations). 8.4 % of rows move by one binary16 ulp (spec-micro): logits change,
  so the token is not owed. Served only under the ±0.12 % perplexity gate, outside this rule.
- KV in int8 or int4 (K8). It is the 8,192 lever (1.21 GB a token), but it changes values: quality.
- Another attention split or job shape. The kernel form's bits depend on its 256-position splits and 64-position
  units; another shape is another form, gated by perplexity.
- Tetra rows at DRAM speed. That asks about 150 instructions a block (recherche §2); at the bit B2 reaches 207, and
  145 needs B1. The bit-for-bit single-token ceiling stays near 260 tok/s at the door (recherche §1).
- More threads or two CTAs a SM: 512 threads already sit at 128 registers; v7 was refused for 24 B of local memory.
- Fewer grid barriers by restructuring: 0.029 ms at most (recherche §7, closed). F removes 36 as a by-product.
- CUDA Graphs (A2): closed by rule 3.
- Speculation keeps the token but is outside this single-token lens; its reopening condition is ETAT §6's, worked
  by another session (spec-mem-m3).

4. THREE EXPERIMENTS
--------------------
All three are arms of fusedrun's LLVQ_PERS_4E bench beside v9, in one job, interleaved rounds, at 5 and 8,192
tokens, plus one stamped launch per arm. Cost from step3-4e (9 min, $0.28, six forms) and N11 (21 min, five larger
modules): about 14 min of L40S, $0.42 at $1.80/h (*estimated*); each alone about 10 min, $0.30. Prereg, cap and the
operator's go first.

E1, v9p (cut 1).
- Build: v9 with prefetch_next removed from the attention phase; qkv(l+1) and o(l+1) prefetched by each CTA after its
  gate/up rows' first pair; the ln1 prefetch moved after pers_ln_1rt's load; KV loads with an evict-first hint. A
  second arm v9p0 removes prefetch_next only, to split the two effects.
- Gate: the head's binary16 logits, written through pers_head_argmax's existing `logits` pointer in a measurement
  symbol, equal to v9's bit for bit on 64 positions at both contexts; tokens equal to the eager path's (64 of 64);
  module with 0 B local. No host proof is needed: the host build compiles prefetch to nothing.
- Expected: 234 tok/s [229, 239] short, 180 [170, 189] at 8,192, from 223.9 and 161.5. Stamps: attention under 650
  us short; ln1 under 200 us.

E2, v9pw (cut 3, on top of E1).
- Build: gate/up pairs claimed from a per-layer global counter, 4 pairs a claim, next claim in flight; counters reset
  by the layer's last wait. q/k/v Tetra rows the same way if the first reading pays.
- Gate: tests/pers_matches_served.rs with claim orders shuffled on the host, mutants killed (claim overlap, a dropped
  pair, a counter not reset); on the card the E1 logits gate against v9.
- Expected: +0.15 ms more [0.08, 0.25]: about 243 tok/s short over E1's 234, 185 at 8,192. Stamps: gate/up slowest
  over mean under 1.04 (today 1.10), inner wait mean under 100 us (today 182).

E3, v9pf (cut 4, on top of E1).
- Build: pers_4e_norm_rope_append's work moved into pers_attention's job prologue; the append by the job holding the
  last position; one phase and 36 waits fewer, 182 a token.
- Gate: host identity of q, the KV cache and ctx against the served chain, past 256 positions, mutants killed; on the
  card the KV cache bytes after 64 tokens equal v9's (memcmp) and the logits gate.
- Expected: -0.09 ms [-0.06, -0.11]: 239 short over E1, 183 at 8,192.

Together, central: 4,467 to 4,027 us, about 248 tok/s short; 6.19 to 5.30 ms, about 189 at 8,192. At plan-300's 41 %:
233 and 172 (*estimated*). B2 composes with all three and stays its own two-week item.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------
That the attention wall is the prefetch: one cross-design pair (4E against the tree, 196 us) and a byte count agree
with it, nothing isolates it; E1's v9p0 arm does. The 8,192 attention time, taken from the door sweep, not stamped.
Whether gate/up rows lose speed without L2 residency in situ. L2 retention of prefetched lines under the KV stream.
The head's prune rate. Any 8B, 14B or other card.
