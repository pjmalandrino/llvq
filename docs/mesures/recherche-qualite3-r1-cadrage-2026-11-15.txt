One question decides whether N6 holds an idea: does the MMLU deficit of the sealed 4B travel in the directions the
calibration average calls cheap? The encoder prices the error in an input direction by that direction's calibration
energy (recherche-qualite3-r0-depot, section 4, fact from the code), and reverse water-filling says a correct layer
encoder gives such directions no rate (recherche-qualite3-r0-litterature, A1, *reported*). If the answer needs them,
the layer objective is mispriced against the task and a principle follows. If it does not, the quiet band of Q25 is a
symptom and N6 holds no mechanism. Nobody has measured it, here or in print (same note, E1 and NOT FOUND). N7 is, on
today's evidence, not an anomaly of generation: the dull reading is not rejected.

1. THE CLOSED PROBLEM AND ITS NUMBER
------------------------------------
Problem. On 570 MMLU questions the sealed 4B answers 66.32 % where FP16 answers 73.51 % (contrast-map lines 48-49,
*measured*), and the deficit exists when the answer is written, at depth 25: 63.9 % against 72.3 % through FP16's
readout (Q25). Which part of the stream before depth 25 carries that deficit: which directions, at which positions,
written by which sublayers?

Number. The share of the 7.19 points (*computed*, 73.51 minus 66.32) recovered at the output when a named part of FP16's stream replaces the sealed
one at a depth before 25, and the sealed blocks run on. Paired on the 570 questions. Its two ends are known: no patch
recovers 0, and FP16's first 25 blocks recover +6.12 of 6.72 on the full split (depth-restore-4b, *measured*).

The gap that makes a phase. At depth 12 and at the answer position the loud bands are intact, slopes 0.92 to 1.13
under 3 % of noise, and the error sits in bands [65, 512] and [513, 2560], slopes 0.64 and 0.47 (contrast-map
report.txt lines 71-79). Whatever the first twelve blocks cost is therefore carried, at that position, by directions
that hold 12 % of its energy (*computed*, 0.0791 and 0.0453 on the depth-12 line). What the first twelve blocks
cost is not read.

2. THE RIVALS FOR N6, AND WHAT KILLS EACH
-----------------------------------------
The R0 note states five. The lead keeps them and orders them by one experiment, the patch map.

- H6-A, the dull one: the quiet band is a symptom; the deficit rides the louder bands and the write block.
  Killed if FP16's band [513, 2560], patched at depth 12 at every position, recovers at least half of what the whole
  stream patched at depth 12 recovers.
- H6-B, a position mismatch: the matrices err most on answer-position inputs, which running text leaves quiet.
  Killed if each type's relative error on answer-position inputs is within 1.5 times its all-position value.
- H6-C, massive activations: one token or a few directions hold the trace of H in some matrices. Over all positions
  direction 1 holds 0.57 to 0.97 of the energy and reads a slope of 1.188 at depths 8 to 16 (report.txt lines 59-62).
  Killed if patching the massive positions alone at depth 12 recovers under a tenth of the whole-stream patch, and
  the pooled error of the matrices that read about 0.001 moves by less than 2 without those tokens.
- H6-D, single heads at layers 7 to 13 lose their focus. Killed if FP16's attention probabilities written into those
  layers recover under a fifth of the band deficit at depth 12.
- H6-E, the lattice MLP of layers 4 to 11 writes the band noise. No clean kill at $0: the September slices moved
  down_proj only, on an older base, and the output slope they would be read on is rescaled by the last block (Q25).
  It is read on the card by the sublayer split of the patch map.

The patch map, one card job. FP16 and the sealed f16 export side by side, as in contrast-map-4b. For each arm, FP16's
stream replaces the sealed one at a depth, on chosen positions and bands, and the sealed blocks run on. Read: accuracy
at the output and through the lens at depth 25, on the 570 questions.
  - depth profile, whole stream, every position: depths 4, 8, 12, 16, 20. Places where the deficit forms.
  - at depth 12, every position, by band: [1, 64], [65, 512], [513, 2560]. H6-A.
  - at depth 12, whole stream, by position: the answer position alone, all but it, the massive positions alone. H6-C.
  - at depth 12, band [513, 2560] at the answer position alone.
  - beside it, with no patch: the relative error of the 252 matrices on answer-position inputs (H6-B), and FP16's
    attention probabilities at layers 7 to 13 (H6-D).
Cost: about $1 (*estimated*: contrast-map-4b held both models and every tap for 918 s and $0.46; a patched forward is
one sealed pass of the 570 prompts with no tap). The script has read taps and no write tap: code first.

What each outcome opens.
  - The quiet band carries the deficit. Then the task weights directions more evenly than calibration energy does,
    and the next step is a conjecture on the encoder's weighting, to be broken at R6 before any encode.
  - The loud or middle bands carry it. H6-A stands, the quiet band leaves N6, and the phase looks at who writes the
    middle band.
  - The massive positions carry it. The idea is about a handful of rows, not a weighting.
No outcome is a mechanism by itself: a patch is a probe (Q20).

3. N7
-----
The dull reading is not rejected. Both GSM8K intervals contain their even-rate figures, +2.94 in [+2.46, +6.18] and
+6.69 in [+5.19, +9.22] (gsm8k-depth-restore-4b lines 4-6, 40-41). MMLU's blindness after depth 25 has a published
explanation and a measured one: the letter is chosen mid-depth and only sharpened after (Wiegreffe et al., *reported*;
Q25, Q26). So N7 reads today as: generation uses every block about evenly, and MMLU cannot see the last eleven.
The literature backs late blocks mattering to generation and reports small gains from treating them apart, +0.45
GSM8K for the best (LFQ, *reported*); a per-block setting is not a result here.

What would still make N7 an anomaly: the late blocks causing more than their share of the token flips of a worked
answer. That is readable at $0. The four GSM8K dumps on disk hold the greedy completions of FP16, the control, and
the two restored arms. The position where each arm first leaves FP16's text is a per-token flip hazard, on 1,319
problems, with no model. If the late restore cuts that hazard by about its weight share, 0.306, N7 closes as
explained. If it cuts it by much more, the late blocks hold a mechanism for generation, and the lens on generated
tokens (H7-C, about $0.1 to $0.3) is the next reading.
