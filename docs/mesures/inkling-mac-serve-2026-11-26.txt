WHAT THE READINGS SAY
  The prompt read slows with its length: 85 tok/s at 2,000 tokens, 60 at 9,000 (*computed*). On CUDA the score
  bias on the host was the step that grew with the square of the length; Metal still builds it on the host, so it is
  the first suspect, *estimated*: no phase profile ran here.
  Decode after 9,000 tokens is 25 % slower than after 2,000 (0.0505 against 0.0382 s a step); the fastest steps read
  0.043 s. The four full-attention layers hold every position, and the one-row score kernel reads them all.
  Memory stays low on the Mac: 9.5 to 10.7 GB after a prompt of 2,000 or 9,000 tokens, 8.03 GB after load. The
  12.87 GB of 2026-11-22 after 2,000 tokens predate the fix of the convolution state (CUDA journal of 2026-11-24).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
  Where the time of a 9,000-token prompt goes on Metal: no `--prefill-phases` run. Whether the server's first token
  at 9,000 tokens moves with the settings: the server ran with them only, there is no server arm without. The
  quality of a long prompt in pieces on Metal: no perplexity was read here (CUDA: -0.042 %, inside the band). The
  CUDA path after this change: not run on a card. The second long request of the server ran on a busy machine.
