VERDICT
-------
The tool's own gate fails. Its header requires the ratios to reproduce the
three measured MMLU gains before they count as anything, and no variant of the
statistic reproduces the ranking of the six measured arms. The best agreement
is +0.54 against the raw delta, unresolved at p = 0.297, and it turns negative
against gain per b/param, the quantity an int4 budget actually spends.

This is the seventh case in the repository where a better local proxy ranks
against the model. The six earlier ones are listed in ROADMAP-QUALITY.

Two facts survive the failure. Both the proxy and MMLU put `down_proj` first,
4.95 and +3.79. And `o_proj` is in the served 4B file for a budget reason, not
a ranking error: `down_proj` whole costs +0.4641 b/param and takes the model to
3.2286, over b_max of 3.00, where `o_proj` at +0.1954 fits at 2.9599
(q5-alloc-int4-2026-09-16.txt).
