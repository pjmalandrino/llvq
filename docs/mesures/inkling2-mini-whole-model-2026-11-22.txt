WHAT WAS RUN
  Not a preregistered gate. A reading, with no threshold stated before it.
  Harness `ops/inkling/evalfull_arms.py`, one arm a student. The teacher is the native checkpoint
  (`thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini` @ ed40e7da) in the reference forward, `transformers` 046bdb8cc2, float32, on
  the Mac's GPU (MPS). A student is the same forward with some tensors replaced.
  Text: WikiText-2 test, 8 windows of 2,048 tokens, the same 8 for every arm (fingerprint f1acc067bf1b610c).
  KL is KL(teacher || student) on the next-token distribution, mean over positions then over windows. Intervals are
  95 % bootstraps over the 8 windows, 10,000 draws. Top-1 is the share of positions where both pick the same token.
  dNLL is the student's loss minus the teacher's on the true token; the perplexity ratio is its exponential.
  Raw rows, one a window and an arm: `inkling2-mini-whole-model-2026-11-22-brut/arms/<arm>/w{0..7}.json`, and
  `arms/summary.json`.
