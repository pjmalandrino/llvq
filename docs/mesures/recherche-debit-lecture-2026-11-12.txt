WHAT THE DESCRIPTION SAYS
  1. Nothing in the bytes, the buffers or the geometry stops the Tetra words from streaming at the card's rate.
     The 0.66 of the first run is the served read pattern: one row a warp, two unaligned u32 loads a block and a
     lane, and at each row's end a reduction, a binary16 tail, a scale and a store.
  2. The three costs are not additive: each alone gives about x1.2, together x1.54, which is the card's ceiling.
     A design that removes one or two of them is not scored by this run.
  3. Staging the activation at every site, and dealing rows to warps inside a CTA rather than striding the grid,
     cost nothing measurable. An empty launch over the sites costs 53 us of the 1,492 us pword takes.
  4. The decode still has to happen. This run says what a read costs, not what a decoder on that read would reach:
     served is x0.82 of pword here as in the first run (x1.219 the other way).
