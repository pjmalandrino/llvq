1. WHAT FORM 4 CHANGES (llvq_pers.cuh section 7b, pers_4e.cu, pers_segment.cu)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Only in 4E, and only when the inputs are 16-byte aligned and the width a multiple of 8. Otherwise the scalar code
  runs, and the install refuses form 4 (below).
  - pers_load_1rt: the activation as uint4 loads, at most 3 a thread a batch (one batch for 9,728 at 512 threads), with
    the sign words and the k rows of `small` in the same batch, all issued before the first use. The halves are
    widened and signed into shared memory, as pers_rot_load does. Without signs it is the int4 down's widening.
  - pers_ln_1rt (ln1 and ln2):
    - x and alpha come as vectors, staged widened in shared memory, with `small` in the same batch;
    - candle's RMS tree is replayed from the staged x (exact_rms_scale_by: exact_rms_scale's body, which
      exact_rms_scale now calls with its global loader);
    - the apply is the same two roundings, by the thread that loaded the vector;
    - the sign words are read again after the scale, from L1.
  - pers_rot_finish<true>: the mix reads the staged rows. At k = 5 and k = 19 it is unrolled (pers_mix_column_k), each
    column chain from +0, t ascending, then + 0; other k run pers_mix_column on the staged rows.
  - Scratch: 4 KB after the row vector (PERS_4E_SCRATCH, pers::p4e_scratch_bytes(4)).
  - The final norm before the head keeps the scalar path.
  Driver:
  - pers::P4E_ROWS_FORMS gains (4, "v6"). pers::P4E_BENCH_FORMS = [v4, v6] is what fusedrun LLVQ_PERS_4E now
    installs; v3 and v5 were read by the shaker. fusedrun prints "4E v6 / 4E v4, round by round".
  - persist4e_cuda::install_rows refuses form 4 when act, x_a, x_b or a layer's ln1 or ln2 is not 16-byte aligned
    (pers::p4e_v6_inputs). The kernel would take the scalar path and time as v4 under v6's name. On success it
    prints the 75 inputs checked.
  The served config is not touched: its rows field still takes v3 and v4 only.
