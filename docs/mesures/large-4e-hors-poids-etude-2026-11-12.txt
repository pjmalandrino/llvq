What the time without weights is made of (5 tokens, E1's v9 stamps, *computed* from the table):
- Byte-free phases and imbalance: 1,658 us at 5 tokens, 2,180 at 1,024, 2,633 at 4,096, 3,730 at 8,192. The brief's
  1.76 ms is the chat column of the budget journal (attention 790.9 there).
- Phases that carry a prefetch: ln1 (173.8 MB), attention (670.3 MB), inner wait and down input (315.4 MB). 1,159 MB,
  84 % of the 1,378 MB of weights, cross the DRAM during "byte-free" phases (*computed*, pers_4e.cu lines 261-287,
  334-351; the down prefetch in pers_segment.cu).
- Phases that leave the DRAM idle: embedding 2.9, norm/RoPE 121.7, ln2 140.6: 265 us at 5 tokens (*computed*). The
  gate/up rows read 360 GB/s against 734 at 85 % of the peak: about 640 us of half-idle DRAM a token (*computed*).
- At 1,024 and 8,192 the attention wall equals the DRAM time of its own bytes plus the prefetch at 85 %: 1,119 us
  against 1,108 measured, 2,559 against 2,646 (*computed*). At 5 and 4,096 the prefetch spills into o.
- E1 measured the consequence: removing attention's prefetch cut that phase by 201 us at 5 tokens and 94 % of it
  reappeared in o and gate/up (large-4e-e1 lines 141-144). A shorter byte-free phase pushes its prefetched bytes into
  a row phase. Only the idle 265 us, the gate/up instruction excess and the attention chain at short rows (where the
  attention job, not the DRAM, holds the wall: v9np reads 398 us for 9.3 MB, large-4e-e1 line 193) are true slack.

What bit identity binds (read in llvq_pers.cuh):
- Tetra rows: lane l adds blocks l, l+32, ... in order, the 24 products in TETRA48_ORDER, warp_sum, three spelled FMA
  sites (lines 9-17). One warp a row: splitting a row over warps changes its sum order (budget note, cut 7). f32 dot on
  the f32 rotated activation.
- int4 rows: q4_staged_dot's order, explicit intrinsics (line 18).
- Rotations: rot_apply's butterfly stages in the same order and the same per-output chains, f32 in shared memory
  (lines 19-21, pers_rot_finish line 526).
- Norms: candle's RMSNorm tree reproduced (1,024 virtual threads, xor butterfly, two f16 roundings, lines 558-646).
  f16 add and mul as f32 RN then f2h; SiLU from a 65,536-entry table filled by candle.
- Attention: 8-position tiles aligned to absolute positions, 64-position units, 256-position splits, folded left to
  right, the fixed ex2 polynomial, p in f32 (lines 1463-1534). This is what fixes "one unit's tiles in order" as the
  critical path at short rows (plan-300.md, step 3, "not coded").
- Buffers between phases: residual x_a / x_b, q_out, ctx and the KV cache are binary16; rotated activations f32 in
  shared memory. Head logits binary16, argmax on a 64-bit key.
- Not bound by identity, and already tried at the bit: where prefetches sit (E1), guards in the tile (v9ft), the row
  loop (v5, K4 arms), every CTA recomputing ln and rotation (a design choice that saves a grid wait).

2. CUTS THAT OPEN OFF THE BIT
-----------------------------
Gains in ms a token, *estimated*, range [low, high]. Low ends apply the 41 % that 4E's versions realised of their
arithmetic (plan-300.md §3) where nothing tighter exists. "Against" names the measured result that argues against.

C1. Attention spread at short rows (K3a's second half), off the bit.
  Mechanism: units of 8, 16 or 32 positions instead of 64 (splits of 4 units), chosen by row length so that a row has
  at most 34 splits (today's scratch, ceil(8,704 / 256)) and at most 142 jobs. At 62 positions: 2 splits of 32 per KV
  head, one step a job instead of 8; at 1,024: units of 16, 16 splits, 128 jobs, 4 steps instead of 8. Units of 64 from
  4,096 up: the v9 form, bit for bit. Where the spread runs, attention's prefetch is dropped (v9np's form, x1.003 of
  v9 at 5 tokens, large-4e-e1 line 61), so the shortened phase pushes no bytes into o and gate/up beyond what v9np
  already pays. Only the fold order of f32 softmax states changes.
  Saving: 5 tokens -0.20 [-0.10, -0.30]; 1,024 -0.35 [-0.15, -0.50]; 4,096 and 8,192 0 (unchanged form). Basis: v9np's
  attention wall 398 us for a chain of 8 steps, 1.4 us a step (*computed*, line 87 of large-4e-e1); one step plus a
  2-split fold about 3.5 us a layer, 126 us a token, near plan-300's 130 us floor. At 1,024 the v9np wall is not
  measured; taken as v9's 1,108 less the 200 to 250 us E1 removed at 5 and 8,192.
  Tolerance risk: low. A reassociated f32 online softmax; the grouped form, which reorders more, read +0.0095 % at 4B
  (gqa-ppl). VRAM: 0 (scratch bounded by 34 splits a row, job count by the grid). Days: 4 to 6, with C0 below.
  Bench: fusedrun LLVQ_PERS_4E with an E1 arm v9as; tolerance needs the new mode C0.
  Against: v9ft cut the stamped attention wall at every context and the timed token kept a fraction of it at 1,024 and
  5, none at 4,096 and 8,192 (large-attn-ft-4e lines 81-97, cause not established). v8's ring halved attention's mean
  work and its slowest over mean still read 4.40 (plan-300 step 2). E1's conservation (94 %) bounds any version that
  keeps the prefetch in attention.

C2. B1's integer dot in the Tetra rows (int16 activation, dp2a), off the bit.
  Mechanism: the rotated activation quantized to int16 at the end of each rotation; Tetra rows dot in integers
  (spec-micro arm i16, pers_vrows.cu tv_vrows_i16k1). The one row phase that leaves the DRAM half idle is the target.
  Saving: -0.13 [-0.06, -0.20] at every context. Basis: i16 x1.143 [1.137-1.148] over v2 on the 252 matrices alone
  (spec-micro-2026-11-08.txt line 52); in-situ Tetra rows 1.81 ms (recherche-debit-maths.md §2): 0.226 ms at full
  transfer, 0.093 at 41 % (*computed*); less 0.01 to 0.05 for the per-rotation quantization (not in the bench timing,
  *estimated*).
  Tolerance risk: low to moderate. 91.6 % of rows identical, the rest one binary16 ulp, worst 6.4e-3 of the site's rms
  on four activations (spec-micro lines 33-41); no perplexity measured. VRAM: 0. Days: 7 to 10 (port from v2's loop
  to 4E's row form 7, three sites, plus the quantization).
  Bench: fusedrun LLVQ_PERS_4E E1 arm, plus C0.
  Against: i16 missed its own x1.15 bar; lane rotation read x0.856 (spec-micro lines 69-70); E1 found gate/up rows slow
  under foreign DRAM traffic (+178 us under 374 MB, large-4e-e1 lines 187-190), so part of an instruction saving may
  turn into a bandwidth wait.

C3. KV cache in int8 for 4E, off the bit.
  Mechanism: K and V stored int8 with per-group scales (LLVQ_KV=q8's format), dequantized in the tile; the eager
  prefill writes the same layout.
  Saving: 5 tokens 0; 1,024 -0.05 [-0.02, -0.12]; 4,096 -0.30 [-0.15, -0.48]; 8,192 -0.60 [-0.34, -0.97]. Basis: the
  marginal cost of KV bytes, (6.211 - 4.227) ms / 1.199 GB = 1.66 ms a GB (*computed*, E1), on 0.604 GB saved at 8k
  less 3 % of scales; plan-300 K8 gives 0.34 to 0.84 at 8,192.
  Tolerance risk: HIGH against this gate. The q8 cache read +0.0494 % [-0.0713, +0.1703] of paired perplexity at 4B over
  12 windows (kvq8-4b-2026-08-15.txt lines 52-53): its upper bound is outside +-0.12 %. That interval is where the
  0.12 % bar itself comes from (ETAT §4). VRAM: lower, 8k peak about 5.95 to 5.4 GB (*estimated*); b/param unchanged.
  Days: 10 to 20 (4E tile, kernel attention form, prefill, door). Bench: none in 4E today; LLVQ_KV=q8 in eager only.
  Against: the interval above; the q8 path cost 5 to 7 % of eager throughput at short context (kvq8-4b lines 142-144).

C4. int4 rows (o, v, 12 downs) split over two warps a row, off the bit.
  Mechanism: o's 18 or 19 rows on 16 warps run two rounds (budget note cut 7); halves of a row on two warps reassociate
  the sum and remove the tail round.
  Saving: -0.04 [-0.02, -0.08] at every context. Basis: o imbalance 81 us at 5 tokens, o at 589 GB/s against a floor
  of 273 us at 85 % (large-4e-budget line 58).
  Tolerance risk: negligible (f32 reassociation of one dot). VRAM 0. Days 3 to 5.
  Against: v5, which spread a CTA's last rows over its warps, read x1.001 (ETAT §6); o is DRAM bound (E1 lines 182-184),
  so a tail round mostly overlaps the bytes.

C5. Norm statistics from the producer, off the bit.
  Mechanism: the o and down epilogues write each CTA's sum of squares of its slice of x; ln2 and ln1 sum 142 partials in
  a fixed order instead of re-reading x for candle's tree. Removes one pass and one reduction an instance.
  Saving: -0.04 [-0.02, -0.07]. Basis: ln1 159 us, ln2 144 us mean without prefetch traffic, 4.4 and 4.0 us a layer
  (large-4e-e1 lines 95-97, arm v9mv); about 0.5 to 1 us of each is the tree and its barriers (*estimated*).
  Tolerance risk: negligible. VRAM 0. Days 3 to 5. Against: no stamp separates norm from rotation; K2 already took
  ln1 from 320 to 227 us at the bit.

Examined and rejected:
- f16 or int8 activations between phases. They already are binary16 in global memory (x_a, q_out, ctx); each CTA reads
  5 KB from L2 a phase, 0.73 MB across the grid. Nothing to cut. Inside the row phase, int16 is C2.
- Attention scores in f16, or MMA for the K/V dot in decode. At short rows the wall is the chain of steps (C1), not
  arithmetic; at 1,024 and up v9's attention runs at the DRAM floor of its bytes (section 1). One query row with 4
  query heads a KV head fills a quarter of an m16n8k16 tile. The MMA study (large-prefill-attn-mma-etude) prices MMA
  for the 32-row prefill, not for one row.
- MMA for the int4 rows. Batch 1 is a matvec; the int4 rows read 589 to 648 GB/s, bound by bytes.
- Rotations folded or at lower precision. Folding into the weights changes stored values (out). An f16x2 butterfly
  halves FP instructions in a phase held by about ten __syncthreads and an L2 round trip; no stamp isolates the
  butterfly, and its error, about 2^-11 relative a stage, is coarser than C2's int16. Not ranked.
- Shortlist lm head. The head is already int4 g64, tied, 218.8 MB in 367 us at 595 GB/s: bytes. A shortlist needs a
  proxy (extra bytes, or low rank: out), and paired perplexity cannot certify it: a target outside the list has no
  logit. The argmax-exact two-pass head of the budget note (cut 6, -0.05 [0, -0.08]) stays the only head cut, and it
  needs no license.
- Fewer grid waits, counters, CUDA Graphs. Off the bit changes nothing here: 0.029 ms at most for barriers (recherche
  §7), K3b under 0.06, A2 closed by rule 3.
- KV in int4. C3's risk, larger: 1.26 / 0.52 ms at 8,192 in plan-300 K8, against a q8 interval already outside the band.

Bit-identical cuts still standing (they compose): F, norm/RoPE/append folded into the attention jobs, -0.09 [-0.06,
-0.11]; W, gate/up claimed across the grid on v9np, -0.10 [-0.05, -0.15] (re-based from v9np, large-4e-e1 lines 209-212).

5. WHAT THIS NOTE DID NOT CHECK
-------------------------------
- No phase was stamped at 1,024 or 4,096 except attention; those columns are means of 5 and 8,192.
- v9np's attention wall at 1,024 is assumed (v9's less 200 to 250 us), not measured. C1's 1,024 figure rests on it.
- The step cost of 1.4 us is a quotient of a wall by a step count; a step at units of 8 or 16 may cost more than its
  share (smaller tiles, the same 16 KB staging granularity).
- C2's quantization cost and its in-situ transfer: never measured inside 4E. B1's perplexity: never measured.
