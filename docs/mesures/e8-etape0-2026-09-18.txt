WHY THIS RAN
-------------
`docs/arbitrage-e8-2026-09-18.md` rests its whole case on the ratio of two
literature constants that this repository never transcribed. Its section 2 says
so and calls verifying them stage 0. The operator funded option A on 2026-09-18.

THE CONTROL, FIRST
-------------------
A Monte-Carlo second moment can be wrong in the estimator rather than in the
lattice. So the same harness measures the integer lattice, whose G is exactly
1/12, with a decoder that is one `round`.

  10,000,000 draws, uniform over [0,1)^8
  E|e|^2 = 0.666762 +- 0.000067   G = 0.083345   against 1/12 = 0.083333
  gap +0.014 %

The estimator is sound. What follows is about the lattice.

THE RESULT
-----------
  10,000,000 draws, uniform over [0,2)^8, Conway and Sloane decoder
  E|e|^2 = 0.573468 +- 0.000040   G(E8) = 0.071683
  cited 0.071682, gap **+0.002 %**, +0.29 standard errors

Sampling over [0,2)^8 needs no Voronoi cell: it is a fundamental domain of
(2Z)^8, which sits inside E8, so the draw is uniform modulo E8.

  ratio G(E8)/G(Leech) = 1.0899 (*cited* denominator)
  Leech leads by 9.0 % of MSE, 0.374 dB

The dossier's bound survives its first check. Half of it is now measured here.

WHAT IS STILL ONLY CITED
-------------------------
G(Leech) = 0.065771. This repository has no infinite-Lambda24 decoder: every
search is shell-capped (`nearest_ball13`, `shell_bests`, `nearest_in_shell2`).
Verifying it needs a full Leech decoder, which nothing else here wants, so the
denominator of the 9.0 % stays a citation.

THE INVARIANTS, EXACT
----------------------
Doubled coordinates make every one of these an integer identity: a point is
stored as y = 2x, and E8 becomes one predicate, all y_i of one parity and
sum(y_i) = 0 mod 4.

  kissing number                    240, split 112 integer / 128 half-integer
  theta coefficients, norms 2 to 10 240, 2160, 6720, 17520, 30240 = 240 sigma3(n)
  cumulative to norm 10             56,880
  minimum |x|^2                     2
  membership                        all 56,880 enumerated points pass `in_e8`
  near misses                       four hand-built vectors refused or accepted
  decoder against brute force       2,000 draws, exact agreement
  decoder stays in the lattice      5,000 draws over a wide box
  covolume                          |det| of a standard basis = 1

Eight tests, all exact, no tolerance except the two float determinant and
distance comparisons at 1e-9 and 1e-12.

FIVE MUTANTS, FIVE DEAD
------------------------
Each mutation was counted at its site before it ran, so a pattern that matched
nothing cannot read as a survivor.

  sum mod 4 becomes sum mod 2            killed
  the parity repair removed              killed
  the flip takes the smallest error      killed
  the coset choice inverted              killed
  the origin kept in the enumeration     killed

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
- Nothing about the model. No weight was encoded, no activation read.
- G is the high-rate asymptotic figure of merit. At 2 bits per dimension with
  shape-gain, neither lattice operates in that regime, which is why stage 1
  scores a real codebook on real blocks instead of trusting this number.
- The b/weight column assumes an integer index per E8 block and ignores what a
  real packing pays in addressing.
- G(Leech) stays cited.
