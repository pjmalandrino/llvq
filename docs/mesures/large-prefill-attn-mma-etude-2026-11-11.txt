0. WHAT THIS STANDS ON
----------------------
*Measured*, L40S, sealed 4B (large-prefill-r32-2026-11-11.txt unless named):
- a12ipg32 first token 0.448 / 2.681 / 7.800 s at 1,024 / 4,096 / 8,191; phase peaks 2.318 / 2.855 / 3.560 GB.
- 32-row pass at 8k: 45.149 ms, attention 34.310 ms, the rest 10.839 ms (medians of 2 stamped passes).
- Served eager phase peaks 2.356 GB at 1,024, 5.946 GB at 8,191 (large-prefill-2026-11-11.txt).
- D1's attention model: a step costs F = 2.92 us plus V = 1.22 us a row; F matches 142 CTAs x 16 KB at 864 GB/s
  (2.69 us). F and V add, they do not overlap (rows study section 5, *computed* from measured stamps).
- Chain against eager, paired ppl +0.0007 % [-0.0078, +0.0092], 12 windows of 2,048 (large-prefill-2026-11-11.txt).
*Computed*:
- KV bytes a position a layer: 8 KV heads x 128 x 2 B x 2 = 4,096 B. At 8,192 positions, 36 layers: 1.208 GB.
- m16n8k16 is 2,048 MAC. The L40S's 362 TFLOPS dense f16 with f32 accumulation is 128 MAC a clock a sub-partition
  at 2.52 GHz: 16 cycles an MMA, the audit's 16.2.

1. HOW THE SCALAR TILE COMPUTES TODAY, AND IN WHAT ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------
Read in llvq_pers.cuh (pers_attn_tile, pers_attn_merge1, pers_attention) and pers_4et_a12g.cu (p4g_attention).
Geometry: positions in 8-position tiles aligned to absolute positions, 64-position units, 256-position splits. A
warp holds two query heads, one a half-warp; lane l16 of a half owns dimensions 8 l16 .. 8 l16 + 7.
Per tile, for one query head:
- score j: t = fma(q_i, k_ji, t) for i = 0..7 from 0 on each lane, then t += shfl_xor(t, off) for off = 8, 4, 2, 1,
  then s_j = t x c. Every add is one RN in f32, in that tree.
- tile max: s_0, then fmaxf over j = 1..7. New max mn = fmaxf(m, mt). carry = ex2(m - mn), the fixed polynomial
  pers_ex2 (6 FMA), never the hardware ex2.
- p_j = ex2(s_j - mn) in f32; ts summed j = 0..7 in order; d' = d x carry + ts.
- a_i = a_i x carry, then a_i = fma(p_j, v_ji, a_i) for j = 0..7 in order. p_j stays f32.
Then:
- units: each of the split's 4 units runs its 8 tiles in order into its own state (m, d, a[128]);
- split: the 4 unit states folded in unit order by pers_attn_merge1 (two ex2, two products, one add each);
- row: the split states written to `part`, and the job that draws the last ticket of (row, KV head) folds the splits
  in ascending order, 8 a round trip, then out = f2h(a / d).
Each row's bits are a function of its length alone. That is why D1 (groups of 4 rows) and R = 32 kept them.

What an MMA formulation changes, and why no form keeps the bits:
- Score: S = Q K^T by m16n8k16 over 8 k-steps of 16 dimensions. Inside one MMA the 16 products are summed by the
  tensor core in its own fused order and precision (not documented; llvq-cuda/tests/host_mma.h says the same and
  models only which operands meet). Today's score is a fixed tree of 8 sequential fma and 4 butterfly adds. No
  sequence of MMAs reproduces that tree's roundings.
- PV: the A operand of the second MMA is P in binary16. Today p_j enters the fma in f32. Rounding p to binary16 is a
  relative error up to 2^-11 a weight that today's path never makes. A hi/lo split (P = hi + lo, two binary16, two
  MMAs) brings it near 2^-22, still not zero.
- Softmax order: the max and the sum run over the MMA tile's keys, held 2 a lane across a quad, then reduced by quad
  shuffles. The online rescale moves from every 8 positions to every MMA tile.
- An exact dot product (integer MMA on split mantissas) would round once; today's chain rounds 12 times. Exact is
  not today's bits either.
Verdict: no. Identity with a12ipg32 is out of reach for any MMA form. What can be kept: the split fold, its order and
its function, and the arm's own identity across R and job shapes (section 4, gate C1).

2. THE GATE: THE SERVED ATTENTION TOLERANCE
-------------------------------------------
Precedent (docs/ETAT.md section 4, operator's gate of 2026-11-01): forms not bit for bit with `expanded` are served
under paired WikiText-2 perplexity within +-0.12 %. Grouped against expanded read +0.0095 % [-0.0048, +0.0238] by
decode over 12 windows of 4,096 (gqa-ppl-2026-11-01, LLVQ_GQA_PPL=4096,12 in fusedrun: the same windows scored by both
forms, by prefill and by teacher-forced decode, window NLLs paired, 95 % t interval). The kernel form (attn_dec.cu's
scalar arithmetic, the one this tile reproduces) read +0.0018 % against grouped over 12 windows of 4,096
(attn-t8-below). The closest precedent is that one: a different attention arithmetic, same band.
What an MMA prefill attention must pass, with prefillbench (no new binary):
- Q1. --ppl 2048,12 --pair-ppl against the existing eager dump (large-prefill-2026-11-11-brut/nll-eager.jsonl, same
  sealed file, config and windows). The chain read +0.0007 % there. Whole interval inside +-0.12 %.
- Q2. --ppl 4096,12 for the MMA arm and for a12ipg32, paired. 2,048 never reaches the long splits where the new
  summation runs over thousands of keys; 4,096 is the precedent's length. Whole interval inside +-0.12 %. a12ipg32
  is the reference here because it is the chain's bits and its process peak stays low (eager's window_nll took
  6.485 GB at 2,048).
- Q3. The 47 texts paired with a12ipg32's (--pair-texts): every divergence at a near-tie, margin at most 0.25 in
  logit, the definition of the first prefill prereg. Expect some divergences: identity no longer holds.
- Q4. GSM8K-200, 4E decoding after an MMA chain prefill against the eager prefill, exact McNemar, not separated.
  This pairing is owed by the chain anyway (r32 journal, WHAT IS OWED); run it once, on the arm that will be served.
  It needs a door: the chain prefill is not wired to the gsm8k binary today.
- Reading, not gate: max |dlogit| of the last row against a12ipg32. The grouped form's prediction on that reading
  missed by x100 (13.13 against 0.12) inside a passing ppl band; sign no interval on it.

3. TWO DESIGNS
--------------
Design A, in the persistent kernel, a new arm a12ipg32m (a text edit of a12ipg32 like ft: p4g_attention replaced by
p4m_attention, the rest of the pass untouched, its module digest pinned beside its parent's).
- Job: (split s of 256 positions, KV head h), every row of the pass in it. At R = 32 and 4 query heads a KV head the
  query block is 128 x 128, binary16 (q_out is binary16 already, so staging it is exact). At 8k: 32 x 8 = 256 jobs a
  layer, 2 rounds over 142 CTAs, 16 steps a CTA a layer.
- Warps: 8 compute warps, each owns 16 query vectors (4 rows x 4 heads) for the whole split; 4 warps issue the
  cp.async of the next stage, as the ring does today. At R < 32 the idle tiles are skipped.
- Step: 32 keys staged, K then V, 16 KB, a 2-stage ring: today's PERS_STAGE and its bytes.
  QK: A = Q from shared (ldmatrix), B = K, which is column-major as stored ([pos][128]): 4 n8 tiles x 8 k-steps = 32
  MMA a warp. Scale by c in f32 (today's s = t x c). Tile max per query: 2 values a lane, 2 quad shuffles. New max,
  carry = pers_ex2_sel(m - mn) (the same polynomial as today). p = pers_ex2_sel(s - mn) in f32, row sum in f32, then
  P packed to binary16. The S accumulator's layout is the A fragment of the next MMA (register reuse, FlashAttention-2
  style). PV: B = V by ldmatrix.trans, 16 n8 tiles x 2 k-steps = 32 MMA a warp.
- f16 inputs, f32 accumulation (mma.sync.m16n8k16.f32.f16.f16.f32, the weight rows' instruction, p4t_mma).
- Registers a compute lane: O 16 x 128 f32 = 64, S 16 x 32 f32 = 16, P 8, max and sum 4, transient fragments and
  addresses about 25: about 120 of 168 (*estimated*). The kernel is one symbol, so its count is the max over phases:
  the risk is spills elsewhere, not here. Q is read from shared each step, not held (32 registers saved).
- Shared: Q 128 x 136 halves (padded) 34,816 B, 2 stages of 32 keys x 2 x 136 halves 34,816 B, the 32-row visibility
  mask 128 B: 69,760 B, inside a12ipg32's 74,240 B attention area. The dynamic area stays 93,568 B of 101,376.
- Causal mask inside the pass: the job of the split that holds pos0 stages positions [base, pos0) from the cache and
  the pass's tree slots [0, R) from tk_l / tv_l once (not per row as p4t_attn_issue does today). Row r sees slot j iff
  j is on its path (chain: j <= depth[r]), a 32-bit mask a row built from L.path. Masked scores are set to -inf before
  the max. A tile row fully masked keeps m = -inf; the ex2 argument then uses 0, so P = 0 and no NaN.
- Fold: each (row, head) split state written to `part` in today's layout ([row][n_kv][split][groups][130] floats,
  already sized for 32 rows). Today the last ticket of (row, h) folds. Here one job carries all 32 rows of h, so the
  8 jobs of the last split would draw every last ticket and fold 32 rows each on 8 CTAs: about 6.9 ms a pass
  (*estimated*, 32 folds x 6 us x 36 layers). So the fold becomes its own sub-phase after one grid wait: 256 (row, h)
  folds a layer dealt over 142 CTAs, pers_attn_merge1 in ascending split order and f2h(a / d) as today. Its function
  and order are kept; its inputs are not.
- Short context: at pos0 below 2,048 a layer has at most 64 jobs, under 142 CTAs. Fix: at few splits, a job takes a
  group of query tiles instead of all 8, the group count a function of pos0 only. Bytes rise there, where they are
  small. Each query's own operations do not depend on which tiles share its job (gate C1 checks it).
- Bytes on the card: none. No buffer moves, no new allocation. Peaks 2.318 / 2.855 / 3.560 GB expected, at or under
  eager's 2.356 / 5.946 GB.

Design B, A with P split hi + lo: two PV MMAs, +8 registers, PV's error from 2^-11 to about 2^-22 relative. MMA work
x1.5. Build it as a compile flag of A and run both in Q2 if A's interval is not well inside the band.

Rejected: the attention as its own launch between phases. The pass is one persistent launch; cutting it at every
layer's attention adds 72 launches and restarts a pass (no CUDA Graphs here). About 0.3 to 0.7 ms a pass of launches
alone (*estimated* at 4 to 10 us each), and the cost is paid at every context. It would free registers (own symbol,
up to 255). Fallback only if A spills.

What would make it wrong:
- F was fitted on D1's steps; one job serving 32 rows may run its compute serially behind the loads (the high edge
  assumes so) or worse if the 2-stage ring starves.
- the fold sub-phase and its grid wait are priced, never measured; `part` traffic (17 MB a layer at 32 rows, 1.2 GB
  a pass written and read) is assumed L2-resident;
- register spills of the one symbol at 168 of 170; ldmatrix bank conflicts; the driver ptxas's schedule;
- the short-context floor f: at 1,024 it decides whether MMA beats a12ipg32 at all;
- the 362 TFLOPS rate is a datasheet figure. Even at half rate the MMA term stays under the byte term.
Unmeasured terms: the fold sub-phase, the softmax's latency factor, f, the overlap of compute and loads.

5. THE ONE-ROW 4E DECODE AT LONG CONTEXT
----------------------------------------
No. One row has 4 query vectors a KV head: an m16 tile would be 25 % filled, and nothing else can share the staged
keys. The decode reads each cache byte once a token already. Its long-context cost is bytes: 1.208 GB at 8,192 is
1.40 ms at 864 GB/s against a growth of 1.985 ms from 5 to 8,192 positions (*computed* from 236.6 and 161.0 tok/s).
The ceiling at full bandwidth is about 178 tok/s at 8,192 (*computed*). The full tile (4E v9ft, job written) already
aims there; the full-tile note's upper edge, 190 tok/s, is above that ceiling and cannot hold. MMA would also take
the served decode off its bits for no byte saved.

6. COST, GATES, JOBS
--------------------
Build, design A with B as a flag: 14 to 18 agent-hours (*estimated*).
- p4m_attention (MMA steps, mask, online softmax, fold sub-phase, short-context grouping): 6 to 7 h.
- arm a12ipg32m, TreeForm, module slot, digests, prefillbench names: 1.5 h.
- host twin: host_mma.h gives the operand map, not the card's sums. Add an ldmatrix model, or load fragments with LDS
  (simpler, a little slower): 3 to 4 h.
- mutants and compile probe: 2.5 h. Prereg and job scripts: 1.5 h.
Mac gates ($0):
- M1. Compile probe (ops/tree-sass-probe.sh, sm_89): at most 170 registers at 384 threads, no local operation inside
  the attention loop, local no larger than a12ipg32's 728 B, shared at most 101,376 B. HMMA count as written.
- M2. Host twin against a12ipg32 at R = 1, 5, 16, 32 and pos0 9, 250, 500, 1,000: every row's attention output
  within a relative bound (proposed 2^-9 of the output's scale), argmax equal on the logits. Not identity: the host
  MMA sums in RN order, which the card does not.
- M3. Host twin against itself: R = 32 equals R = 16 equals R = 1 bit for bit, and the short-context grouping at two
  group counts gives the same rows.
- M4. Mutants, each one site, counted: mask off by one (slot j visible to depth j - 1); P not rescaled by carry;
  V fragment read with K's layout (no .trans); fold in descending split order. Each must break M2 or M3.
Card jobs (l40sx1 at $1.80/h, *estimated* from D1 and R32's 19 min with three modules):
- C1 speed and closeness: a12ipg32m (and its B flag) against a12ipg32 at 1,024 / 4,096 / 8,191, 3 timed rounds, the
  8k phase stamps, the phase peaks, the 47 texts paired, the arm's own identity R = 32 against R = 16 on the card
  (the tensor core's sums are deterministic for a fixed tile; the card must show that a query's bits do not depend on
  the other rows of its tile). About 25 min, $0.75, timeout 30 min ($0.90).
- C2 quality: Q1 and Q2 (--ppl 2048,12 and 4096,12, two arms, two processes). About 25 min, $0.75.
- C3 GSM8K-200 (Q4), once a door exists, on the arm to serve. About 25 min, $0.75.
Total about $2.25, $2.70 at worst, over 3 jobs.
Speed gate to sign: first token at 8,191 at most 4.5 s, and at most the full tile's measured first token less 1 s.

7. RECOMMENDATION
-----------------
Stop at the full tile now, and run its job (large-prefill-ft.sh, $0.84). Reasons:
- the full tile keeps the bits and is built and gated; MMA gives up identity for the whole prefill and needs Q1 to
  Q4 and a door before it can be served;
- at 1,024 tokens MMA gains nothing over the full tile, and most chat prompts are short;
- the gain over the full tile is 1.2 to 2.4 s at 8,191 only, and this note's interval is not measured.
Build MMA if the full tile's job shows its 8k 32-row pass still above 15 ms of attention, and the operator wants
long prompts (4k and up) served. Then design A, with B ready as a flag.
