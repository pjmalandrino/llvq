WHY IT WAS BUILT, AND WHAT DID NOT NEED BUILDING
-------------------------------------------------
The Schur pilot of 2026-09-14 already satisfies the six items the diagnostic
asks a dump to carry. Verified field by field on a 4B row: original weights
(`diagnostic.original`), the quantizer's compensated input
(`snapshots[].working`), the served reconstruction with its codes and gain
(`witness`, `witness_codes`, `branches[].gain`), the conditional metric with
its provenance (`branches[].continuous_lower_bound` over a fingerprinted
Hessian file), calibration and held-out activations in SEPARATE files
(`hessian.f64le` and `validation.f64le`), and the seed, the visit order, rho_H
and the pinned checkpoint. Nothing in the schema was missing.

What was missing is coverage. The pilot is Qwen3-0.6B, two projection families,
three layers, four rows, two seeds. The served object is the 4B.

THE DEFECT, MEASURED BEFORE ANYTHING IS READ FROM THE SET
----------------------------------------------------------
At the pilot's volume every Hessian at the 4B is rank-deficient. Samples per
dimension, 2,048 tokens over n (*computed*): 0.80 for q_proj and gate_proj,
0.50 for o_proj, **0.21 for down_proj**. What makes those matrices invertible is
the damping, not the data.

The v64 arm prices that. Same cell, same seed, same rotation, same held-out set,
eight times the calibration:

  family            held-out regret 2,048   at 16,384   ratio
  self_attn.q_proj          4.725e-04       1.677e-04    2.8
  self_attn.o_proj          1.625e-03       4.577e-04    3.6
  mlp.gate_proj             6.012e-04       1.652e-04    3.6
  mlp.down_proj             4.770e-03       2.517e-04   19.0

The held-out regret falls in every family, by 2.8 to 19 times. On the
calibration side the direction is not consistent: q_proj falls 2.1 times and
gate_proj 3.4, while o_proj rises 1.2 and down_proj rises 5.0.

So a regret read at the pilot's volume is mostly a statement about the sample
deficit. The high-volume arm is the one later work should read, and the
low-volume arm is kept as the control that compares to the 0.6B pilot.

WHAT THIS PUTS UNDER SUSPICION, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT REFUTE
------------------------------------------------------------
The alternative-directions run of 2026-09-16 read its held-out number off the
pilot's dumps, at the pilot's volume. Its -8.763 % was therefore scored against
activations whose regret scale this file measures as 2.8 to 19 times too large.

That is a flag, not a refutation. A gain ratio and a regret magnitude are not
the same quantity, and nothing here recomputes that run. Recomputing it on the
v64 arm is the cheapest way to find out, and it is not done in this file.

NAMING
-------
The quantity the branches carry is a LOCAL activation metric over one
projection, computed from a calibration Hessian with damping. It is J_local. It
is not the model's loss, it is not perplexity, and no MMLU statement follows
from it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
- Nothing about quality. No token was evaluated, no arm was scored.
- Three layers of 36 and four rows of thousands. The rows are chosen by
  `positions`, on dimensions alone, before any outcome exists.
- One model. The 0.6B pilot and this set share a schema, not a conclusion.
- `mlp.up_proj` and `self_attn.k_proj` are absent, and `down_proj` sits at one
  layer of three.
