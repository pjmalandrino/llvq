THE VERDICT
-----------
Refuted as proposed. It survives only as a $0 Stage 0 on the Mac, under a go.

The continuous half of K1 has already run on the sealed 4B and moved MMLU by +0.02 pp. The claim therefore rests
entirely on two things nobody has measured: the Tetra membership rate of a lattice step, and a corpus effect that
the same night's P3 estimates at -0.5 to +1.0 MMLU. The cost is understated by a factor of 2 to 4, because the 4B
does not fit an L40S with weight gradients and the 14B does not fit one H200. The Stage 2 gate as written cannot
credit the lattice step and does not enforce the no-trade-off rule. My interval is MMLU 0 to +1.5 and GSM8K -1 to
+4 at 4B. Neither reaches the note's own bar of +3.4 MMLU or +4.8 GSM8K.

1. ALREADY TRIED OR CLOSED?  Holds on the letter, refuted on the substance.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No form of code-moving training was ever run. llvqtune's invariant forbids it: "the gradient never reaches the
  decoded directions" (ops/llvqtune/README.md:63). K1 is new on that axis.
- CLAUDE.md rule 3 does not forbid it. PV-tuning and Block-AP are discarded in ROADMAP-QUALITY rows 21 and 22
  (lines 80-81), not in ETAT §6 (lines 208-228). No §6 row covers code moves or the training corpus.
- End-to-end KL training of the continuous parameters on the sealed 4B was measured. Row scales plus the 73 RMSNorm
  vectors took the KL from 0.2622 to about 0.20 and moved MMLU +0.02 pp [-0.31, +0.36], p = 0.93 (*measured*,
  sealed-rownorms-2026-09-23.txt:19-45). K1's P phase is that run plus centroids and tails. Its continuous half is
  measured null on the very file K1 starts from.
- Training on non-generic text was prepared once and never run. The operator cancelled the mmlu-aux arm in
  SCHEDULING on 2026-09-23, billed $0.00. The reason written was priority, not contamination: "the operator's target
  is memory, not the training corpus" (proofs/preregistration-mmluaux-4b-rowscales-2026-09-23-ECARTS.md:12-13).
  The objection applies unchanged to a self-generated corpus. K1 is a training-corpus lever, and it buys no byte.
- The frame in force since 2026-11-11 is throughput, with quality, b/param and VRAM held fixed (operator memory
  cadre-debit-voir-large). A quality campaign is outside it. Tonight's P3 calls the corpus "the model's own generic
  output" (recherche-methode-quantification-2026-11-11.txt:204-206). It is distillation text aimed at GSM8K, which
  the same frame says never to report alone.

2. THE NO-TRADE-OFF RULE.  Unproven, and the gate as written is refuted.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every time the training text or the trained set changed, the metrics moved apart.
- DCLM row scales: perplexity to 1.0074 x f16 while MMLU stays nine points under (*measured*,
  dclm-rowscales-2026-09-20.txt:129-147). Both rose, by unrelated amounts.
- Second training of the sealed file: KL -23 %, MMLU +0.02 (sealed-rownorms:39-44).
- L01 radial constant: MMLU +1.23 stratified, +0.13 unweighted, perplexity +9.5 % (ROADMAP-QUALITY:152).
- int4 embedding: MMLU -0.35, perplexity +1.52 % (ROADMAP-QUALITY:63).
- Calibration seed 1: worst perplexity, best MMLU (ROADMAP-QUALITY:68).
- Our 4B reads 6.9 % under AWQ in perplexity and 4.76 MMLU under it (ETAT:101-102, 92).
No case of MMLU rising while GSM8K falls is resolved in the repository. GSM8K was never read before and after any
training run, so the cross-metric risk of a GSM8K-aimed corpus is unmeasured in both directions.

The note contradicts its neighbour. K1 credits the corpus with +0.5 to +1.5 MMLU (centre +1.8 minus the discrete
+0.5 to +1.5, innovation-encodeur-reseau:102-104). P3, written the same night on the same corpus, estimates
MMLU -0.5 to +1.0 (recherche-methode-quantification:193). Two thirds chat and reasoning text also moves the
student off WikiText-2, the perplexity column of ETAT §2. Nothing bounds that move.

Selection contamination is measured here. Paper 2's one held-out check of a test-selected choice read +3.12 on the
2,280 questions that selected it and +1.55 on the 11,762 others (paper2/sections/experiments.tex:305-317). A
recipe with τ, V-phase period, mix ratio and step count tuned on MMLU or GSM8K test would inherit up to that factor
of two. The prompt pool has no overlap gate against GSM8K test; ops/mmlu_aux_overlap.py covers MMLU only. The
format lever is small: 8 cap stops and 7 last-number extractions on 1,319 (*measured*, jobs.csv gsm8k-4b-served),
so at most about 1.1 pp of GSM8K is formatting.

The Stage 2 bar reads "+4.8 GSM8K **or** +3.4 MMLU" (innovation-encodeur-reseau:153). That is a best-of-two
criterion, and it admits a file that gains GSM8K and loses MMLU or perplexity.

Honest protocol. Freeze every hyper-parameter on the 0.6B by held-out KL. Gate the prompt pool against GSM8K test
and MMLU test, dev and validation by n-gram overlap before generation. Select only on MMLU validation (1,531
questions, which no harness reads today: mmlu.rs:668-669 loads test and dev) and a held-out slice of GSM8K train.
Touch the test splits once. Adopt only if all hold: MMLU paired lower bound >= 0, GSM8K paired lower bound >= 0,
WikiText-2 paired perplexity not above +0.5 %, held-out chat KL not above the sealed file's. Cost of the
validation code: about a day, $0. Validation scoring adds about $0.10 an arm (*estimated*, 1,531 / 14,042 of
$0.70).

3. NOISE.  Refuted as designed.
-------------------------------
- Pairing against the sealed file is valid for one claim: this file beats that file. The paired CI95 half-width is
  about 0.6 pp of MMLU (*measured*, embed-q4-swap; gains-criteres-qualite:22-25) and 1.3 to 1.9 of GSM8K.
- It is not valid for the recipe. METHODE §3 (lines 62-65) reads anything that recalibrates against the draw's σ,
  and asks for two draws for a smaller effect. K1 moves codes and re-encodes up to 1 % of blocks a V phase with the
  production encoder. The draw σ is 1.7 pp on the served DCLM recipe on one degree of freedom, 2.92 on Planes14
  (revue-structurelle-2026-10-20:36-40; bruit-mmlu-graines). K1's own Stage 1 admits it: "the three draws say
  whether the effect survives a change of encoding, which one encode cannot" (innovation-encodeur-reseau:148).
  Stage 2 then drops the draws at 4B.
- The training-seed spread of llvqtune is unknown. Every training journal says "one seed, one draw"
  (sealed-rownorms:66). The only bracket: a retraining of scales and norms flipped 4.2 % of answers, which cancelled
  (SE 0.17), and two encodings of one recipe flipped 28.1 % (σ 1.7). A file whose codes move sits between.
- What it takes to credit the lattice step, B - A > 0 at CI95, with n seeds a arm (*computed*, two-sided,
  SE = σ_s √(2/n)): at σ_s = 0.3, n = 1 resolves +1 pp. At σ_s = 0.5, n = 2. At σ_s = 1.0, n = 8. The note's
  "second seed of arm A when B - A lies under 1 pp" measures nothing about B's spread.
- Cost of that: a B arm is $14 to $22 and an A arm about $6 (section 4), plus $2.83 of MMLU and GSM8K a file. Three
  seeds a arm: about $80 to $105 at 4B. Eight: about $200 to $280.

4. COST AND TIME.  Refuted.
---------------------------
- The l40sx1 rate is $1.80 an hour (130 min billed for $3.90, tetranu-rowscales row of jobs.csv). The h200 is $4.97
  an hour (118 min for $9.78, dclm-14b-rowscales). At $1.80, 4.5 h is $8.1, which matches the note. The note
  prices it on the L40S.
- The 4B does not fit an L40S with weight gradients. Measured row-scale peaks: 72.65 GB at 8B, 119.4 GB at 14B, at
  2,048 tokens a step (dclm-8b-rowscales:74; dclm-14b-rowscales:111). A line through them gives about 43 GB at 4B
  (*computed*). The note adds three bf16 buffers on 2.86 G Tetra weights, 17.2 GB, so about 60 GB of 48. The
  note's 39 GB assumes activation checkpointing. llvqtune's --grad-checkpoint flag is inert, since the model stays
  in eval() (ops/jobs/dclm-14b-rowscales.sh:63), so every measured peak ran without it. Making it work adds one
  forward a step, so it costs time either way.
- On an H200 the 4B costs about $14 to $22 for training alone (*computed*, 2.8 to 4.5 h at $4.97), against the
  note's $8 to $9. The V phases are host-bound and do not shrink on a faster card.
- The on-policy phase, "the student writes, the teacher scores", generates from a dense f16 export in
  transformers. It is not priced.
- The 14B does not fit one H200: 119.4 GB plus 3 x 26.4 GB of buffers on about 13.2 G Tetra weights is about 198
  GB of 141 to 150 (*computed*). Offline teacher logits remove about 29.5 GB and leave about 169. The note's $36 is
  for a run that cannot start on its flavor. The 32B needs two cards or host offload, and it has never been
  encoded (ETAT:143-145). The bar's "carries to larger models" fails at 14B on a single card.
- Reference chains: row scales alone cost $4.00 at 4B (9,507 steps, 1.71 h), $5.66 at 8B (9,507 steps, 3,797 s on
  h200) and $9.78 at 14B (9,507 steps, 7,040 s). The 8B chain was $10.19 with its census. The 14B chain was $32.17,
  of which encoding $15.35, training $9.78, census $1.95 (jobs.csv; HISTORIQUE 2026-09-22 and 23).
- The row-scale gain was acquired before step 1,210 of 9,681 (tetranu-rowscales:52-53) and the second training's KL
  flattened inside the first eighth (sealed-rownorms:39-41). Ten thousand steps buys V phases, not continuous gain.
- Build. llvqtune has row_scales, row_norms, free_params (scales and tails, never run) and low_rank
  (ops/llvqtune/README.md:23-30). Centroids are not trainable there. K1 needs gradient EMAs on the weights, a
  tangent projection, a V-phase driver, a Rust closed-form screen, membership, a Newton re-encode driver and a
  `codeswap` binary, none of which exist. The trainer split columns in the natural basis against a rotated tail
  once already (E8, dclm-rowscales:118-120). Two to three weeks is plausible for the code. It excludes the
  0.6B gates and the mutant suites METHODE requires.
- The spend cap in force is $25 from the go of 2026-11-09, with $12.66 spent on 2026-11-11 evening (operator memory
  cadre-debit-voir-large). The 4B program at the note's own $25 to $40 exceeds what is left.

5. DOES ANYTHING ELSE MOVE?  Holds for serving, refuted for production and publication.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Byte widths are unchanged, so b/param, VRAM, decode speed and first token do not move by construction. The
  codebook_fingerprint pins the index map, not the word values, and survives. The S grid needs c1/c0 in
  [1/3.14, 3.14]; the note clamps it. These hold.
- The object changes. A moved code is a new sha256. Paper 2 cites three repositories and three digests that never
  move (operator memory perimetre-publication-4b; ETAT:61-63). A K1 file supersedes nothing published. It becomes a
  fourth object beside the cited ones, on the overwritable -served repository only.
- Every downstream artifact is rebuilt per size: the sealed file, hfpack safetensors and its bit-for-bit gate, the
  MLX door, the 50-problem served pilots, the MMLU and GSM8K census, the WikiText-2 perplexity, the Hub card. ETAT §2
  then reports a different object from paper 2's tables.
- Cost of producing the object is one of the six criteria. Today it is about $4 at 4B, $10 at 8B and $32 at 14B
  with encoding. K1 adds $15 to $25 at 4B by the note's figures and $28 to $39 by mine. At 14B it cannot be priced on
  one card. That is a trade-off on the sixth criterion, and the note's "every other criterion unchanged" omits it.

6. THE SIZE OF THE CLAIM AGAINST THE GAP.  Refuted.
--------------------------------------------------
Gap to FP16 at 4B: 6.77 MMLU [6.05, 7.50] and 9.63 GSM8K [7.69, 11.57] (ETAT:92).
What the repository says post-hoc training recovers:
- Row scales from an untrained base: +3.15 / +3.29 / +1.67 MMLU at 4B / 8B / 14B (ETAT:147-149). The sealed file
  already holds them.
- A second continuous training on the sealed file: +0.02 (sealed-rownorms).
- Row scales keep 0.66 to 0.76 of a base gap (revue-structurelle-2026-10-20:43-44).
- Sub-additivity 0.62 to 0.79 over seven arms, 0.56 across families (ROADMAP-QUALITY:46-47).
- The note's discrete evidence is PV-tuning on Llama 2 at AQLM 2.01 bits: +1.2 to +1.6 of zero-shot average above
  a continuous step that trains learned codebooks (innovation-encodeur-reseau:92-94). No MMLU, no Qwen3, no lattice.
  This repository has six dissociations between proxy and MMLU.
- The repository's only discrete-choice experiment on Tetra lost when chained: +2.393 % local, +0.911 % chained,
  -8.763 % on reserved activations (tetra-directions-2026-09-16.txt:62-66). K1 scores moves by a diagonal Fisher
  model and accepts many at once.
My interval at 4B (*estimated*): MMLU 0 to +1.5, centre +0.5. GSM8K -1 to +4, centre +1.5. The MMLU centre is P3's
corpus centre, plus a discrete part discounted by sub-additivity and by an unknown membership rate. The GSM8K range
keeps P3's corpus effect and halves the discrete part. K2 adds at most P1's range, +0.5 to +1.7 MMLU, which the
2026-10-20 review computed for YAQA proper (lines 71-73). K2 is a local descent from GPTQ codes, below that.

THE WEAKEST POINT
-----------------
The continuous half of K1 is measured null on the sealed 4B (+0.02 pp, sealed-rownorms). Everything claimed rests
on a lattice step whose Tetra membership rate is unmeasured, and on a corpus effect its sibling note puts at -0.5
to +1.0 MMLU.

THE DECISIVE TEST AND ITS PRICE
-------------------------------
Step 0, $0, Mac, under a go, about one hour at LLVQ_THREADS ncpu-4 and nice 10. Qwen3-0.6B, one existing Tetra
  encoding. Measure the membership rate of the best move and Spearman of predicted against realised held-out KL
  change over 20 move batches. Kill if membership < 20 %, Spearman < 0.3, or held-out KL rises. The note's Stage 0
  as written; it is sound.
Step 1, about $9, before any code-moving build. P3's constant-code arm: retrain the 4B row scales on the mixed
  corpus from the pre-row-scale DCLM codes, scored on MMLU, GSM8K and WikiText-2 (recherche-methode-quantification:
  200-203). If the corpus alone does not lift GSM8K with MMLU and perplexity held, K1's GSM8K centre falls to its
  discrete part, +1 to +3, under the bar.
Step 2, about $80 to $105, only after both pass and with a new cap. The sealed 4B, arms A (continuous, mixed
  corpus, l40sx1) and B (A plus V phases, h200), three training seeds each, hyper-parameters frozen from the 0.6B.
  Selection on MMLU validation and a GSM8K-train slice only, test touched once. Adoption needs all four gates of
  section 2. Credit to the lattice step needs B - A > 0 at CI95 over the seeds. A second calibration draw is what
  METHODE asks before the gain is called a recipe: encode $0 on the Mac, its row scales $4, one B arm and its
  scoring, about $20 to $30 more (*computed* from the prices above).
Without Step 0 and Step 1 green, nothing above $0 is justified.
