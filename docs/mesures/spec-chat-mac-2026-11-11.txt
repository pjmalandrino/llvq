METHOD
------
Text. No card text exists for MT-Bench: the text is the MLX door's own greedy decode, 256 tokens after `first`,
<|im_end|> included, Qwen3 chat template with an empty reasoning block (thinking off), one user message, prompt ids as
specbench builds them. The house chat text is arbre32's mac-greedy.json, unchanged. 17,145 MT-Bench tokens; teacher-
forced agreement of the capture with the text at least 0.9883 a prompt (*measured*).
DFlash: arbre32's drafter forward and draft_tree_scored construction, top 4 a depth, at every pending position; "ref"
= checkpoint widened to f32 (the card runs f16, M2 shows f16 moves 0 prompts), "int4" = int4 g128 on the 35 linear
weights and fc (M2's passing arm, 3.300 b/param). Chains of depth c accept min(depth-15 chain, c). Check: house chat
ref tree 15 1.937 on the Mac against 1.92 on the card (M3 a12ip log); chat-00 1.695 against 1.69.
Tables: spec_sans_poids's comb (lookup spine when its match n >= 2, the rest from a warm recycling table, k 4, shape
`mid`, q 0.9) and look (lookup chain alone), verify rows exact (tree forward of the target; gate 24/24 text rows and
9/9 off-text rows top-1, top-8 overlap 1.000, *measured*). Tables warm across prompts in file order, MT-Bench and house
separate. comb with k 4 was not run before (k 8 read 0.605 on house chat; k 4 reads 0.580 here).
Gate. Below a threshold no verify pass runs: the served 4E emits one token, 4.46 ms. Three costs for that token:
- round: the feature comes from the DFlash round, which already ran: 4.46 + 4.04 = 8.50 ms.
- pre-check: a depth-1-only drafter readout. The card's drafter wall on chat (a12ip, measured) is context 522, block
  1,461, head 581, readout 394 ms: a depth-1 readout still pays context and the whole block (the block attends over
  all 16 positions), 0.692 of the round = 2.80 ms (*computed*). Token cost 7.26 ms. When the pre-check says yes the
  round costs 4.04 as before.
- free: the target's top-1 probability at the row that produced the root, read from the previous pass. Token cost
  4.46 ms. Caveat: 4E emits no taps, so the drafter's context misses every 4E token; the card would need 4E to emit
  the five tap rows, or the next pass to recompute them. Not costed.
For tables the gate is the lookup match length n, a host check: free, no tap issue. The 4E token's row feeds the
recycling table its top 4 (assumes 4E returns a top 4; it returns an argmax today).
Halves: MT-Bench even file index = train (questions 81, 83, ...), odd = test, 5 a category each. Thresholds chosen on
train for the highest tok/s (41 quantiles of the feature), frozen, reported on test; house chat 10 is a second
held-out set. EMA policy: speculate while an EMA (alpha 0.5) of accepted counts stays >= theta, else `probe` 4E tokens
then one probe pass, no drafter round on 4E tokens (same tap caveat).
Costs (card, L40S, M3 2026-11-11, *measured*): 4E 4.46 ms a token (224.2 tok/s; chat greedy measured 224.0); DFlash
round 4.04 ms; table drafter 0.75 ms; a12ip 9.56 ms a 1-row pass, 10.9 ms a 16-row pass; a12 8.58 ms a 16-row pass.
A pass of R rows is a linear interpolation between 1 and 16 rows (*interpolation*; M3's oracle:3, 4 rows, read 9.69 ms
against 9.83 interpolated). a12 at 1 row was never measured: 9.56 x 8.58 / 10.9 = 7.52 ms (*estimated*).
Break-even accepted a pass at 16 rows (*computed*): DFlash 1.83 (a12), 2.35 (a12ip); tables 1.09 (a12), 1.61 (a12ip).
Check of the cost model: house chat ref tree 15 projects 232.7 (a12) and 196.6 (a12ip) against 240.3 and 200.2
measured on the card: the projection reads 1.8 to 3.2 % low.

READING
-------
- Chat is not one number. MT-Bench's conversational half (writing, roleplay, humanities, stem) accepts 1.9 to 2.5 a
  pass, the house set's level; its task half (reasoning, math, coding, extraction) 3.1 to 6.3. The 10 house prompts
  were not unlucky; they were all of the conversational kind.
- On the conversational half the 16-row pass at a12 sits at break-even (1.83) and at a12ip under it (2.35). Gates
  move the result by 1 to 7 %: the drafter's depth-1 confidence and the target's confidence separate good passes from
  bad ones poorly. The perfect-gate bound (+19 to +43 % there) shows the room is real; a feature that captures it is not
  in hand.
- The "free" target gate is the best on a12ip (+7 % on test) but only because 70 % of tokens go to 4E: it is close to
  "do not speculate on chat", and it needs taps from 4E to be deployable.
- At 2.73 b/param the lookup gate is the only policy that does not lose on average: it speculates on 4 to 8 % of
  passes, where a long n-gram match makes the pass pay, and leaves the rest to 4E. Worst prompt -6 to -7 %.
- int4 against f16 drafter: -0.05 accepted a pass on MT-80 (-1.6 %), -1.2 % tok/s; M2's verdict holds on chat.
- Smaller trees and chains do not pay on this verify pass: its cost is nearly flat in rows.

NOT DONE
--------
- No card run: every tok/s is a projection from card costs; the model reads 1.8 to 3.2 % low on the house chat.
- The pass at a12 for fewer than 16 rows was never measured (estimated by a12ip's ratio). It matters only for the
  adaptive tree, which never picks a small tree anyway.
- MT-Bench's second turns (multi-turn context) were not run.
- The texts are the Mac target's greedy, not the card's; the house check (1.937 against 1.92) says the replay matches
  the card where both exist.
- A gate feature beyond the ones asked (e.g. a learned predictor of the accepted count from the drafter's 15 depths,
  or the rejection history) was not tried.
