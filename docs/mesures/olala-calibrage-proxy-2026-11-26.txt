
OLALA V1.0 MINI 7A1B, THE NOISE PROXY AGAINST A REAL ENCODE ON THREE LAYERS, 2026-11-26

RESULT
  Q1. The noise proxy reads the KL of a real data-free Tetra encode: KL(T1)/KL(N1) = 1.037 for one word and
  KL(T2)/KL(N2) = 0.952 for two, over 8,192 positions (*measured*). Both are inside the [0.7, 1.3] band written
  before the reads, so the proxy is called faithful. Window by window the ratios run 0.987 to 1.100 and 0.920 to
  0.980.
  Q2. Two Tetra words read a lower KL than int4 g128 in the model: 0.00561 against 0.00694, a paired difference of
  -0.00133 [-0.00173, -0.00092], negative on all four windows (*measured*). By the rule written before the reads
  two words are called better: 19 % less KL for 4.06 bits a weight against 4.25. Top-1 agreement does not separate
  them, 96.52 % against 96.56 %.
  The levels expected before the reads were wrong. The proxy at one word reads a KL of 0.0318 where 0.010 to 0.013
  was written, and at two words 0.0059 where 0.0013 was written.
  The KL falls more slowly than the error. An error 11.5 times smaller divides the KL by 5.4 under noise and by 5.9
  under the real encode, an exponent near 0.7 (*computed* from the measured arms).
  Every encode here is data-free, H = I, on the routed experts of three layers of 36. No file exists, and nothing
  here says what GPTQ against real Hessians adds.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE PLAN
  The plan is not edited. Three things were not in it.
  1. The Tetra reads were first scripted on 4 threads (`eval2-stopped-before-any-read.sh`) while the base and the
     noise arms had run on 3. That script was stopped before its first read and replaced by `eval2b.sh` on 3
     threads.
  2. Two null controls were added before the Tetra reads, section 2.
  3. Section 6 was computed after the reads from the saved per-position results. It has no rule written before it.

HOW TO READ IT
  What it supports. On these three layers, a data-free encode after a rotation behaves in the model as isotropic
  noise of the same relative error, for Tetra at one word and two and for int4 g128, in KL and in top-1. The noise
  arms of the two earlier journals can be read as estimates of a data-free encode, and no longer only as a ranking.
  A second word is worth more than int4 g128 in the model for fewer bits, on these layers.
  What it does not support. Layers 6, 17 and 28 are none of the layers with massive activations (0 to 3, 5, 7,
  19, 23). The dense matrices, the embedding and the head are untouched. The text is OVH's report and one of our
  documents in windows of 512, not WikiText-2 and not the calibration corpus. One noise draw an arm. The published
  recipe encodes with GPTQ against real Hessians, which the proxy ignores and this test does not run: the faithful
  proxy bounds a data-free encode, not the file.
  What it changes for the plan. The KL cannot be scaled from the error: a second word divides the error by 11.5
  and the KL by 5.9. Nor can it be scaled from a part of the model to the whole by the share of the layers: three
  layers of 36 read a fifth of the whole-model figure, under the caveat of section 6 on the two sums.
