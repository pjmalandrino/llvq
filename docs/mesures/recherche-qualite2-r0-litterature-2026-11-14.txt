The idea as a whole is not found after 32 web searches: no source picks zero-bit gains for a coherent gain of 1 along the
depth, unbiased rather than MMSE, and judges by output contrast over noise. Its pieces are published one by one. The
output-side shrink is measured: quantization multiplies decision margins by c, median 0.29 at 3 bits under RTN, and
0.83 (GPTQ) or 0.91 on MMLU at 4 bits ([S42]). Corrections that restore norm or variance exist (Banner et al. 2019
[T4], Norm Tweaking [S12], GSRQ [S37]), and WaterSIC shrinks on purpose at low rate ([S38]). Unbiasing the stages of a
cascade of MMSE estimators is an old communications idea ([T13]). Left unstated: the coherent gain of a quantized weight
matrix and its product along the depth; unbiased against MMSE gains compared on task accuracy for LLM weights. One
published counter-fact: raising c from 0.24 to 0.44 left the flip rate at 0.43 against 0.46 ([S42]).
Nature: literature only. No build, no run, no model, no job. Web search and fetch on 2026-11-14. [S#] are the sources
of recherche-qualite-r0-litterature-2026-11-13.txt, reused where they answer; [T#] are new, listed at the end. Every
figure is *reported* by the source named on its line. "abstract only" means the full text was not read for that claim;
"snippet only" means the source page was not opened or did not show the figure.

1. ATTENUATION OF LOGITS OR MARGINS AGAINST FULL PRECISION. Verdict: PARTLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Multiplicative margin model, Wu, Dhiman, Koshiyama [S42], re-read today. The first-token margin is fitted
  m' = c m + b + e over 16 models. Median c: 0.86 at 4 bits, 0.29 at 3 bits, 0.00 at 2 bits, pooled, mostly RTN and
  activation-aware runs. Table 6 at 4 bits, pooled: MMLU c 0.83 with 16 % flips under GPTQ (AutoRound builds), 0.91
  with 12 % under the authors' activation-aware scaling, not AWQ itself. Qwen3-4B at 3 bits RTN: c 0.94 on code tokens,
  0.13 on whether-to-call. The paper states it does not explain the mechanism and never frames c as depth attenuation.
  This is the closest published object to the note's slope. It is fitted on two candidates, not on four centred logits.
- Confidence on the true label falls. Proskurina et al. [S13], abstract: 4-bit GPTQ lowers it, most on samples the
  full model was already unsure of. Yang et al. [T1], Table 12, 8 models, GPTQ 4-bit: mean confidence on correct
  predictions 0.8358 at FP against 0.8089 to 0.8087 quantized, on wrong ones 0.7225 against 0.7042 to 0.6999. NF4 stays
  at 0.8353 and 0.7233. The text speaks of "degrading the separation between reliable and unreliable outputs".
- Calibration. Zhong et al., ACL 2025 [T2], abstract: quantized LLMs are consistently worse calibrated. "Under-confident
  on correct, over-confident on incorrect" is snippet only; the abstract gives no direction. Their fix is soft tokens.
  [T1] fits one temperature per model (Appendix B.2): calibration improves, accuracy and FP agreement do not move. No
  fitted temperature value was found in the part read.
- Decision flips. Razan, Shivam, NeurIPS 2026 workshop [T3], abstract: an isotropic first-order flip criterion
  under-predicts flips by 3.5 to 454 times; the pre-quantization margin alone predicts them at AUC 0.95 against 0.69.
- Scale drift. Norm Tweaking [S12]: activations drift from zero mean layer by layer; the direction of the variance
  change is not stated in the text read. Chen et al. [S4], NVFP4, Qwen3 and others: 88.9 to 98.7 % of the squared
  relative error of the final hidden state is angular, so little of it is a norm change at that format.
Not found: a regression of the full answer-logit vector of a weight-quantized LLM on its teacher's, with a slope.

2. BIAS, VARIANCE AND SCALE CORRECTIONS: SHRINK OR RESTORE? Verdict: PARTLY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
| source | what it fits | shrink or restore | evidence (*reported*) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nagel et al. 2019 [S36] | output mean shift E[eps x] into the bias | mean only, no scale | MobileNetV2 8-bit per-tensor 0.12 -> 52.02 % top-1 |
| Banner et al. 2019 [T4] | per channel: mean shift mu_c and xi_c = norm(W_c - mean) / norm(W_c^q - mean) | restores the centred weight norm | 4W8A ImageNet: ResNet-18 59.7 -> 67.4, Inception-V3 38.4 -> 59.5, mean +6.0 |
| Variance correction for sparsity [T12] | multiply survivors by sqrt(Var dense / Var pruned) | restores variance, pruning not quantization | LLaMA3-8B 2:4 Wiki-2 19.13 -> 16.19; 0-shot 58.01 -> 58.87; degrades Mistral, so omitted there |
| Norm Tweaking [S12] | LayerNorm weights, loss = L2 on channel means plus L2 on channel variances | restores activation mean and variance | 2-bit GPTQ LAMBADA LLaMa-7b 11.88 -> 21.39; its loss beats MSE, BLOOM-7b 57.48 against 55.87 |
| WaterSIC [S38] | per-column gamma by least squares, "rounding errors biased away from 0" | shrinks; gamma < 1 observed, not imposed | ablation in relative MSE only; WaterSIC-FT Qwen3-8B 2.125 b Wiki-2 11.37 -> 10.18 |
| WaterSIC-FT [S38] | row and column rescalers T, Gamma, codes frozen, KL to teacher (cross-entropy elsewhere in the text) | starts at the LMMSE values | whether trained values end above or below them: Fig. 4, values not in the text read |
| GSRQ [S37] | gain-shape centroid against the l2 centroid, KV cache | restores: the l2 centroid's norm shrinks | LLaMA-3-8B Wiki-2 at 0.375 bit/element: l2 19.50, gain-shape 12.77; at 2.0: 6.21 against 5.91 |
| mlx-lm DWQ [T5] | affine scales and biases of layers under 8 bits | not characterised | source code: KL on logits at temperature 2.0 by default, top-1024 teacher logits; weights frozen |
- GSRQ is the only source found that puts an MMSE-shrunk reconstruction against a norm-restoring one on a downstream
  metric. It argues from the law of cosines: shrinking the norm lowers the objective without improving the direction.
  It claims gain-shape MSE is "often" lower too, and it does not discuss depth. Its object is the KV cache, not weights.
- Banner's xi_c matches norms; it does not set the coherent gain to 1. Algebra on its formula: if W^q = a W + n with n
  orthogonal to W, matching norms gives a^2 + |n|^2/|W|^2 = 1, so the coherent gain stays under 1 when n is not zero.
- None of the eight sources argues for unbiased gains because of depth. None compares unbiased and MMSE gains on task
  accuracy for weights. [S42] never uses the words "unbiased" or "MMSE".

3. DEPTH: MULTIPLICATIVE LOSS AGAINST ADDITIVE NOISE. Verdict: PARTLY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mean field. Blumenfeld, Gilboa, Soudry, NeurIPS 2019 [T6], abstract: quantized activations, not weights, degrade
  signal propagation at initialization; maximal trainable depth scales about as N^1.82 in the number of levels.
- Error grows with depth, stated without a ratio: QEP [S39]; GPTQ's "symmetric calibration" lets errors from quantized
  inputs build up, and GPTAQ fits each layer to the full-precision output instead [S40], abstract only.
- Domino test [S28], re-read today: Llama-3.1-8B, layers 0 to k at 2 bits, failure-subset accuracy 15.03 % at k = 0,
  0.38 % at k = 5. The authors call 4-bit error "cumulative noise" that weakens the correct signal until "linguistic
  noise" overtakes it, a "competition failure". No attenuation factor is measured.
- Counteraction [S4]: about 50.2 % of the cumulative added error cancels across blocks in pretrained Qwen3-32B at NVFP4.
- Cascades of estimators. Sparrer, Fischer [T13], abstract: in turbo-type iterative recovery, computing extrinsic values
  equals unbiasing a biased MMSE estimate. A snippet of its search listing adds that the end-to-end cascade diagonal must
  equal 1 to avoid bias. This is the stated form of "unbiased stages in a chain", outside neural networks.
- Textbook, not fetched: the MMSE reconstruction of a Gaussian at distortion D has variance sigma^2 - D (Cover and
  Thomas, Elements of Information Theory, chapter 10).
Not found: a statement or measurement, for a quantized network, that the coherent signal multiplies down the layers while
the noise adds.

4. DIRECTION DEPENDENCE AND WHAT IS FORGOTTEN. Verdict: PARTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------
- High-variance input directions are protected by construction. ExLlamaV3 [S20]: LDLQ steers error away from directions
  the data exercises; isotropic weight noise overstates KL sensitivity by up to about 6x. The GPTQ objective weights
  error by the input covariance (stated in many sources, for example [S20]).
- At 2 bits the error lies in the signal subspace: alignment about 0.8 against 0.3 at 4 bits, Llama-3.1-8B [S28].
- Textbook, not fetched: reverse water-filling gives zero rate to components whose variance is under the water level.
- Long tail. Hooker et al. [T7], abstract, image classifiers: compression hurts rare, atypical examples while top-line
  accuracy holds. Jaiswal et al., ICLR 2024 [T8], abstract: perplexity misses knowledge loss; quantization holds better
  than pruning on their knowledge-intensive benchmark.
- Factual recall at 2 bits fails across the board on 39 relations [S28]. AQLM's appendix: MMLU and GSM8K fall more than
  perplexity and zero-shot suites [S17]. Eight perplexity-against-task inversions are tabled in the 2026-11-13 note.
Not found: a measurement that quantization preserves high-variance activation directions and damages low-variance ones,
read along the depth of an LLM.

5. ATTENTION FLATTENING AND TEMPERATURE. Verdict: PARTLY
--------------------------------------------------------
- Quantization-aware vision transformers. Q-ViT, NeurIPS 2022 [T9]: q and k distributions distort when quantized
  (DeiT-T first block query variance 1.2124 against 1.6533 at full precision). Its rectification module restores q and k
  mean and variance with learnable affine terms to raise their entropy. DeiT-S top-1, module alone: 68.2 -> 69.9 at 2
  bits, 77.8 -> 78.2 at 3, 79.7 -> 80.2 at 4. The paper says attention distances become "indistinguishable"; it does not
  say "flatter".
- Binarized BERT. BiBERT, ICLR 2022 [T10], snippet only: an entropy-maximising threshold for binarized attention.
- Small LMs. Squat (formerly EdgeQAT) [T15], abstract: "entropy-guided and distribution-aligned distillation" against
  attention distortion; no number on the page.
- Attention activation quantization in LLMs. HiFA4 [T11], abstract: 4-bit QK^T and PV on Qwen3-8B; all 3.6M measured
  layer-0 tiles lose net probability mass, median -0.064, "a coherent output-scaling error". The fix recovers 37.5 % of
  the MMLU gap. Not weight quantization.
- Mean shift after the post-attention norm when attention weights are quantized, small after the MLP: D2Quant [S7].
Not found after 4 queries ("quantized LLM attention entropy increases", "learnable temperature attention softmax
quantization-aware", "post-training quantization LLM attention temperature scaling", "weight-only quantization attention
map entropy per head"): an attention-entropy measurement under weight-only LLM quantization, or a per-head temperature
used to compensate in PTQ or LLM QAT.

6. REPAIR BY A LINEAR MAP. Verdict: PARTLY
------------------------------------------
- Ruled out at the logits. [S42] proves that l -> alpha l + beta with one alpha > 0 and one beta changes no sign, so
  temperature and global offsets cannot repair a flip. Its label-free repairs (per-item offsets, protected layers,
  gradient rounding, dithering) win on no majority of models.
- Logit amplification [S28], 4 bits: the logits of the lowest-entropy layer are multiplied by alpha (3, 9, 7, 2 by
  model). Pararel failure subset after the basic repair: Qwen3-8B 40.24 -> 79.88 %, Llama-3.1-8B 67.91 -> 75.19 %. How a
  positive scalar changes the final argmax is not explained in the text read. It fails at 2 bits, numbers not given.
- Zero-bit gains trained on the output: DWQ scales and biases [T5]; WaterSIC-FT rescalers [S38]; RMSNorm-only tuning
  in AQLM, Wiki-2 8.18 -> 8.31 [S17]; per-column scales in the LLVQ paper, Qwen3-4B 2 bits MMLU 60.7 -> 62.8.
- Output-side PTQ. LFQ, ICML 2026 [T14], abstract: re-quantizes the final block on cross-entropy to the teacher's logits;
  no figure on the page.
Not found: an affine map on hidden states or the readout, zero or near-zero bits, evaluated at 2 to 3 bits on an LLM.

Nearest neighbours, each missing a part:
- [S42] measures the output shrink c and finds raising it does not cut flips (Qwen3-4B, 3.25 b average, nine of 36
  layers protected: c 0.24 -> 0.44, flip rate 0.46 -> 0.43, App. A.14). It proposes no gain choice.
- [T4] and [T12] restore norm or variance per channel, without depth or contrast as the reason, on CNNs and pruning.
- [S37] shows a norm-restoring centroid beating the MMSE one, on the KV cache, without depth.
- [S12] matches activation mean and variance and beats KL and MSE losses, without a coherent-gain target.
- [S38] goes the other way: MMSE shrink called "indispensable for low-rate quantization".
- [T13] states unbiasing in cascades, for communications receivers.
The claim that KL or perplexity objectives push toward shrink is not stated anywhere found. What is published is that
they can move against task accuracy (2026-11-13 note, section 8; Q5 and Q14 of the repository).
