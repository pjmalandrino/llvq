- The step to 2,017 MiB is the tree pass's row buffers (the file prints 0.153 GB). install_tree allocates them right
  after compiling its 306,060-byte module (sha256 ce1c354d). So 107.9 s go to that compile and its setup.
- The last 253.9 s hold the a12ip module (422,018 bytes, sha256 63155df9) through NVRTC, then the in-place check.
- The check builds each of 253 tree-tiled copies (1.295 GB in all), launches p4t_tile_words and p4t_ip_check, reads
  back one word, syncs and frees the copy (llvq-llm/src/persist4e_cuda.rs, install_tiled). No copy shows in the
  sampler; each lives under its 50 ms period.
Reading (*estimated*): the in-place check is not where the 419.5 s go.
- M3 loaded a12 in 382.5 s with no check and its 253 copies held, against 343.8 s for a12ip with the check
  (spec-mem-m3-2026-11-11-brut, both first runs). The check does not make the load longer.
- The check is two grid launches and a 4-byte read a projection; a few ms each, about 1 s for 253.
- The cost is NVRTC compiling the two tree modules: about 108 s and about 250 s, beside eager's whole load.
- Why the chain process reached 4E's installed level 43 s sooner than eager (57.7 against 100.6 s) is not
  established.
A served form would compile once, not at every load: the cubins built at image time, or cached on disk keyed by the
module's sha256 the file already prints, then loaded with no NVRTC. The in-place check is cheap and can stay at every
load. With both tree modules cached, the chain load should come near eager's 100.6 s (*estimated*, not measured).
