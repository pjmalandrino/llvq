SPEC FIXED-COST AUDIT: WHERE THE TREE PASS'S 0.54 TOKEN AT ONE ROW GOES, SEALED 4B, L40S, 2026-11-11. $0.
=======================================================================================================
Result: of the 2,425 us a one-row pass costs over a 4E token (a10t384r16, the a12 pass without taps, N11, *measured*),
this audit locates 1,458 us with measured stamps or computed counts, puts 517 us on estimates with a stated basis, and
leaves 451 us unexplained. The largest single item is still the row phases: the median CTA is busy 1.52 times as long
as 4E's mean CTA in the same projections (+1,487 us). The fold phases are the largest measured item outside them
(604 us, 145 grid waits). The prologues are no longer a cost (+68 us). The tree's attention is cheaper than 4E's
(-207 us).
Scope: read only. No build, no run, no job, no model loaded. Sources are the journals named on each line and the code
of llvq-cuda/kernels/pers_4e.cu, llvq_pers.cuh, pers_segment.cu, pers_4et.cu, pers_4et_stream.cu, pers_4et_a9.cu,
pers_4et_a10rc.cu and pers_4et_a12.cu at be93408.

READING
-------
- The fixed cost lives in the per-CTA work of the row phases, not in a separate phase. The median CTA's row work is
  1.52 x 4E's mean CTA's; the loop factor explains half of the excess, unit overheads a quarter.
- The 254 extra grid waits cost about 1.3 us each bare (fold and prologue residues), about 330 us in all, already
  inside lines 1 and 2. They are a third of the fold line; the rest is a latency chain on 2 to 13 CTAs, with CTA 0
  folding q, k and v one after the other.
- The prologue route (a10) is spent: CTA 0 now does the work 4E's CTAs each do, at the same speed.
- Nothing in the ledger needs a second copy of the weights. Every lever named below reads the same words.
- In the loop, a 16-row pass costs 8.53 ms (spec-loop-sp8). That is c_16 at the loop's longer contexts (N11 rag:
  c_16 1.870 of a 4,732 us token, 8.85 ms); the one-row fixed cost of this ledger is about 2.4 ms of it.
- 4E's own attention reads 207 us above the tree's. If its prefetch is the cause, it is a lever on 4E, not on the
  tree. That is a side note, not this audit's question.

THE THREE CHEAPEST CARD EXPERIMENTS (not written; each one arm beside a12 at [8,10,8,20,6], N11's bench, chat)
-----------------------------------
E1. a12fs, bit for bit with a12: the fold step dealt over all CTAs (pair p on CTA p mod grid), its S partial loads
    issued before the first add. The order of the adds and the epilogue are unchanged, so the bits are a12's.
    Read: the five fold rows of vt1 and c_1.
    - Fold is a latency chain on few CTAs: fold rows fall from 604 to 300-380 us, busy max falls about x3, c_1 falls
      0.05 to 0.07.
    - Fold is the 145 waits: fold rows stay at 500 us or more, wall above busy max stays near 184 us, c_1 moves less
      than 0.02. Then only removing the fold waits (folding in the consumer phase) can pay.
E2. a12h, bit for bit with a12: in p4t_unit_rc, the first sub-step's weight loads (p4t_stream_load or p4t_ip_load)
    issued before p4t_cp_wait and the __syncthreads. They read weights, not the staged slice, so nothing changes in
    the values.
    Read: the four row phases of vt1, their busy medians, and c_1.
    - Unit starts expose a DRAM round trip in lockstep: the four row phases fall 130 to 260 us (0.3 to 0.6 us on each
      of 430 units), c_1 falls 0.03 to 0.06.
    - Not: they move less than 40 us and c_1 stays within 0.01; line 5b-ii then sits in the unit's close and staging.
E3. a12nm, a floor arm with void values: the three mma.sync of each sub-step replaced by an integer stand-in that keeps
    the A and B fragment loads live (an XOR of the fragments into the accumulator's bits). Decode, STS, LDS and
    __syncwarp stay. Guard and identity are not read; vt1's argmax is not read.
    Read: the four row phases of vt1 and c_1, against a12.
    - The MMA chain carries the loop's excess (three dependent HMMA a sub-step, 7 of 8 columns idle, 3 warps a
      sub-partition): the four row phases fall 300 to 700 us, c_1 falls 0.07 to 0.15.
    - The A tile's shared round trip and the decode carry it: they fall less than 180 us (the tensor bound, computed).
Cost (*estimated* from N11: 20.2 min of load and install for 5 modules, about 4 min a module, 1 to 2 min of rounds):
each experiment alone about 12 min of L40S ($0.36 at $1.80/h, a12 and the arm); the three arms with a12 in one job
about 20 min ($0.60). Measured on a12ip instead of a12, the install builds no tree-tiled copy and is shorter; the
arms then sit beside a12ip.

bench's a12. R above 1: the extra row (0.012 of a token) is not in this ledger.
