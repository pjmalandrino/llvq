WHAT IT CHANGES
---------------
- CERVEAU P4 said an int8 cache does not pass the tolerance at long context, from a 12-window interval at 4,096 on
  Metal whose upper bound was +0.1703 %. At 8,192 on the sealed file the whole interval is inside ±0.12 %. P4 is
  rewritten in this commit.
- k8v4's point is +0.02 % and its half width 0.157 %: the int4 values widen the window-to-window spread by 2.6 against
  k8v8 (0.157 against 0.061). The prereg allows one extension to windows 12 to 35, under its own note and go.
- k4v4c at +141 % is 14 times above the top of its predicted range. A static symmetric step a RoPE pair, clipped at
  the 0.999 quantile of 4,096 dclm-edu tokens (dclm-edu dbad8ad7; steps 7.25e-3 to 40.4, median 0.62), does not
  describe Qwen3's keys. Whether the scheme or its code is at fault was not looked at. The arm is closed as run.
