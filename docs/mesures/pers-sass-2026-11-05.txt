WHAT BOUNDS THE TETRA STREAM AT 410 GB/S: THE TABLE LOADS THROUGH L1 FILL THE L1/SHARED DATA PIPE (RULE F OF L1, AND A MODEL FOR L2), 2026-11-05
===========================================================================================================================================

Result: the one-launch Tetra pass of L1 (tpersall, 2.394 ms, 410 GB/s) is bound by the SM's L1/shared data pipe, on
this model and with no fitted parameter. Its loads, counted on the sealed 4B's words and priced at one wavefront per
distinct 128-byte line, need 2.20 to 2.52 ms of that pipe at 2.52 to 2.20 GHz (*estimated*); its issue floor is 0.92
to 1.06 ms and its DRAM floor 1.13 ms (*computed*). Three decoder tables read at random indices through L1 carry about
60 % of the wavefronts, the activation's reads 29 %, half of them bank conflicts. With the tables in shared memory the
same loads cost a third of the wavefronts, and the floor becomes DRAM's, about 1.1 ms: 4E's 300 tok/s needs about
2.0 ms (490 GB/s).
None of this ran on a card; three arms added to L2 test it there.

Prereg:  none (analysis at $0). It re-anchors proofs/preregistration-pers-l2-2026-11-04.md (not stamped).
Cost:    $0. cuobjdump 12.4 in nvidia/cuda:12.4.1-devel-ubuntu22.04 on the Mac; the replay on the Mac's CPU.
Inputs:  the card's cubins of L1 (pers-l1-2026-11-04-brut/cubin/, driver ptxas 580.178.04, sm_89); the sealed 4B
         (~/hub-stage/qwen3-4b-tetra-sealed/qwen3-4b-sealed.bin, the published file); the L2 arms compiled by the
         devel ptxas (ops/nvrtc-check.sh, indicative).
Tools:   ops/sass_loops.py (loops of a SASS listing, by opcode), llvq-llm/src/bin/tetragather.rs (per-warp load
         counts on a file's words), ops/pers_lsu_model.py (the three floors per arm).

RULE F: THE CARD'S SASS (tv_tetra48_persall, 56 registers, 0 local bytes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Occupancy: 56 registers x 256 threads, 4 CTAs a SM by the register file, 32 warps a SM of 48.
  Inner block loop (0x04e0-0x1400): 243 instructions a warp-iteration, one block a lane, no unroll, no prefetch.
    LOP3 47, IMAD 44, PRMT 36, FFMA 25, FADD 24, SHF 19, IADD3 7, SEL 6, IDP 6, LDS.128 6, LEA 5, ISETP 5,
    LDG.E.CONSTANT 4, LD.E 3, LDG.E.U8.CONSTANT 2, LDG.E.U16.CONSTANT 1, UMOV 1, FMUL 1, BRA 1.
  Loads of one block, in order of dependence:
    1. the two stream words, generic LD.E (the pointer comes from the site table, so NVVM cannot prove .nc);
    2. branches (u16) and prefixes (u8) and the three rank rows (u32, 16 KB table), LDG.CONSTANT, on the words;
    3. suffixes (u8), on the branch entry: a third dependent level;
    4. invnorm, on the decoded point's norm (six IDP.4A): a fourth level; gscale, generic LD.E.
  Six LDS.128 a block: confirmed in the card's SASS, as the PTX probe showed (ptx-probe-2026-11-04.txt).
  Site 1 fused: FMUL (acc_b x gscale) then FFMA (x invnorm + acc), the form the served replay models.
  Lane 0's tail: a loop unrolled 16 deep, LD.E.U16 + HADD2 + FFMA a column (site 2 fused).
  Reading: the decode is held, unchanged; nothing in the SASS contradicts the host replays. The decision frozen
  "until the SASS is read" (spec-lot-a-2026-08-05.md:132) has its condition met; lifting it is the operator's.

THE MODEL
---------
  A warp load costs the L1/shared data pipe (assumptions, which the L2 arms test):
    global, hit in L1   one wavefront per distinct 128-byte line its active lanes touch (the tag rate);
    shared, 32-bit      the largest number of distinct 4-byte words one bank is asked for;
    shared, 128-bit     the same over each quarter-warp, by 16-byte bank group.
  The pipe passes one wavefront a cycle a SM. Issue: one warp instruction a cycle a scheduler, 4 a SM.

  Counted on the sealed 4B's words (tetragather, every 4th row of its 168 Tetra matrices, tile 64), per warp-iteration:

    load        d_in 2,560 (26.5 lanes)          d_in 9,728 (31.15 lanes)
                L1 lines   shared wavefronts     L1 lines   shared wavefronts
    words x2      3.50         3.50                3.85         3.85
    rows x3      67.10         9.28               78.14        10.30
    branches     12.38         2.97               13.81         3.29
    pref, suf,    4.00         4.00                4.00         4.00
    invnorm, gscale
    tables       83.48        16.25               95.95        17.59
    x, stride 24 (6 LDS.128)    40.50                           47.08
    x, trio 28 (6 LDS.128)      21.00                           23.54
    x, stride 25 (24 LDS.32)    24.00                           24.00

  Uniformly drawn labels give the same counts within 3 % (tables in L1 85.19 and 98.06): the file's indices are
  near uniform, so no reordering of the codebook would gather them into fewer lines.
  At stride 24 each 128-bit activation load meets a two-way conflict in every quarter-warp: lanes l and l + 4 start
  96 x 4 = 384 bytes apart, the same 16-byte bank group. Trio stride 28 has none.

WHAT FOLLOWS, AND WHAT DOES NOT
-------------------------------
  - Tables in shared memory are the lever this model names: about 145 wavefronts a warp-iteration fall to about 48,
    and the floor moves from 2.2-2.5 ms to DRAM's 1.1 ms. At 1.4 ms (the L2 prereg's point) a pass streams at 676 GB/s;
    rule H of L1 then gives 4E 328-379 tok/s at 650 GB/s and 286-331 at 550 (*estimated*, ops/pers_projection.py with
    L1's other inputs). 490 GB/s, the target's need, is 2.0 ms.
  - The refusal of 2026-08-05 ("table en shared (régression)") does not transfer: it was Slot32's 24.6 KB copied by
    every CTA of a normal grid. On Tetra's 252 launches that is 138,240 copies of 18.8 KB a token, 2.6 GB of L2 reads
    against 0.98 GB of weights (*computed*); a persistent launch makes 142 copies, 2.7 MB.
  - Today's 4 CTAs of 256 cannot hold the tables: 4 x (18.8 + 6.1 + 1 KB reserved) is over sm_89's 100 KB. A persistent
    CTA of 768 or 1,024 threads holds them once, which is the L2 geometry.
  - Not established: the one-wavefront-per-line rate itself (an assumption the fit supports at one point only), the
    sustained clock, the latency side (24 warps, one block in flight a lane), anything at 8B or 14B.

ARMS ADDED TO L2 (code on branch claude/serene-bohr-1edf69; host-proven; nothing launched)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  pdec_l1    the decode without the dot, tables through L1: the 24 values built and XOR-folded, shell and scales read.
  pdec_smem  the same with the tables in shared memory. Its rows must equal pdec_l1's (gate 9 of the L2 prereg): the
             card-side check that the shared copy is the global table.
  pl1_t28    pl1 at trio stride 28: the activation conflicts removed, the tables still through L1.
  Readings (L2 prereg, rule I): pl1_t28 / pl1 at or below 0.92 with psmem / pl1 at or below 0.80 confirms the pipe;
  pl1_t28 / pl1 at or above 0.97 with psmem / pl1 at or above 0.90 refutes the line-rate model and points at latency.
  Host: pers_matches_served.rs::the_decode_arms_read_one_table (equal rows; a branch table swapped in the shared copy
  is caught), every_bench_arm_runs_the_served_rows over the fifteen arms. NVRTC 12.4, devel ptxas, no spill: pdec_l1
  55 registers, pdec_smem 47, pl1_t28 71.
