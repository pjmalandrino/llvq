DEPARTURES

Literal readings where the prereg leaves a choice. None changes a definition.
1. G1 "characters" are Unicode code points of the stored `completion` strings, with no normalisation.
2. M1 "the four MMLU dumps against FP16": FP16 against itself reads beta 1, sd_res 0; it is printed in report.txt
   and not read.
3. M1, September dumps: fitted against mmlu-4b-f16-FULL.csv, the reference the prereg names for M1 and the R0 note
   names for these slices, on the questions common to the four (all 14,042).
4. Bootstrap: one generator default_rng(20261115) a reading; G1, G2 and G3 share the same 10,000 x 1,319 draws.
   Percentiles 2.5 and 97.5 by numpy's linear interpolation.
5. G2 thirds: 1,319 is not a multiple of 3. Problems ranked by FP16 n_gen, ties broken by problem order, third =
   rank x 3 // 1,319, sizes 440, 440, 439; n_gen 246 and 324 fall on both sides of a cut. Membership is fixed per
   problem in the bootstrap, not recut a draw.
6. G3 interval: the prereg names "the interval" with no method; G1's bootstrap is used.
7. G3 parser: ASCII '-' is read as minus (the prereg writes U+2212); LaTeX \times and \div as × and ÷; '**' markers
   removed; '\$', '$', '€', '£' removed; commas between digits removed. Numbers are unsigned, so a step with a
   negative operand or result is not parsed. A step preceded, after spaces, by a digit, '.', an operator or '^', or
   followed by an operator or '^', is part of a chain and not parsed. \cdot is not read. Division by zero is false.
   A right side followed by a letter is parsed (see the parser check above).
8. G2 and G3 rule tables: their rows are not exclusive (an interval can lie above 0 and below +3). The script prints
   every row that holds; on this run one row held in each.
9. M2: the share of changed picks also counts picks that move between two wrong options; the margin is given as mean
   and median, the prereg names neither.
10. Self-check: the first self-check run, before any real file was read, failed a planted slope (0.2931 for 0.3000,
    1.1 sampling sd) because a 2 % tolerance was tighter than the slope's sampling sd at that noise. The tolerance
    became 4 sampling sd. A 30-draw check gave a mean standardised error of -0.23, sd 1.03.
11. The d = 0 counts and the 7-step parser check were read after the run; they are not prereg readings.
