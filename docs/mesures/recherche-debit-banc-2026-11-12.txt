WHAT THE DESCRIPTION SAYS
  1. A row that decodes nothing (24 instructions a block against 226) is only x1.22 faster than the served row. The
     decode is at most 18 % of the rows' time at this geometry. The R1 note's sentence, the rows are bound by the
     decode, and the repository's attribution of 0.66 to 0.92 ms to instruction time, do not survive this.
  2. The same read skeleton streams 493 GB/s where an int4 read streams 675 and the bare probe 751. The gap that
     matters is between how Tetra words are read (one row a warp, each lane its own narrow loads) and how the card
     can be read. One u32 a load already costs the probe 13 % (652.9 against 751.1).
  3. gate/up alone reads 429.5 against 359 at 5 tokens and 385 at 8,192 in the token (computed from stamps, E1): 10
     to 16 % of the rows' rate is lost to the rest of the token.
  4. The L2 prefetch is worth 11 % to the rows (served_np x0.892).
  5. A reassociated sum, which the bit forbade, is worth 2 % on the file and 7 to 10 % on rows of 4,096 and 9,728.
  6. The shapes: d_in 2,560 is the fastest alone (409.6), 9,728 the slowest (374.2). No journal had a rate a shape.
