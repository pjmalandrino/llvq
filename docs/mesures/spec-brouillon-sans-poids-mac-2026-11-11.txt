METHOD
------
Text: arbre32's. GSM8K and house math on the card's greedy text (SP8 gsm200.jsonl, 768 and 256 tokens after
`first`); house chat, code, rag on the MLX door's greedy text (arbre32 mac-greedy.json). `first` from arbre32
rows.json. Every prompt length checked against the card's log. Replay: a pass at root m emits min(a + 1, left);
accepted a pass = tokens over passes minus one, summed over prompts.
Tree: root (the last emitted token) + at most 15 drafted nodes = 16 rows, as DFlash's tree of 15.
Drafters, fed only by the text so far and by the target's logits on rows of earlier passes:
- rec (token recycling, arXiv 2408.08696): table [151,936][k] int32; after every pass, for every row of the tree
  (accepted or not, root included), table[row token] = that row's top k. Tree grown best first from the root:
  child of rank r scores parent * p[r], a static priority; a missing entry is skipped.
- look (prompt lookup): longest suffix of prompt + text (8 down to 1 tokens) seen earlier, most recent occurrence,
  its continuation as a chain of up to 15.
- comb: the lookup chain as the spine when its match is at least 2 tokens (spine child scores parent * q), the other
  nodes from the recycling table by p, best first, 15 in all, duplicates of a sibling skipped.
Cold: the table is cleared at each prompt. Warm: kept across the prompts of a set in file order (causal); house and
GSM8K have separate tables. The frozen GSM8K run starts its warm table empty at problem 40.
Verify rows are exact, not a lower bound. A row on the text is the teacher-forced row. A row off the text is the
target over text[:j] + its off-text tail, computed by a tree forward of the sealed target (dense f16 weights decoded
once from the Tetra words through the door's own dequantizer, prefix K/V of the teacher-forced pass, per-row RoPE
positions, ancestor mask), cached per prompt and shared by the arms. 583,310 off-text rows in the search, 835,648 in
the frozen run.
Gate (*measured*, first prompt): tree forward against teacher forcing on 24 text rows, top-1 24/24, top-8 overlap
1.000; against a fresh full forward on 9 off-text rows (depth 1 to 3), top-1 9/9, top-8 overlap 1.000.

READING
-------
- A warm table is worth +0.23 to +0.25 accepted a pass on GSM8K and +0.17 to +0.22 on the house set over a cold
  one (*measured*, rec and comb).
- k = 4 equals k = 8 within 0.02 everywhere: the `mid` shape rarely reaches rank 4. At equal acceptance the k = 4
  table is the smaller, 2.43 MB, +0.0048 b/param (*computed*). comb with k = 4 was not run.
- Recycling never accepts past depth 6 (histograms in report.txt): 15 nodes spread over ranks leave no depth. Lookup
  gives the deep passes (up to 15 on rag and code) but 0 on 75 % of GSM8K passes (8,428 of 11,210).
- The verify pass costs 8.53 ms whatever it carries, so every arm under 1.03 (GSM8K) or 1.06 (house) accepted a pass
  is slower than greedy. comb8w is +11.7 % on GSM8K and -6.2 % on the house set (*estimated*).
- The table is a 0.010 b/param question; the speed is a factor of two short of DFlash, not a few percent.

NOT DONE
--------
- GSM8K 100-199 (time). The 60 reported problems give DFlash 5.376 against 5.37 on all 200, so the subset is not
  easier or harder for DFlash.
- No card run: the 0.75 ms drafter cost and the 8.53 / 8.58 ms verify are the card's figures, not measured with these
  drafters; lookup on the card would cost less than 0.75 ms (host-side match).
- Prefill rows were not fed to the table (strict reading: the card computes only the last prompt row).
- Larger trees (the paper's ~80 nodes) are outside the 16-row pass.
