RESULT
------
On the CPU in f32 the grouped form is class B: its logits leave the expanded form's at step 1 and differ in some bit
at 2,046 of 2,047 steps, max |dlogit| 8.144e-4 against a largest |logit| of 34.77, and no step whose argmax differs.
Both forms generate the same tokens at a 5-token prompt (256 new) and at a 2,048-token prompt (64 new), in every one
of the 10 runs of each arm. The grouped form decodes x1.555 [1.550-1.566] faster after 2,048 tokens (6.515 against
4.186 tok/s) and x1.033 [1.029-1.045] after 5 tokens (9.302 against 9.007). The time to the first token at 2,048 does
not move (x0.997). The peak footprint is bimodal in both arms and gives no clean reading. Nothing is served: under
class B a served CPU config needs a tolerance run first (section "What the decision would need").

WHAT THE DECISION WOULD NEED (class B, so no perplexity gate was run)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The card's license is a paired WikiText-2 perplexity, by prefill and by decode, on 12 windows of 4,096 against a
band of +-0.12 % (gqa-ppl-2026-11-01). The same reading on the CPU:
  - by decode: `cpurun lockstep` extended to 12 windows of 4,096 with a paired NLL interval (the mode prints one
    window's two perplexities today). Estimated wall time on this Mac at 6 threads: a step costs about 0.111 s +
    6.3e-5 s per cached position under exp and 0.108 s + 2.2e-5 s under grp (fitted on the G-iii rates at 5 and
    2,048 positions, computed), so one window of 4,096 costs about 980 + 630 s, 27 min, and 12 windows about 5.4 h
    (estimated). The fit reproduces this lot's lockstep: 625 s predicted against 617 s measured.
  - by prefill: `cpurun gate` pairs the CPU backend against the dense reconstruction, not two forms; it would need a
    two-config arm. At about 210 s a window of 4,096 for each arm (cpu-tetra-2026-11-07, 12 threads), 12 windows
    would cost about 1.4 h at 12 threads and more at 6 (estimated).
That is one night of this Mac, at $0. It is the operator's call.

WHAT THE READING DOES NOT SAY
-----------------------------
- It serves nothing. library.json, llvq-runtime and the served configs are unchanged.
- One window and one model. The lockstep read 2,047 steps of one WikiText-2 window; the tolerance is not measured.
  Equal tokens over 256 + 64 generated tokens do not license the form.
- One machine, NEON only, 6 threads, f32. AVX2 and AVX-512, other thread counts and f16 were not run; the bits may
  move differently there.
- The 2,048-token prompt went in one chunk. A chunked prefill, as `llvq serve` may send it, was not run.
- The footprint at 2,048 is bimodal in both arms; this lot did not find why.
