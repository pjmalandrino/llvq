WHAT THE SHAPE SAYS
--------------------
Between 1.05 and 1.08 the perplexity moves from 42.38 to 52.18, +23 % for three
points of scale. The objective is not gently curved around its minimum; it has a
cliff on the high side. Two consequences.

A global scalar is worth 0.836 % and no more, because it must serve matrices
whose own optima differ. The bias map of 2026-09-14 reads the served-rule bias
from 0.98052 on q_proj at layer 0 to 1.00274 on gate_proj at layer 27, a spread
of 2.22 points around a mean residue of 0.66. One number cannot sit at twelve
optima, and the sweep is measuring the compromise.

And a per-matrix correction cannot be estimated loosely. A matrix pushed three
points past its own optimum is on the cliff. Whatever sets the per-matrix scale
has to be exact, not fitted on a handful of blocks.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
-----------------
Four points, one model, one seed, one evaluation set. It locates a compromise
optimum on the 0.6B and measures nothing about where each matrix's own optimum
lies. It is the empirical anchor a closed-form rule has to reproduce, and that
is the use it is kept for.
