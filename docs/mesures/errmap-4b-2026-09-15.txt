MY PROJECTION WAS WRONG BY A FACTOR OF TWO
--------------------------------------------
Before the run I estimated -2 % to -8 %, reasoning that the 4B is far less
degraded than the 0.6B (x1.32 against x2.15) and so has less to recover. The
measured figure is -14.93 %.

The reasoning was wrong in a way worth recording: the recoverable error is not a
fixed fraction of the degradation. The 0.6B recovered 33.5 % of its excess, the
4B recovers 60 % of its own — a smaller excess, a larger share of it. Depth
apparently helps the correction rather than hurting it, which is the opposite of
what "less to recover" assumed.

THE TEST THAT WAS VOID, AND IT IS MY ERROR
--------------------------------------------
"The map's signs, constant size" reads -0.151024 — identical to the map, to the
digit. That is not a finding. In one-sided mode the curvature is reported as
zero, so `optimum_within` already returns ±T by sign: the map IS a map of signs,
and the arm compared it with itself.

The 96.2 % figure from the 0.6B came from a two-sided map, where the arm meant
something. Nothing here confirms or refutes it, and the run design should have
caught that before spending the arm. It is recorded rather than quietly dropped.
