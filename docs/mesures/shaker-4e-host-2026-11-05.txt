1. ROW FORMS (llvq_pers.cuh, PERS_4E_ROWS, set when the module is built)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1  v3's stream: the default, the served config's 4E, and the module text of every launch so far (no define).
  2  v4: pers_tetra_rows_static, v2's row on v3's static rows.
     - The same lambdas as the stream: row_of, start (lane 0 before the blocks), epi.
     - So v3's mixed q/k/v split, its deferred SiLU, and its residual at a down row's start are kept as they are.
  3  v5: pers_4e_rows_cta.
     - A CTA's rows modulo 16 are split into (row, lane step) units dealt to every warp. Each unit keeps
       pers_fold's factors, t = a x gscale[g] and invnorm[m], in a 16 KB scratch after the activation staging.
     - After a CTA barrier, the owner folds them in i order: the same fma chain, from 0, on the row's blocks.
       Then warp_sum and pers_epilogue.
     - gate/up's binary16 values wait in the scratch's first 2 KB for exact_hmul(silu[g], u) after a barrier,
       the stream's own product.
     - A CTA with more than 512 pairs, or more units than the scratch holds, takes form 2's path. On the 4B's 142
       CTAs: 69 pairs, and at most 40 units a phase.
  Computed gains, from the measured cycles: v5's busiest warp drops from 40 to 35 steps in gate/up, from 26 to 15-16
  in a Tetra down, and from 12 to 9-10 in q|k. That is about -308 us a token on v4's.
  persist4e_cuda::install_rows takes the form; install keeps form 1. LLVQ_PERS_4E now installs the three forms at
  512 threads (the 768-thread symbol spills under the card's driver) and names each "4E v3/v4/v5 at 512 threads".
  devel ptxas 12.4: pers_4e_tokens_f1_t512 120 / 122 / 121 registers, the stamp symbols 125 / 122 / 121, all 0 B
  stack.

2. INNER STAMPS (pers_4e.cu, the stamp symbols only)
---------------------------------------------------
  A layer has 15 events instead of 10, in the order they happen:
    - q/k/v: ln1+rotation done, arrival, release;
    - norm, attention, o: arrival and release each;
    - MLP, inside pers_segment_body's `sub`: ln2+rotation done, gate/up done, the inner wait released, the down's
      input ready; then arrival, release.
  pers::p4e_phase_report reads them as 7 parts (P4E_SUBS), each CTA work, which sum to their phase's mean work. The
  inner wait is checked like a grid wait: its last arrival before its first release.
