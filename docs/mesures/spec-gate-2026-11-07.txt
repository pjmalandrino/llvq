Where the time goes (from the arms' own timings):
- The verify pass does not share enough. Two rows cost 1.95, 2.05 and 2.25 greedy tokens; at 14B more than two 4E
  tokens. Each extra row adds 0.65 to 1.25 of a token, against the 0.378 of the row phases alone (shaker-4e).
  The verify symbol spills at both block sizes (512 threads: 128 registers, 144 B local; 768: 272 B), it runs the
  prologues and attention once per row, and the down shares only at the 4B (2 rows a group; 1 at the 8B and 14B,
  whose inter does not fit twice in 99 KB). The install line printed "4 at hidden" for the 8B and 14B; the kernel's
  groups were 4 and 3 (the line omitted the staged `small`; fixed after the run).
- The drafter costs 2.5 ms a launch at k = 1, 3.9 at k = 2, 5.4 at k = 3: about 1.4 ms a token plus a launch, where a
  token of the 4B costs 4.4. A round that keeps its draft also pays a catch-up launch.
- Acceptance at k = 1 is 0.43 to 0.69 on chat, code and summary and 0.75 on GSM8K: a round emits 1.43 to 1.76 tokens.
