FOUR NEW RESULTS
----------------
1. The shell bound is m <= 26, not 27 (*computed*, llvq-bench/examples/tetrashell.rs, rewritten
   today; tetrashell.txt). The old sweep gave one residue to all eight coordinates of a section,
   so it was not a bound: it printed 144 per section, and a mixed pattern byte reaches 152. The
   new one decomposes over the 4,096 codewords, both p, both r and both delta, takes the
   per-section maxima over the allowed rows, and decodes the word that reaches the maximum:
   n2 = 416, word 0x7994070f3202, m = 26, max |y_j| = 10. A million random words stay under it
   (max 384). The kernel's 32-entry table of 1/sqrt(16m) covers it either way.
2. Tetra against Planes14 on the 2,280-question sample is +2.11 [-0.95, +5.17], McNemar 346/304,
   p = 0.11 (*measured*, mmlupair --no-fpc on the committed dumps mmlu-4b-llvq.csv and
   mmlu-4b-tetra.csv; mmlupair-planes14-tetra-2280.txt). The paper had printed 2.10 without an
   interval. The sample does not separate the two codebooks, before any question of draw or drift.
3. The size-up budgets, summed from unrounded bytes, are 6.39 (Tetra 14B), 3.87 (AWQ 4B) and
   3.31 (AWQ 4B with 4-bit tables), not 6.38, 3.88 and 3.32; the weight differences are 0.10 and
   1.05 GB, not 0.09 and 1.06 (*computed*; sizeup-2026-09-28 is corrected in place, same day).
4. The instruction count is about 150 a block for the rank decode and its 24 multiply-adds, not
   141 (*computed* from llvq_f1rank_v3.cuh). The header's table counts the one-prmt mask, which
   is not compiled; the default mask is two IMAD and one LOP a quad. So 60 FMA-pipe, about 85
   ALU (81 plus up to six LOP.AND on variable selectors), six loads. The served
   llvq_tetra48.cuh adds about 20 for the gain and the norm. The header's own split, 54 + 85,
   did not add to its 141.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE PAPER, BY KIND
-----------------------------------
Wrong labels or wrong objects:
- "Three draws moved MMLU by 2.92": 2.92 is the standard deviation; the range is 5.83, on the
  Planes14 codebook and 2,280 questions (bruit-mmlu-graines-4b-2026-08-25). Three places.
- The 4B MMLU 63.37 was scored on the file one step before sealing, int4 rebuilt at load; the
  sealed file matches it on 57 of 57 questions (embed-q4-swap-2026-09-23, sealed-4b ECARTS E1).
- The cited LLVQ 60.7 / 62.8 use the 0-gain-bit, 48-bit ball of 2.8e14 points, not the 47-bit
  ball with a gain bit that paper 1 served (llvq-paper-notes.md tables 6 and 8).
- "We estimate this price instead of measuring it": it was measured, 88.80 against 91.98 %
  retention on 20,000 Gaussian blocks, 3.2 points on 20,000 real blocks of Qwen3-0.6B
  (f1-encodeur-blocs-reels-2026-09-05).
- "The best tile depends on the card, not on the format": f1d-2026-09-10 shows Planes14 fastest
  at 128 and Tetra at 32 on sm_120; there Tetra runs at 0.82 to 1.03 times Planes14.
- 4B load times 12.1 / 81.8 s were an earlier file; the sealed file loads in 4.1 / 72.9 s.
- v_proj in int4: +3.47 was the 2,280 sample of an arm restored at load; the full test set
  reads +1.73 [0.87, 2.59] (tetra-nu-full-2026-09-18).
- The "15 points below FP16" came from a C4 encoding; the paper now starts from the DCLM base
  of Table 6, about 12 points.
- Planes14 reads a 12 KiB class table; "only Tetra reads tables" and the legend "no tables"
  were wrong. The no-weights control reads the tail and the row scales.
- The Table 2 AWQ arm is llm-awq's GEMV ported to our harness, not Marlin. Its GB/s divides by
  the fastest round.
- 2.148 b/weight bills the tail in f32, as the files store it; the text had claimed f16 for the
  figure. Only the served kernel holds the tail in f16.
Arithmetic and rounding: 54 + 85 = 141 (above); 1.68 -> 1.69 (150/89 = 1.685); 14B row-scale
  loop time 1.8 h, not the 2.0 h job time; "about 92" -> 92.04, 92.95, 92.12.
Claims narrowed to their evidence: the gap to AWQ shrinks from 4B to 14B, and the 4B-to-8B step
  is not separated (z = 1.1); "loses more on GSM8K at 4B only" holds in points, while counted in
  errors it holds at every size; the 256 identical tokens are one prompt that loops; the
  decoder chain is one dependent load, not three; "no three-way cut" -> "no split into three
  sections of eight"; cls uses ranks {1,2,5,6}; one list instead of 256, not 4^8; the
  encoder is a rule, not the nearest point; row scales come from the row norm, not least
  squares; "every measurement on one L40S" -> every table and figure.
Additions for completeness: the same-engine GSM8K gap 9.02 [7.07, 10.97]; two preregistered
  misses and one hit to the prediction table, now captioned Table 8; the 14B stop-rule override;
  the exception of the ten-arm run to preregistration; the online rotation, 96 launches a token.
Figures: Figure 2a now shades a Golay codeword (the old pattern had weight 13); Figure 3 labels
  no longer overflow; Figure 5 legend, axis and caption; Figure 6 labels at two decimals.
Bibliography: Forney's name parses as G. David Forney, Jr.; Vardy and Be'ery are cited for
  decoding, not for the three-section trellis.
Commands: fusedrun needs the spelled-out flags and 256 tokens to reproduce the table; mmlu needs
  LLVQ_MMLU_DUMP for mmlupair to have an input.
