WHAT THE RUN DOES NOT SAY
-------------------------
- The layer judge compared across groups is not what the encoder does. The encoder minimises the layer objective inside
  each matrix, code by code; it never trades one group against another. This run does not show that an output-aware
  objective would change a single code, nor that it would gain a point of MMLU.
- Four disjoint groups are four points. Agreement on an order of four is an indication, not a law; by chance two of
  the 24 orders pass the ordering half of the rule.
- KL and NLL on running text are not MMLU points. Only orders are compared, never magnitudes.
- One calibration draw: this is the sealed file's order, not the encoder's.
- Note 1's x is not the encoder's x (a sealed prefix, its own block at f16, its DCLM draw), and W_hat is no longer the
  encoder's minimiser: it carries row scales trained on KL after encoding, and the 84 int4 records are RTN of W.
  Restoring o, part of qkv and part of down measures their int4 records too.
- The arithmetic is torch, not the candle path of the truth. Rule D bounds the difference on perplexity to 0.033 %; it
  does not remove it.
- The 252 per-matrix values of matrices.tsv are written and not read here.
- Nothing about speed, memory, GSM8K or another size.
