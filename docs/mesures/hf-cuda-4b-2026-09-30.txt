WHAT STAGE 4 ESTABLISHED
------------------------
The three unknowns the prereg named are answered, all yes:

- `nvcc` compiling `llvq_tetra48.cuh` outside NVRTC keeps the arithmetic. Arm 2,
  three shapes, two orders under the bar, reproduced on two instances.
- The buffer binding holds. Arms 3 and 4 ran a whole model through it.
- The `rot_apply` arm works on a card. Every Tetra record of the 4B carries a
  rotation and the ids are exact.

The int4 arm does NOT work there, and the prereg's own text expected it to. That
is the one thing stage 4 refutes about its own plan: CUDA's int4 wall does not
exist in `tv_q4_h.cu`, which serves `d_in` 9,728 today, but nothing binds it as a
torch op. Binding it is a named piece of work, not a discovery.
