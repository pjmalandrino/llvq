THE 4B WINS ON PERPLEXITY AND LOSES ON MMLU, AND THE ORDER REVERSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Against AWQ w4g128 at the same size:

  size   perplexity against AWQ   MMLU against AWQ
  4B            -6.93 %              -4.76 pp
  8B            +3.03 %              -4.21 pp
  14B           +1.91 %              -2.46 pp

The 4B reads 12.58 where AWQ reads 13.52, so it is the better model on
wikitext-2 while scoring 4.76 MMLU points below it. The two metrics also order
the three sizes differently: perplexity names the 4B our best object relative
to AWQ, MMLU names the 14B. This repository already holds six dissociations
between the two; this is the seventh, and the first where the sign of the gap
to a baseline differs between the metrics.

perplexity repaired to 0.74 % of f16 while MMLU stayed nine points down, and
this run adds a case rather than removing one.

file on one card; the per-window NLLs are on disk for anyone who wants to pair
two arms.
