THE MECHANISM THE PREREG NAMED IS NOT REFUTED, IT IS UNTESTED
--------------------------------------------------------------
Section 2 predicted the effect would pass through `down_proj`, the only
activation short of samples: 13.5 per dimension at x1 against 51 for q, k, gate
and up. Nothing in this arm separates the types, so the mechanism is neither
confirmed nor refuted. What is measured is the sign of the whole.

A reading that fits and is not established: more windows from a **contiguous
prefix** of one shard may add redundant text rather than coverage, and a
Hessian averaged over more of the same thing is not better determined, only
smoother. `LLVQ_CALIB_SEED` draws windows at random instead of the prefix and
has never been used on this axis.

WHAT THE EIGHT-TIMES ATTEMPT COST, AND WHY IT WAS KILLED
---------------------------------------------------------
It ran 105 min to block 16 of 36 at 393 s a block, against 178 at x1. The Mac
reached **0.8 GB free, 23.2 GB in the compressor, 20.8 GB of swap and 1.4 TB of
swapouts**. Killing it returned the machine to 53.2 GB free within seconds.

The volume ceiling on that machine is memory, not time, and the estimate that
put the paper's volume at 10.6 h was right on time and silent on memory. The
activations of every window stay alive while the loop advances them block by
block: 20.97 MB a window at 2,048 tokens and hidden 2,560 in f32, so the
paper's 6,100 sequences need **128 GB**. No flavor in `ops/run.py` holds that;
the h200's 141 GB VRAM is the closest and still short with the model loaded.

A phase anomaly worth recording rather than explaining: `advance (pass 2)` took
0.7 s at x1 and 1,120 s at x4. A factor of 1,600 for a factor of 4 in volume is
not accounted for by the volume, and nothing here diagnoses it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
- No significance on a one-point effect: one draw, 2.92 pp of re-encoding noise,
  and -1.20 sits well inside that band. The honest statement is "no benefit
  visible at four times, and the point estimate is negative".
- Nothing about the paper's factor of 95, which is out of reach on this machine
  and on every rentable single card in the list.
- Nothing about random window draws, which is the untested variant above.
- Nothing about bare `Tetra`: this arm carries `v_proj` at int4.
