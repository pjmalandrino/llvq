THE SEVEN ERRORS, ALL CORRECTED
-------------------------------
1. Section 3.4 claimed the pseudocode and the field table gave an implementer everything; the
   output permutation, the three octads and the nibble order were missing. The claim is gone,
   and the octads (0x00149f, 0x0f6840, 0xf08320), the reading order, the four rank lists and
   the tie-break are now stated.
2. Section 5 said the CUDA decoder matches the Rust one on each 48-bit word. The test covers
   4,000 words: the origin and the all-ones word, each with and without the gain bit, and
   random words (llvq-cuda/tests/tetra48_matches_rust.rs).
3. Section 5.2 placed the sm_120 sweep on "other 4B files". Its Tetra file is the one of the
   bench since 2026-10-06; the sentence now says "the same 252 matrices".
4. The 4B FP16 and AWQ perplexities were labelled as read on an Apple GPU. Both were read on an
   L40S on 2026-08-06 (a4-campagne-4b-ppl-BRUT-2026-08-06.txt: 12.2369 and 13.5207). Every
   perplexity of the paper is now an L40S reading, and the Apple-GPU exception and its device
   check are gone. The 4B ratios do not move at one decimal.
5. Table 8's caption counted five kernel arms; it has six since the cuBLAS row.
6. Appendix B said one campaign departed from its preregistration. Several did, and the
   perplexity campaign also overrode a decision rule after the result was known (the 14B
   below its base, published on the paired interval). Both overrides are now named.
7. Appendix B listed three results without a preregistration. The FP16 and AWQ perplexities
   come from runs before 2026-08-10, and two more results had none: the paired cost of
   sealing at 14B and the IQ2_XXS speed.
