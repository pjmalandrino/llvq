The conjecture of R5 is refuted in its premise, on the patch map's own figures. What a band of the stream gives back
when FP16's replaces the sealed one follows the noise energy that band held, not its relative noise. The quiet band
gives back most because it holds most of the noise: it is 2,048 of the 2,560 directions under a noise that is nearly
flat. No encode was run and the noise map of R5 was not run.

Sources: recherche-qualite3-r6-attaque-code-2026-11-15.txt (attack 5 above all) and
recherche-qualite3-r6-attaque-litterature-2026-11-15.txt (section 0 and C), two agents who did not write R5. The

WHAT BREAKS IT
  Noise energy a band at depth 12, share of the stream's energy times noise over signal (*computed*): [1, 64] without
  direction 1 0.000462, [65, 512] 0.001812, [513, 2560] 0.004766. Shape share given back over it: 70.6, 79.3, 70.2.
  It is flat across bands whose signal a direction differs 600 times.
  The ratio s([513, 2560]) / s([65, 512]) reads 2.33 [1.72, 3.39] (*computed* by the attacker, paired bootstrap on
  logits.tsv). Cost by noise energy predicts 2.63, inside. Cost by relative noise, the conjecture, predicts 4.17,
  outside.
  One direct published measurement agrees, on a small model: equal absolute noise in each principal direction of
  GPT-2's stream costs about the same (arXiv 2604.20682, *reported*, no statistic).
  The lead's own section 3 was wrong as well: it counted the loud band twice. Counted once its sign turns, and with
  the loud band's slope known within a factor 100 it decides nothing.
  The form "no bit, no kernel change" is not free: the head is the embedding, and the final norm's gain does not
  fold into it. The ways out cost 0.026 to 0.435 b/param (*computed* by the attacker), and a re-encode in a new basis
  voids the trained row scales.

WHAT STANDS
  Q29's measurements: the depth profile, the quiet band's give-back, the context positions, the early MLP. The
  noise at depth 12 is far flatter than the signal. Rows are coded apart, in 24-wide blocks along one row's inputs.
  The reading of Q29 changes: the deficit rides the quiet band because the noise is there.

WHAT THE ATTACK ADDS
  - A band's give-back is not a band's cost. The three bands give back 0.121 [+0.039, +0.208] more than the whole
    stream (*computed*): their logit errors point the same way.
  - 38 to 55 % of the band "noise" at depth 12 is a linear map of FP16's stream, not a residual (*computed* from
    contrast-map's linear fit). The mis-scale of direction 1 is 4.8 times all the band noise in energy.
  - The int4 g64 embedding already writes a noise that is flat a direction at depth 0, and costs nothing resolved
    (Q13). Flat noise is what any code in the canonical basis writes.
  - F1 is the stream after twelve blocks. What each matrix writes by band was never read.
  - The noise map of R5 could not have decided: Gaussian noise is the wrong null (Q7, the linear part), it tests the
    blocks after depth 12 and not an encoder, and the sealed late blocks are tied to their own inputs (N8).

WHAT IT LEAVES THE AXIS
  On these 570 questions the cost of the first twelve blocks follows the energy of the noise they write. That is Q7
  again, read inside the network: the amplitude of the error carries most of the gap. A layout of the same noise
  energy by variance has nothing to gain. A lower noise energy at the same rate is bounded by the code: 0.60 dB (C7).
  Not excluded: a sensitivity that is uneven over directions in a basis that is not the variance order. The
  published two-sided objective prices the output error by a measured downstream sensitivity and reports gains at 2
  bits on another code (YAQA, *reported*: KL 0.356 to 0.241 on Llama-3.1-8B). Whether that sensitivity is uneven on
  the sealed 4B's stream is not measured here. The one-sided form did not gain (Q24).
