THE 4B QUALITY GAP TO FP16: WHERE IT SITS, WHAT TUNING HAS LEFT, AND WHETHER ONE MECHANISM CLOSES HALF (2026-11-12)
=====================================================================================================================
Nature: desk study, $0. No job, no build, no model load. Arithmetic in one python3 -I one-liner. Web for literature.
Read:   docs/ETAT.md, docs/ROADMAP-QUALITY.md, docs/HISTORIQUE.md (2026-09-06, 09-14, 09-15), ops/llvqtune/README.md,
        ops/llvqtune/llvqtune/__main__.py, docs/data/jobs.csv, and the journals cited by name below.
Bar:    +3.4 MMLU or +4.8 GSM8K at 4B (half of 6.77 and 9.63), at 2.73 b/param, same VRAM and decode speed, by a
        method that carries to every future model.
Labels: *measured* (a journal), *computed* (arithmetic here), *estimated* (judgement), *reported* (a paper, URL).

VERDICT: UNDER THE BAR
-----------------------
No single mechanism reaches +3.4 MMLU at its central estimate. The best one, an output-aware encoder objective
(YAQA-style) run on the same file format, projects +0.5 to +1.7 MMLU and +1 to +5 GSM8K at 4B. It touches the GSM8K
bar only at the top of its range. Tuning the free parameters with codes frozen is spent: it took 26 % of the base gap
at 4B, and a second pass took nothing. Training with codes that move is refuted in its cheap form (K1) and, in its
full form (QAT), costs 10 to 50 times more for a gain the 2026 literature does not separate from a good PTQ objective.

Unattributed by any measurement on the sealed object: about 5.6 of the 6.77 MMLU points (83 %), and all 9.63 GSM8K
points. GSM8K was never scored before the row scales, so even the row-scale share of GSM8K is unknown.

Three measured facts say what kind of loss the unattributed part is.
  - The error is structured, not amplitude. Gaussian noise of the measured logit amplitude predicts 58.53 for bare
    Tetra; it reads 53.49 (logit-snr:100). Five points come from where the error lands.
  - It lands on a shared fragile bank. 15.6 % of questions flip sign against f16, and independent encodings flip the
    same ones at 2.4 to 3.2 times chance (logit-snr:105-124). Restoring any single projection type repairs
    overlapping sets, Jaccard 22 to 36 % against 3 to 6 % chance (logit-snr:138).
  - Gains do not add across types: the seven single restores sum to 23.85, all seven together give 14.73, ratio
    0.618 (computed from m2-attribution:53-63). Across matrices of the int4 move they add to 96 %
    (vod-int4-full:27-31).
  - Perplexity is closed and MMLU is not: x1.0074 of f16 after row scales at 61.11 (dclm-rowscales:131-134). The KL
    on DCLM still reads 0.2342 nats a token (dclm-rowscales:72), about 0.20 after the second pass
    (sealed-rownorms:40).

The 4B gain was acquired before step 1,210 of 9,681, 2.4 M tokens (tetranu-rowscales:56-58). Data was never the

Never tried:
  - free_params mode (f16 tails, centroids): code and tests exist, never ran (ops/llvqtune/README.md:26).
  - the int4 records' group scales and offsets, about 12 M values, and the q4 head's, about 12 M (computed). They are
    min/max RTN from the checkpoint and excluded from every training (sealed-rownorms:48). This is mlx-lm's DWQ.
  - rotation signs (QuIP# trains them as real vectors). The input-side diagonal is close to the RMSNorm training,
    which moved +0.02.
  - block-wise training of any kind (output matching per transformer block). Every run is global.
  - any corpus other than DCLM-edu. The mmlu-aux arm was cancelled at $0 on 2026-09-23 (ROADMAP-QUALITY.md:73).
  - codes that move. The llvqtune invariant forbids a gradient through the decoder (README.md:63).

4. JUDGEMENT
------------
Exchange rate. The repository's law gives 7.3 MMLU points per e-fold of noise power. YAQA's own Qwen3-8B 2-bit
points give 8.5 points per e-fold of KL (computed: 63.94 at KL 0.285 to 66.75 at 0.205). The two agree. Half the
gap, +3.4, asks noise power / 1.59, -2.0 dB, or KL from about 0.20 to about 0.13. The whole codebook to Shannon is
worth about +2.3. So half the gap needs a lever bigger than a perfect codebook, at the same bits, and it must act on
the structure of the error, not its size. Caveat: the row scales cut training KL 3.8 % for +3.15 MMLU, so KL does not
predict MMLU on this object; the law is an order of magnitude.

Candidates against the bar:
  - More frozen-code tuning (free_params, int4 DWQ, norms, signs, more tokens, blockwise P-step): +0.2 to +0.8.
    Measured second pass +0.02. The int4 and head tiers hold at most about 0.9 of the 6.77. UNDER.
  - Codes that move by lattice steps (K1, PV V-step): refuted, step 0.332 of block norm > error 0.290, ceiling
    +0.15 to +1.30 with a perfect gradient (refutation-k1-maths-2026-11-11.txt:80-110). UNDER.
  - Gain-bit flips by gradient: the cheapest code move our frame allows. Its encoder form is closed (Euclidean gain
    rule dead end to end, ETAT.md:228). UNDER.
  - Full QAT: unknown gain, LC-QAT shows parity with a good PTQ objective, $500 to $1,500 at 8B and a GPU Tetra
    encoder that does not exist (recherche-methode:286-295). Fails the cost axis; not "the same object cheaply".
  - Blockwise compensation at encode (QuIP#/QTIP style, P5 of recherche-methode): +0 to +1.5 above row scales; the
    closest analog, QTIP's whole FT on Qwen3-4B, gave +2.1 and includes the end-to-end half we already took. UNDER.
  - Output-aware objective in the encoder (YAQA, P1 of recherche-methode): the best of the lot. Below.

The one mechanism, if one: YAQA-style output-aware rounding objective, then today's row-scale distillation.
  What:       replace the per-row tr(E H_in E') of GPTQ by tr(H_O E H_in E'), H_O a sketched output Kronecker factor
              from one torch backward over calibration text. The Tetra search, the file, the kernels do not change.
  Range at 4B: MMLU +0.5 to +1.7, centre +1.0; GSM8K +1 to +5, centre +2.5 (estimated). Reasoning: YAQA closes 23 %
              of the MMLU gap and 27 % of the GSM8K gap on Qwen3-8B 2-bit (computed from its table); transferred to
              our gaps that is +1.6 and +2.6; our row scales then absorb part of it (YAQA's KL edge falls to 10 to
              20 % after recovery fine-tuning), and gains here compose at 0.62 to 0.79. Chance of passing the bar:
              about 5 % on MMLU, 15 to 20 % on GSM8K (estimated).
  Frame-specific? No. A fixed decoder table means the encoder is the only place output information can enter, which
              makes the encoder objective our main lever, but it does not make YAQA cheaper here. Our row-by-row
              block-of-24 sweep has no cross-row feedback today; that is new code on both axes.
  Definitive? Yes as a method: model-agnostic, recurs per model at the sketch price.
  Other criteria: b/param, VRAM, decode speed, first token unchanged (same format, same bytes). Production cost: sketch
              1 to 2 L40S-hours at 4B ($2 to $4), encode x1.2 to x2 (2 h 27 to 3 to 5 h on the Mac), H_O storage about
              31 GB at 4B (estimated, recherche-methode:140-142).
  Cost at 4B: 5 to 10 agent-days of code; per arm sketch $2 to $4 + Mac encode $0 + row scales $4.00 + MMLU $0.70 +
              GSM8K $2.13, about $10 to $11; three draws per arm against the draw sigma, two arms: about $60 to $65 and
              4 to 6 days of wall clock (estimated).
  Refuted by: (a) the served error is not output-aligned: if the ratio tr(H_O E H_in E') / (mean diag(H_O) x
              tr(E H_in E')) sits within 1.0 +- 0.1 on the sealed 4B's matrices and H_O's eigenvalue spread is small,
              there is nothing to steer; (b) held-out KL to f16 after row scales not 10 % lower than the GPTQ arm in all
              three draws at 1.7B; (c) the YAQA arm breaks the same fragile bank (overlap lift still about 2.6).
  First experiment: (a), the output-alignment ratio of the sealed 4B's error. Mac, no card, $0, needs the operator's go
              for a model load: one backward on a few windows for H_O of a sample of matrices, H_in from hcapture
              (exists), E = decoded minus checkpoint. Kill if the ratio is near 1 and H_O is flat.

Adversarial checks against the repository's negatives.
  - Spherical GPTQ feedback (+31.89 % ppl): a change of the encoder's feedback that composed badly. YAQA is also a
    feedback change; the same failure mode is possible and the KL gate must come before any MMLU spend.
  - Five local-proxy improvements composed worse here (ROADMAP-QUALITY.md:71). YAQA's proxy is less local, not global.
  - The calibration draw is a hyperparameter (sd 1.7 to 2.92): one encode per arm cannot read a +1 effect.
  - KL is not an MMLU proxy on this object (row scales: KL -3.8 %, MMLU +3.15; second pass: KL -24 %, MMLU +0.02).
    The projection above leans on YAQA's own MMLU and GSM8K, not on KL.
  - ROADMAP-QUALITY L10 scored the lever 0 on a misreading (gains-criteres-qualite:118-121); not an ETAT §6 lead.

WHAT I DID NOT CHECK

- GSM8K attribution of any kind; the GSM8K effect of the row scales.
- YAQA's and LC-QAT's numbers were read from tonight's survey, not re-fetched. EfficientQAT and QTIP were fetched.
- PrismML Bonsai's recipe (unpublished).
- Whether H_O at 4B fits the Mac (31 GB estimated) for the first experiment; it may need a sampled subset of matrices.
- The SNR law's validity for objective changes; it was fitted on encodings and restores.
