THE RESULT THAT MATTERS
------------------------
The corrections do not point the same way. Of the 140 matrices that are not q or
k, 76 want to be dilated and 64 to be shrunk. There is no global scale error:
there is a per-matrix disagreement whose mean is nearly nothing.

That is why the sweep's global scalar bought only 0.836 %. A single number is
averaging over corrections that cancel. It also explains the sign flip of
prediction 4: the pooled post-hoc scale is 0.99456 and the re-encoded sweep's
minimum is 1.02, and neither is a summary of the other.

Measured, not extrapolated: moving 8 matrices of 196 took the NLL from 3.73499
to 3.66946, which is 41.8875 to 39.2295 of perplexity, -6.35 %, at identical
rate, format and decoder. Subject to the defect at the top of this file.

The map claims -3.0126e-1 of NLL over all 196, which would be 30.99 of
perplexity. That number is an additive extrapolation over 196 terms, 147 of them
pinned at the edge of the trust region, and it is NOT supported by anything here.
The 8-matrix figure is.

WHAT FELL WITH PREDICTION 1
----------------------------
An interpretation written yesterday: that the three objectives order monotonically
by how much of the stack they see — 0.906 for the weights, 0.999 for the layer,
1.02 for the model. The map breaks it. The model's own optimum at FIXED CODES is
0.99456, which sits beside the layer's 0.999 and not beyond it. The 1.02 belongs
to re-encoding, which changes which level each block picks and what later
columns are compensated against.

So the gap is not an horizon effect. Two of the three objectives agree closely
once the comparison is made at fixed codes, and the odd one out differs by being
a different operation. The horizon story needs the depth experiment or it should
be dropped.

LIMITS
-------
The defect at the top. One model, one seed, one corpus. ε = 0.01 and a trust
region of 0.03, with 147 of 196 optima at that edge, so the map is bounded by
its own guard rail rather than by curvature. Cross terms unmeasured; the only
evidence they are small is the 9.25 % of the 8-matrix test. Post-hoc scaling is
not re-encoding.
