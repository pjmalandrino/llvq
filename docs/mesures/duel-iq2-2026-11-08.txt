WHAT THE NUMBERS DO NOT SAY
---------------------------
- Our peak after 8,192 tokens is not a storage figure. The same process prefills the 8,192-token prompt eagerly in
  chunks of 4,096 and runs the eager check; the peak is that prefill's. llama-bench prefills the depth with flash
  attention in ubatches of 512. The 4B reaches its first token in 34.5 s on our side (measured, sweep-4b-p8192). The
  peak after 5 tokens is the comparable storage figure.
- unsloth's UD-IQ2_XXS at 4B loops under greedy decoding: 999 of 1,319 GSM8K answers hit the 1,024-token cap repeating
  one sentence. Qwen3's model card advises against greedy decoding; the prereg fixes greedy for every arm. At 8B and
  14B the cap catches 39 and 9 of its answers; IQ2_S 52 / 14 / 1, Tetra 8 / 9 / 5.
- The 14B rung sits 0.067 b/param below us (2.6886 against 2.7556); at 4B and 8B it sits at +-0.05. The 14B quality
  gap is measured against a file 0.12 GB smaller.
- llama.cpp's MMLU picks move with the server batch (D4): 2 of 400 picks on the Mac. Every arm ran at -b 16384.
- Our quality forms differ from theirs (dense reconstruction for MMLU and perplexity, the kernel for GSM8K, f16);
  theirs come from the served engine, as the prereg names them.
- The GB/s use file bytes. At 8B and 14B both files carry an untied input embedding that a token reads one row of:
  llama.cpp keeps it in host memory, we keep it on the card as int4 g64 (0.35 / 0.44 GB).
- Our speeds are same-day readings on Space 0536a87c (D1): 227.4 / 144.5 / 90.0 tok/s, against the 221.5 / 142.5 /
  86.3 published for served v9.
