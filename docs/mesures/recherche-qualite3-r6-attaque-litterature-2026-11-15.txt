R6, attack by the literature on the R5 conjecture (noise shaped by the residual stream's principal basis), 2026-11-15

VERDICT. Standing in its exact form, but weakened on its premise and partly known in its principle.
Exact form ("rotate the stream to its PCA basis so per-row noise follows signal") not found in any paper I could read.
The principle (price output directions by downstream sensitivity) is published as YAQA; the premise (cost set by
relative noise) has one direct contrary measurement (uniform absolute sensitivity, GPT-2) and no supporting one.

Scope. Web search and paper reading only. No code, no model, no job. "Opened" means the HTML full text was read
through a fetch tool, sometimes partially; "abstract only" means only the abstract or a search snippet was seen.
Every number below is *reported* by the cited paper unless marked *inference* (mine) or *computed* (mine).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. One reduction that frames everything below (inference)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A fixed-rate quantizer with one scale per output row writes, at high resolution, an error energy per row proportional
to that row's energy. The total error energy is then proportional to the Frobenius norm of the writer. That norm is
invariant under any orthogonal change of the output basis.
So a change of the stream's basis cannot lower the writer's total output-noise energy. It only moves that energy
between directions. Its gain exists only through a weighting: if the cost of direction k is w_k times its noise
energy, the PCA layout beats a flat layout iff w_k falls with the signal variance s_k^2 strongly enough.
The R5 layout (equal relative noise in every direction) is the optimum of a weighted reverse water-filling exactly
when w_k is proportional to 1/s_k^2 (a whitened, Mahalanobis output metric). With w_k constant, the optimum is equal
absolute noise per direction, which is what a random or Hadamard output rotation already gives.
The conjecture is therefore equivalent to one empirical claim: downstream sensitivity per unit absolute noise falls
roughly as 1/variance across the stream's principal directions. Section C is the test of that claim.

A second caveat (inference). The noise in direction k after the rotation follows the writer's own energy in k, not
the stream's variance in k. The stream at depth 12 sums the embedding and all earlier writers, and its top
directions in Qwen-class models are dominated by massive activations on a few tokens (Sun et al., below). A writer
row aligned with such a direction carries weight noise to every token, while the signal there is large only on the
few tokens that carry the massive activation. Pooled PCA variance can thus overstate what an ordinary token can
bear in the top directions.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Basis changes of the stream chosen for quantization
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A1. SliceGPT, arXiv 2401.15024, 2024. Abstract opened.
Measured: PCA rotation of the residual stream folded into weights (computational invariance under RMSNorm), then
deletion of the lowest-variance directions, up to 25% of parameters; 99% zero-shot retained on LLaMA2-70B and OPT-66B,
90% on Phi-2. Not a quantization method.
Bearing: the exact folding machinery R5 proposes exists and is cheap. It was used to delete quiet directions, the
opposite of protecting them. Its success at 25% says the quietest quarter carries little at 70B scale; the R5 patch
band is 80% of directions, so the two are not in contradiction (inference).

A2. QuaRot, arXiv 2404.00456, 2024. Abstract and snippets only.
Random Hadamard rotation of the stream, inherited from SliceGPT's invariance. W4A4 headline; GPTQ weights. Random,
for incoherence. No PCA arm.

A3. SpinQuant, arXiv 2405.16406, 2024. Opened.
Measured: LLaMA-2 7B, W4A4, RTN, 100 random rotations spread 13 points of zero-shot accuracy (Fig. 4); random
float rotation 49.4 +- 2.8, random Hadamard 59.0 +- 1.0, learned (Cayley) 61.6 to 61.8 (Table 4).
Weight-only W4A16 (Table 17): SpinQuant 65.9 avg / 5.6 ppl against GPTQ 64.5 / 11.3 on 7B; 72.6 / 3.5 against
71.9 / 3.9 on 70B. No W3A16 or W2A16; no PCA arm.
Bearing: the basis of the stream matters a lot under activation quantization, much less and unisolated under
weight-only. Learned rotations stay close to their Hadamard start (a 2026 snippet, not opened).

A4. DFRot, arXiv 2412.00648, 2024. Abstract and snippet only.
Reported: the gap between random Hadamard and random orthogonal in QuaRot vanishes once the tokens with massive
activations are kept in FP16. W4A4. Bearing: the stream's few massive-activation tokens drive basis effects. This is
the token-sparse structure that caveat 2 of section 0 raises.

A5. ResQ, arXiv 2412.14363, ICML 2025. Abstract and HTML summary only.
PCA of activations picks a subspace of about 1/8 of the hidden size kept at 8 bits, the rest at 4 bits, with random
rotations inside each subspace. Weights, activations and KV cache. Reported up to 33% lower WikiText ppl than
SpinQuant on Llama and Qwen2.5.
Bearing: the closest published use of the stream's PCA basis for quantization. It spends more bits on loud
directions and then still randomizes inside each band. It never lets noise follow signal at equal rate.

A6. ROSAQ, arXiv 2506.13472, 2025. Abstract only.
PCA of a layer's input activations; top-eigenvalue directions kept in FP16, the rest INT3/INT4; weight-only. Input
side, salience by variance, mixed precision. Not the output side, not the stream.

A7. OSTQuant (2501.13987), FlatQuant (2410.09426), KurTail (2503.01483), DartQuant (2511.04063), 2024-2025.
Abstract level only. All learned or calibrated transforms aimed at flattening distributions, W4A4 headline. None is
a PCA basis for weight-only coding. BASE-Q (2506.15689, abstract only) keeps rotations fixed and adds bias
correction; reported gap closures of 50.5% vs QuaRot, 42.9% vs SpinQuant, 29.2% vs OSTQuant, W4A4.

A8. CAT, Federici, van Breugel, Whatmough, Nagel, arXiv 2603.04359, ICML 2026. Opened.
Measured: SQNR splits into a concentration term and an alignment term between weight and activation dominant
directions; no orthogonal transform changes alignment. CAT uses (Sigma_w # Sigma_x^-1)^(1/2) per input block,
then a Hadamard. W4A4 RTN, Qwen3-8B: CAT block 10.82 ppl / 66.64 avg untrained, 10.50 / 66.93 trained, against QuaRot
13.69 / 59.82 (Table 1). Input side, per layer, not the stream; no PCA arm.
Bearing: the data-driven basis that wins is not the PCA basis; it is a geometric mean with an inverse covariance,
then incoherence on top.

A9. "Transforms for LLM Quantization: The Great Inversion and Format Co-Design", arXiv 2608.25188, 2026. Opened
in part (three of four chunks).
Claims (theory): the KLT (PCA) is optimal for allocation-flexible coding and concentrates energy; a shared-scale,
equal-rate kernel wants the opposite, flattening. Quote: "Where the KLT concentrates, the deployed kernel wants a
transform that spreads."
Measured: one TinyLlama-1.1B q_proj, G=128, shared-scale cost over the flat floor: Hadamard 3.1x, identity 1.3x, KLT
1.05x (Fig. 3; the authors call it an illustration). It also relays Ordentlich and Polyanskiy (2026) on
Llama-3-8B covariances: GPTQ with a random rotation lands within about 0.1 bit of WaterSIC.
Bearing: the theory is about the axis inside a scale group (the input axis here, which R5 leaves incoherent). R5
rotates the output axis, where each row has its own scale and its own 48-bit blocks; the concentration argument
does not transfer directly (inference). The survey's 43-method taxonomy contains no output-axis PCA method that I saw.

A10. Influence-inspired spectral rotations (BBT), arXiv 2605.25203, 2026. Opened.
Fixed Walsh-Hadamard plus per-input-channel scaling by a spectral energy proxy, weight-only W2A16 g64 with
AutoRound. Qwen3-0.6B ppl 136.76 to 88.99 with a per-head PCA variant; Qwen2.5-1.5B 36.93 to 28.16. No comparison
against QuaRot, SpinQuant or a plain PCA basis; W4A16 shows no gain (9.65 vs 9.71).

Answer to A's key question. No paper I found compares a residual-stream PCA basis against a random or learned basis
for weight-only coding at 2 to 3 bits. The established weight-only low-bit practice (QuIP, QuIP#, QTIP, the
project's own LLVQ basis) randomizes both axes of every matrix. I found no paper reporting that a PCA basis hurt in
weight-only coding, and none reporting that it helped.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Output-side and two-sided objectives
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

B1. YAQA, Tseng, Sun, De Sa, arXiv 2505.22988, ICML 2026. Opened.
Measured: Kronecker Hessian H_O x H_I of the end-to-end KL; H_O is an output-side factor from gradients at the
layer's output. QTIP quantizer, incoherence processing, no finetuning (Table 1). KL Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 2 bits:
LDLQ 0.356, YAQA-A 0.284, YAQA-B 0.241; at 3 bits 0.069 / 0.050 / 0.044. 70B at 2 bits 0.497 / 0.378 / 0.335.
Zero-shot average, 8B at 2 bits: 62.51 / 64.06 / 64.32. No MMLU, no generation task.
Reported: real H_O has strong spectral decay and is close to low rank (Fig. 3); no quantitative spectrum and no
relation to the stream's principal directions.
Bearing: this is the principled form of the R5 premise (stop pricing every output direction alike) and it is
published, with about -30% KL and +1.8 zero-shot points at 2 bits on 8B. It uses the measured sensitivity H_O, not
1/variance. Whether H_O's top eigenvectors sit in the stream's quiet or loud directions is not reported.

B2. OAC, arXiv 2405.15025, AAAI 2025. Abstract only.
Output cross-entropy Hessian per layer instead of the layer-wise l2; reported best at 2 bits and binary over SpQR
and BiLLM. No per-direction analysis seen.

B3. GuidedQuant, arXiv 2505.07004, 2025. Abstract only.
End-loss gradients on layer outputs weight the layer objective, plug-in for scalar, vector and W+A quantizers;
reported consistent gains on Llama-2-7B. Numbers at 2 bits not retrieved.

B4. Output-aware residual stream pruning, arXiv 2609.35579, 2026. Abstract only.
Selects the kept residual subspace by eigendecomposition of a sensitivity-weighted covariance (second-order output
KL), instead of activation covariance alone; reports lower KL and better perplexity than activation-only pruning.
Bearing: the literature's move is from "variance" to "variance times sensitivity", not to "relative to variance".

B5. WaterSIC, Lifar, Savkin, Ordentlich, Polyanskiy, arXiv 2603.04956, ICML 2026. Abstract only.
Water-filling rate allocation across input columns; within 0.255 bit of the information-theoretic limit for any
input covariance; reported state of the art from 1 to 4 bits on Llama and Qwen. Input-side, layer-wise MSE.

No paper found that weights a layer's output error by the inverse of the output's own variance.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. The premise: sensitivity per unit relative noise across principal directions
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

C1. "Variance Is Not Importance", Salfati, arXiv 2604.20682, 2026. Opened (HTML).
Measured: Gaussian noise along each PCA direction of (apparently) the residual stream, with the SAME absolute size
in every direction (sigma = 1% of the token's l2 norm); sensitivity "approximately uniform across all 768
dimensions" of GPT-2, stated qualitatively, no statistic. PCA projection of GPT-2 blocks 3 to 8: 256 of 768
directions (99.9% of variance) give ppl 145 against 47.
Bearing: this is the only direct measurement found, and it contradicts the premise. Uniform sensitivity per unit
ABSOLUTE noise means cost follows noise energy (w_k constant), which makes the layer-wise objective right and the
flat layout optimal (section 0). Weak points: GPT-2 only, one layer unstated, single author, no number given for
the uniformity.

C2. Geometric and behavioral stratification of residual streams, arXiv 2608.12447, 2026. Opened in part.
Measured on 18 models, 7B to 120B: permuting and sign-flipping the low-variance complement F (norm kept) costs 2.8
nats mean KL; scaling F by 0.5 costs 0.047 nats, about the same as rotating the top-variance strata (0.005 to 0.052).
Reported: the top strata have higher causal weight per dimension; F dominates in aggregate by its dimension count.
Bearing: mixed. The quiet complement is load-bearing in direction, tolerant in magnitude. Higher per-dimension weight
in the loud strata runs against w_k proportional to 1/s_k^2 (inference; the paper does not normalise per unit noise).

C3. Massive activations, Sun et al., arXiv 2402.17762, 2024; rogue dimensions, Timkey and van Schijndel, arXiv
2109.04404, 2021. Abstract level, from prior knowledge, not reopened.
A few dimensions carry huge values on a few tokens and act as biases; a few dimensions dominate variance without
driving behaviour. Bearing: the top principal directions are token-sparse and partly bias-like, which supports
"loud directions tolerate noise" for ordinary tokens only if their noise is also token-sparse; weight noise is not.

C4. Sensitive directions, Heimersheim and Mendel 2024, and Lee and Heimersheim, arXiv 2410.12555, 2024. Secondary
sources only. Real activations sit on plateaus; directions toward other real activations are more sensitive than
random ones, and the original random baseline was flawed. Nothing per principal direction.

C5. The R5 patch result read against C1 (inference, computed). If the stream noise at depth 12 is nearly flat over
directions, the band 513 to 2,560 (2,048 of 2,560 directions) holds about 80% of the noise energy. A cost set by
noise energy (C1) predicts about 80% recovery from patching that band. A cost set by relative noise predicts about
98% if the R5 ratios are amplitude ratios (2,048 x 0.64^2 against 448 x 0.15^2), about 95% if they are power ratios.
Measured: 86%. The point sits between the two hypotheses, closer to the energy one, and patching is not additive.
The patch map does not discriminate the conjecture from its null; this matches the authors' own caution.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Noise shaping and weighted rate-distortion
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1. Weighted reverse water-filling (textbook, Cover and Thomas ch. 10; perceptual transform coding). For independent
Gaussian components with variances s_k^2 and distortion weights w_k, the optimal per-component distortion is
min(theta / w_k, s_k^2). Equal relative noise is optimal only for w_k proportional to 1/s_k^2. Fixed equal rate per
row cannot water-fill; it forces D_k proportional to s_k^2 in whatever basis the rows live (section 0).

D2. GPFQ, Lybrand and Saab, arXiv 2010.15979, 2021; SPFQ, Zhang and Saab, arXiv 2309.10975, 2023. Abstract level.
Greedy path-following quantization with error feedback, a sigma-delta-like dynamical system along the input axis.
GPTQ's feedback is the same mechanism. Neither shapes noise across output directions.

D3. Survey 2608.25188 (A9) treats error feedback but no output-direction noise shaping. No paper found that applies
classical spectral noise shaping across the output directions of a weight matrix by a downstream masking model.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. The decisive question
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

E1. Published in this or an equivalent form? NOT FOUND for the exact form: an orthogonal PCA basis of the residual
stream folded into writers and readers so that a per-row fixed-rate quantizer writes noise that follows signal.
The machinery is published (SliceGPT folding). The principle of non-uniform output pricing is published (YAQA,
OAC, GuidedQuant, output-aware pruning), each using measured output sensitivity, not inverse variance. ResQ and
ROSAQ use a PCA basis to give loud directions MORE precision, the reverse of letting noise grow with signal.

E2. Is the opposite the established practice? Yes. QuIP, QuIP#, QTIP and the LLVQ baseline randomize both axes of
every matrix at 2 bits; QuIP# Algorithm 3 rotates the output dimension m and the input dimension n
(arXiv 2402.04396, opened). Reported 2-bit Llama-2 WikiText ppl at ctx 4096 with RHT: 6.19 / 5.35 / 3.91 (7B / 13B /
70B, with finetuning), 8.22 / 6.06 / 4.16 without. No paper I found ablates the output-side half of that rotation.

E3. Does that practice's success count against the conjecture? Weakly. Incoherence on the output axis is justified
by bounds on rounding (LDLQ's proof) and by layer MSE, which section 0 shows is basis-invariant for a per-row
scaled quantizer anyway. Its success says nothing about the weighting w_k, which is the only thing the conjecture
bets on. The evidence that does bear on w_k is C1 (against, GPT-2, qualitative), C2 (mixed, per-dimension weight
higher in loud strata), YAQA's low-rank H_O (sensitivity is anisotropic, direction unreported), and C5 (the
project's own patch point, non-discriminating).

Where I am unsure. C1 is one small-model preprint with no statistic. I could not read YAQA's H_O spectrum against
the stream basis, which would settle the matter cheaply for anyone holding a YAQA run. Chunk four of A9 (348 KB)
was not read; an output-axis method could hide there. The 2026 preprint space is large and moves fast.
