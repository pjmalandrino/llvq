L36, THE CAPTURE-ONLY PASS: IT RUNS ON THE SERVED OBJECT, AND ITS COST IS NOT
WHERE THE SURVEY PUT IT (2026-09-13)
================================================================================
Prereg      : none, and none is owed. Nothing is measured about the model here:
              this is the wiring check of a new instrument, on 256 calibration
              tokens, whose output no row of any table reads. The first run
              that produces a number a row reads needs one.
Code        : branch claude/fine-tuning-cost-mmlu-gain-kic9ua, this commit.
              `cargo build --release -p llvq-llm --features metal --bin hcapture`
Job         : none. **0.00 $.** Mac only, no card, no bucket.
Nature      : *measured* for the wall clocks and the emission counts,
              *computed* for the memory arithmetic, *estimated* where it says so

WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------
  `capture_model_hessians` (llvq-llm/src/calib.rs), the `HessianSink` trait and
  `HBasis` beside it; `Hessian::with_moments`; the `hcapture` binary; and the
  extraction of `CalibCorpus`, `calib_chars`, `window_starts` and
  `ROTATION_SEED` out of `bin/smoke.rs` into `corpus.rs` and `calib.rs`.

  That extraction is the point, not a tidy-up. The capture has to draw the
  **same windows** as the encoding it describes; a copy of the window builder
  would have drifted, and an H captured on other windows is the right shape,
  the right symmetry and the wrong matrix.

THE WIRING CHECK
----------------
  LLVQ_CALIB=c4 hcapture ~/tetra-q5-2026-09-09/qwen3-4b-tetra-q5.bin \
                2 128 metal /tmp/hcap

    served object      252 matrices, 3 633 315 840 quantized weights, 1.795 GB
    load               39.2 s
    emissions          **144** = 36 blocks x 4 activations, one each
    capture            7.2 s     (the forward passes)
    reduce             126.3 s   (f64 readback, rotation, writing)
    total              172.7 s
    output             433 files, 11 MB (diagonal, E[x], per-token norms)

  The dump is provably in the encoder's basis. `meta.csv` carries the seed each
  activation was rotated by, and the four of block 0 read

    Attn 17 891 053 · AttnOut 17 956 589 · Mlp 18 022 125 · MlpOut 18 087 661

  which is `effective_rotation_seed(0x110FEED, 0, act)` for act index 0..3,
  exactly (*computed*). A capture rotated by any other seed describes a basis
  the served model never used, and nothing in the numbers would show it.

THE COST IS IN THE REDUCE, NOT THE CAPTURE — AND THAT WAS NOT EXPECTED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  126.3 s of reduce against 7.2 s of capture, a factor **17.5** (*measured*).

  The reduce is the f64 readback plus `rotate_hessian`, 144 times. It does not
  depend on the calibration volume: it is the same 126.3 s at 256 tokens as at
  131 072. The capture does depend on it.

  So the survey's "one Mac hour once the code exists" is not 394.9 s of pass 1
  plus change. The pass-1 figure it rests on is the **capture** phase of a
  published encoding; the reduce is work that encoding did inside its
  factorization and never billed separately.

  ⚠️ Do not extrapolate the 7.2 s. At 128 tokens per window the accelerator is
  badly under-fed and per-call overhead dominates: scaling it by the 512x token
  ratio gives 3 686 s, which is 9.3 times the 394.9 s the same machine measured
  for the same phase at 64 x 2048. The honest statement is that the capture at
  the served volume is *estimated* at 400 to 600 s, the reduce is *measured* at
  126.3 s, and the total is under 15 minutes — not that it is one hour.

MEMORY, AND IT IS NOT A CONSTRAINT
-----------------------------------
  One block at a time, the four Hessians alive together (*computed*, from the
  widths 2560, 4096, 2560, 9728 read off the config):

    f32 device accumulators    498.07 MB   (MlpOut alone 378.5 MB)
    f64 host copy              996.15 MB   (MlpOut alone 757.1 MB)
    both alive                   1.49 GB

  All 36 blocks retained at once in f64 would be 35.86 GB. Nothing retains
  them: the sink is called per activation and the buffer is reused, so the dump
  costs 11 MB for the reductions and 35.86 GB only under `LLVQ_H_DENSE=1`,
  which is off by default for that reason.

  The survey's "378 MB for `down_proj`" is the **f32 accumulator**, not the
  retained copy. The copy the factor path builds is f64 and twice that.

WHAT THE INSTRUMENT IS LOCKED BY
---------------------------------
  `llvq-llm/tests/l36_capture.rs`, 10 tests, and the one that matters is
  `the_captured_hessian_is_the_one_the_encoder_would_factor`: block 0's four
  emissions equal, entry for entry and with no tolerance, what a hand-rolled
  accumulation over the same windows produces. Five mutants were run against
  the file and all five die:

    M1  drop the pre-rotation shrink            killed
    M2  pass the base seed, not the derived one  killed
    M3  do not advance the hidden states         killed
    M4  accumulate Sx instead of S|x|^2          killed (after the test was fixed)
    M5  drop the 1/N on the first moment         killed (after the test was fixed)

  M4 and M5 **survived the first version of the tests** and are the reason this
  section exists. The norm test checked length, sign and non-zero — every shape
  property and no arithmetic one. What kills M4 is the identity L05 actually
  reads, `S_t |x_t|^2 / N = tr(H)`; what kills M5 is Jensen,
  `|E[x]|^2 <= E[|x|^2] = tr(H)`. Both are exact, both cost one line, and
  neither would have been written without running the mutant.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------
  - Nothing about any row of the quality table. 256 tokens is a wiring check;
    the per-block traces above are not a measurement of anything.
  - The consumer does not exist. `llvq-bench --example hstats` is unwritten, so
    no row is decided yet. The capture is half of L36.
  - L36 decides **eight** rows, not the ten the survey claims. L06's own cell
    asks for a paired re-encode and L15's for kurtosis on `f1recdump` blocks;
    neither reads H, and no statistic in this dump answers them.
  - Nothing was run at the served calibration volume. The 400-600 s above is
    *estimated*, and the operator has given no go for that run.
  - The capture reproduces the encoding's H **by construction and by test on a
    toy model**; it has never been compared against a real block's H from a
    real encoding, because no encoding retains one.
