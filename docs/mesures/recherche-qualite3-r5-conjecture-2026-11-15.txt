QUALITY PHASE 3 (CERVEAU O15), R5: THE CONJECTURE AFTER THE PATCH MAP, AND WHAT WOULD KILL IT, 2026-11-15. $0.
==============================================================================================================================
The patch map says where the first twelve blocks' cost rides: the quiet band of the stream, at the context positions,
written by the MLP (Q29). It does not say that an encoder can do better. This note states the conjecture, the one
measurement that would kill it before any encode, and the lead's own arithmetic, which does not favour its simplest
form. Written by the lead; to be attacked by agents who did not write it (R6).

2. THE CONJECTURE
-----------------
C. What a writing matrix costs the model is set by its noise relative to the stream's signal in the direction where
   the noise lands, not by the noise's energy. The sealed encoder writes a noise that is nearly flat over the
   stream's directions outside the massive one, so the quiet directions take a relative noise 80 times that of the
   loud ones (F1). A noise of the same total energy laid out by what each direction can bear would cost less on MMLU
   and on GSM8K.

A form that needs no bit and no kernel change, if C holds: code the writing matrices in a basis of the stream where
rows are the stream's own directions. A code whose error scales with each row then writes a noise that follows the
signal, direction by direction. The basis change is orthogonal and folds into the weights of the writers and the
readers before encoding, as published rotations of the stream do (QuaRot, SpinQuant, SliceGPT, known to the R0 note
only by name). What it does to the norm gains, the int4 embedding and the int4 records is not worked out here.

3. THE LEAD'S ARITHMETIC AGAINST THE SIMPLEST FORM (*computed*, on point values with wide intervals)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shape share given back a unit of relative noise removed, band by band (F3 over F1): [513, 2560] 0.335 / 0.636 = 0.53;
[65, 512] 0.144 / 0.152 = 0.95; [1, 64] 0.033 over about 0.03 = about 1.1, known only to [-0.4, +2.6].
If cost were linear in relative noise with those slopes, a noise at the same relative level in every band, 0.164,
would cost about 0.164 x (0.53 + 0.95 + 1.1 + 1.1) = 0.60, counting [2, 8] and [9, 64] apart, where today's layout
costs 0.335 + 0.144 + 0.033 = 0.512. Equal relative noise would then gain nothing.
So C in its simplest form, "make the noise follow the signal", is not favoured by today's point values. Everything
hangs on what relative noise costs in the loud bands, which the patch map read with an interval as wide as its
value, and on whether cost is linear in relative noise at all. Both are measurable without an encoder.

4. WHAT KILLS IT, BEFORE ANY ENCODE
-----------------------------------
A noise map, one card job on the patch-map tool. FP16's first twelve blocks, a Gaussian noise added to the stream at
depth 12 at every position, band by band at chosen relative levels, then the sealed blocks as in arm d12. Read as
the patch map reads: shape share, accuracy beside.
  - Validity first. The noise at the measured layout (F1) must bring arm d12 back near the control: s near 0, where
    d12 reads +0.390. If Gaussian noise of the sealed run's own layout does not cost what the sealed blocks 0 to 11
    cost, noise energy by band is not the variable, and the map decides nothing on C.
  - The dose curve of each band: relative noise 0.04, 0.16, 0.64 in one band, none elsewhere.
  - The layouts at equal total noise energy: the measured one; equal relative noise, 0.164 in every band; the noise
    moved to the loud bands.
  C is killed if no layout of equal energy costs less than the measured one beyond the interval. C stands, as a
  conjecture still, if one does; that layout then says what an encoder should aim at, and the next step is the
  attack on the form that would write it.
Cost: of the order of the patch map's, $0.61 for 16 arms (*measured*); to be costed by the agent that writes it.

5. WHAT THE ATTACK SHOULD TRY
-----------------------------
- Is F4 right in what it implies? Does our encoder leave an error that scales with each row, once the gain code, the
  row scales trained after it and the GPTQ feedback are counted? What do the int4 writers (o_proj everywhere,
  down_proj at layers 12 to 23) leave?
- Is the flat noise a property of the encoder or of propagation through twelve blocks? The matrices' own output
  noise by band was never read; only the stream's.
- Is the basis change free? The norm gains, the tied int4 embedding and head, the input rotations the format
  already applies, the kernels.
- Is it published? Rotations of the stream chosen for quantization, by principal directions or learned, at 2 to 3
  bits weight-only, and what they gained.
- Does Gaussian noise stand in for quantization noise here? Q7 says the sealed 4B's error costs more than Gaussian
  noise of its size on the answer logits.
- The GSM8K side: the map reads MMLU only.
