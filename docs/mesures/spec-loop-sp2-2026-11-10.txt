DECISION (the prereg's rule)
--------
Equality holds everywhere and the stand-ins' oracle ratios sit inside their intervals: the loop is sound and its
overhead is known (outside the verify launches, 0.1 to 0.3 % of the loop's wall with the null drafter). DFlash: equality
holds, and its lines are read against their predictions: the acceptance, the end-to-end speeds and the head's share
fail; the drafter's isolated equivalence and its round time hold.

READING (not part of the decision; *computed* from the tables above)
-------
- The stand-ins price the loop: the null drafter's x0.62 is a12's c_1 (1.60) plus nothing; oracle:14 loses 15 % to its
  check line, oracle:3 2 to 4 %. The host outside the launches is under 1.3 ms in 64 tokens.
- DFlash proposes, the verify pass accepts almost nothing, and equality holds: the loop does what it must when the
  drafter is wrong. The drafter itself is right against its fixture (f32, 6 of 6). The disagreement is therefore between
  what the loop feeds the drafter on the card (the target's taps of a12 at layers 2, 10, 18, 26, 34, the positions, the
  committed context) and what the fixture's reference fed it. That is the first thing to examine; this job does not
  locate it.
- At zero acceptance each DFlash pass costs a 16-row verify (7.97 ms against the null drafter's 7.05) and a drafter round
  (4.6 ms): about 12.6 ms a token against 4E's 4.4.
