THE PERSISTENT KERNELS' TEXT EQUALS THE SERVED TEXT BIT FOR BIT ON THE MAC: TETRA ROWS IN THREE LAYOUTS, INT4 ROWS, THE ROTATION AT SIX WIDTHS, CANDLE'S NORM TREE, THE ARGMAX KEY, THE TWELVE L2 ARMS, ROUTE P'S MLP SEGMENT AND 4E'S HEAD PHASE (2026-11-04)

Prereg: none. Host replays at $0, step L0 of docs/archive/debit-vague5-2026-11-02/persistent.md §5.
Cost:   $0, no card. clang++ on the dev Mac (arm64), NVRTC 12.4.127 in nvidia/cuda:12.4.1-devel-ubuntu22.04.
Code:   branch claude/serene-bohr-1edf69. llvq-cuda/kernels/{llvq_pers.cuh, tetra48_pers.cu, tetra48_persall.cu,
        pers_segment.cu, pers_ref.cu}; llvq-cuda/src/{pers.rs, persbench.rs}; llvq-llm/src/persist_cuda.rs.
Tests:  llvq-cuda/tests/pers_matches_served.rs (20 tests), llvq-cuda/src/pers.rs (26 unit tests),
        llvq-llm/tests/served_unit_copy.rs (1), llvq-llm/src/boundary.rs (10).
Nature: every equality below is *measured* on the host: the kernel text executed by clang++, compared byte for byte.
        Nothing here ran on a card.

METHOD
------
  - tests/host_grid.h runs a whole cooperative grid on the host: every thread of every CTA a fiber of one
    scheduler, 32-lane warps, real barriers and shuffles, per-CTA shared buffers, spin-waits that yield, and a
    seeded scheduler that leaves runnable fibers out of a pass so warps drift apart.
  - tests/host_pers.cpp is built twice, both without contraction. The served side runs the served text
    (tv_tetra48_h, tv_q4_hh, tv_emb_q4_h, rot_apply, tv_tetra48_seg) and a replay of tv_tetra48_h whose three
    contractible sites can be fused. The unfused replay equals the served text bit for bit on y and on the f32
    value before f2h (4 shapes); the fused replay, with the three fusions the served unit's PTX shows
    (ptx-probe-2026-11-04.txt §2), is the reference. The fused and unfused replays differ on some rows: the sites
    are exercised.
  - Fixtures are built by bit construction, so both builds see the same bytes.

  RMSNorm           6 widths (block 1,024 and block 32), 64 to 768 threads: identical to the replay of candle's

  argmax key        every binary16 pattern at 12 indices: identical to tok_argmax.cu's key.
  L1 persall        a two-part fused group and a single, grids 1 to 12: identical to tv_tetra48_seg site by site.
  L2 arms           all twelve on two layers of the served pattern at the real widths, three launches, three

FINDINGS
--------
  1. Host clang does not stand in for NVVM. With -ffp-contract=fast it fuses the epilogue and leaves
     `acc += t * invnorm` alone: 9 of 16 rows matched the fused model. So the served reference is a replay
     proven equal to the served text when unfused, with the PTX's fusions applied.
  2. Two candle-side contractions cannot change a bit on binary16 inputs: `tmp += xi * xi` in rmsnorm (an 11-bit
     square is exact in binary32) and the int4 dequantization (an 11-bit scale times a 4-bit code). The load-time
     choice between the two rmsnorm spellings, which the design carried, is moot.
  3. A binary16 activation hides site 2: a binary16 tail times a binary16 value is exact. The rows read the rotated
     f32 vector, so the fixtures draw f32 activations.
  4. The draft's chained arms shared one counter array and took their own launch count as the epoch: the second
     chained arm's counter waits were met before its rows existed. Each arm now owns a region and an epoch.
  5. The draft's boundary A/B matched names with ends_with("gate_proj"); every record name ends in .weight, so no
     arm would have split anything.
  6. Comparing binary16 outputs alone is weak: a 1-ulp move of the f32 value survives the narrowing about once in
     8,192 rows. The host and the L2 identity check compare the f32 value before f2h as well.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------
  - Anything on a card: SASS, timing, memory ordering between real SMs, the driver's ptxas, cooperative launch.
  - candle's real SiLU table and rsqrt instruction: both sides call the same stand-ins on the host.
  - 4E's attention, RoPE, KV append and token loop: not written (q/k-norm is the block-32 tree, already tested).
  - persist_cuda.rs and the two fusedrun modes run nowhere on the Mac; they type-check for Linux only.
