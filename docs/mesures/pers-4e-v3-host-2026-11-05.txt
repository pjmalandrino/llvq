WHAT BOUNDS 4E'S ROWS, READ OFF THE CARD'S SASS AND THE V2 PHASE TABLE; 4E V3 ON THE HOST, 2026-11-05
=====================================================================================================

Result: 4E v2's rows are not bound by issue, by the L1/shared pipe or by the decode's arithmetic. They are bound by
latency, row by row, and only on the Tetra rows:
- the int4 rows (o, the head) streamed at 587-588 GB/s on the mean CTA's work (*computed* from the v2 table);
- the Tetra rows (q/k, gate/up, the Tetra downs) streamed at about 260 GB/s.
A Tetra block iteration is 241 instructions on the card (the one-launch pass's: 243), and a d_in 2,560 row has only 4
of them a lane. Every row then pays dependent round trips: its first words, lane 0's rscale and tail, and in gate/up
the SiLU table lookup. On v2's q/k/v phase that is about 6.7 us a row, of which the iterations' issue is at most about
1.5 us (*estimated*).
v3 runs the Tetra rows as one stream a warp, with the lane's next 4 blocks in flight across row boundaries and the
epilogue's loads issued at the row's start; it gives every CTA the same mix of q/k and v rows, and at a short context
stages several tiles of each non-empty unit a step. Every row keeps its summation order: 27 host tests green, 9 new
mutants caught.
Host estimate (*estimated*, ops/pers_4e_v3_estimate.py, from the v2 card table):

  context    v3 tok/s          v2 (measured)
  5          273 [214-333]     178.7
  8,192      166 [145-182]     131.8

300 tok/s needs the Tetra rows at about 610 GB/s (*computed*, same script). One card job is ready, not launched:
ops/jobs/pers-4e-v3.sh under proofs/preregistration-pers-4e-v3-2026-11-05.md (not stamped), about 9 min and $0.27,
ceiling $0.90.

Prereg:  none for this host work ($0); the card reading has its own (above).
Cost:    $0. cuobjdump 12.4 on the card's cubins (pers-4e-v2-2026-11-05-brut/cubin/, driver ptxas 580); the host
         harness; NVRTC 12.4 and ptxas 12.4 in nvidia/cuda:12.4.1-devel-ubuntu22.04; the CUDA half in llvq-check.
Inputs:  pers-4e-v2-2026-11-05.txt and -brut/ (phase table, bench.txt, cubins); pers-sass-2026-11-05.txt.

1. THE CARD'S SASS (pers_4e_tokens_f1_t512 and tv_tetra48_persall, driver ptxas)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The cubins are the ones planesbench's process compiled: the same persistent unit text, compiled by the same driver,
  but with planesbench's tile define. That compile reports 128 registers and a 16 B stack for the 512-thread symbol;
  fusedrun's loaded module reported 126 registers and 0 B local. The loops read the same.
  4E's Tetra block loop (q/k, gate/up, down, 6 copies):
    - 241-242 instructions a warp-iteration: LOP3 46, IMAD 46, PRMT 36, FFMA 25, FADD 24, SHF 19;
    - its loads: LDS.128 6 (x, natural stride 24), LDS 4 + LDS.U8 2 + LDS.U16 1 (the tables, in shared), LD.E 3 (the
      two stream words of the next block, prefetched one iteration ahead, and gscale), one block a lane;
    - dependent chain an iteration: the words, then prefix, branch, rows and suffix (three shared-memory levels), six
      IDP.4A for the norm, invnorm, then a 24-deep FFMA chain.
  tpersall's block loop: 243 instructions, the tables through L1 (LDG.CONSTANT 4 + U8 2 + U16 1), the same chain.
  4E's int4 word loops: 75-80 instructions for 8 weights (one word a lane), and int4 rows stream at 587-588 GB/s.
  Instructions a byte: Tetra 241 / 200 B a warp-iteration = 1.2; int4 75 / 128 B = 0.59.

2. WHAT BOUNDS THE ROWS (from the v2 table, *computed* unless said)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  Bytes a token from the shapes (ops/pers_4e_v3_estimate.py): Tetra q/k 123.4 MB, gate/up 469.1, Tetra down 156.4;
  int4 v 50.1, o 200.5, int4 down 158.8, head 218.8.
    - int4: o 200.5 MB in 341 us of mean CTA work (ctx staging out) = 588 GB/s; head 218.8 MB in 372.6 us = 587 GB/s.
    - Tetra: take out of q/k/v and MLP their fixed work (2-4 and 5-9 us a layer: norms, rotations, barriers) and their
      int4 rows at 560 GB/s. What remains streams the 748.9 MB at 252 to 272 GB/s.
  Not issue. A SM issues 4 warp-instructions a cycle. At 1.2 instructions a byte that is about 1.2 TB/s over 142 SMs
  at 2.52 GHz, above DRAM, and the int4 rows reach 588 with half the instructions a byte.
  Not the L1/shared pipe. 4E holds its tables in shared memory already. On the one-launch pass, tables in shared were
  slower (x0.948, v2 card), but that variant ran 3 CTAs a SM against 4 (24 warps against 32, its bench line): an
  occupancy loss, not a pipe result.
  Not the in-row latency alone. The one-iteration prefetch moved 4E from 290 to 317 GB/s and the one-launch pass by
  x0.994: a row of 4 iterations has little to overlap.
  Latency, per row. A Tetra row runs 4 iterations then a serial tail on lane 0:
    - warp_sum;
    - rscale[r] and the 16-column tail, global loads issued only now;
    - in gate/up, the SiLU table at index g, a load that depends on the row's result;
    - the next row's first words, also issued only now.
  The q/k CTAs take 2.71 rows a warp a layer in 21.2 us of mean work, less 2-4 us of norm and rotation: about 6.7 us
  a row (*estimated*). Four iterations at the issue rate of 4 warps a scheduler (964 cycles at 2.52 GHz) are about
  1.5 us of it; the rest is round trips, several per row, none of them hidden by the 16 warps a SM. The MLP phase,
  4.28 pairs a warp a layer, reads the same.
  The int4 rows have none of it: no tail, no rscale, and the o rows were already in L2 from v2's prefetch.

3. WHY 768 THREADS SPILLS ON THE DRIVER
---------------------------------------
  At 768 threads a thread holds at most 80 registers (65,536 / 768, in steps of 8). Under the devel ptxas 12.4 the v2
  token symbol fit in 80 with no local memory; the card's driver ptxas (580) gave it a 96 B stack (cubin res-usage:
  REG 80, STACK 96), and the runtime refuses any local memory. The live state across the token loop does not fit 80
  under that allocator: the launch and layer pointers, the shared-table pointers, the loop counters, and the
  attention phase's stage and fold. v3 does not pursue 768. The stream needs a 4-deep ring and the epilogue's 9
  registers, about 120 in all, which only the 512-thread geometry has. The 768 symbols keep v2's rows, and the
  runtime will refuse them on this driver as before.

4. WHAT V3 CHANGES (each keeps every bit)
-----------------------------------------
  The Tetra row stream (llvq_pers.cuh::pers_tetra_stream), the 512-thread symbols only:
    - a warp takes a static list of rows (lo + warp + k x nwarps);
    - the lane's blocks of the whole list form one sequence, item k x nit + i for block lane + 32 i of row k, with nit
      the same on every lane;
    - the next 4 items are always in flight, from a 4-slot ring indexed at compile time, across row boundaries;
    - the (row, item) counters advance by counting. A first draft divided an item index each item, which cost about
      160 instructions a block more (devel SASS);
    - lane 0 loads the row's rscale, its tail as one or two 16-byte chunks, and START's extras when the row's first
      item is consumed;
    - per lane and per row: the blocks summed in increasing j, warp_sum, then sites 2 and 3 as pers_epilogue spells
      them.
  Call sites:
    - q/k: one list over q | k;
    - gate/up: gate then up as consecutive rows. Lane 0 keeps g; at up it issues the SiLU load and stores the
      previous pair's activation, the same product exact_silu_mul makes;
    - Tetra down: the residual x[r] is loaded at the row's start;
    - int4 downs and v: static by warp with the word prefetch.
  q/k/v balance: in the stream path each CTA takes [qk c / g, qk (c + 1) / g) of q | k and [v c / g, v (c + 1) / g)
  of v. v2's contiguous slice left the int4 v rows to the last 24 CTAs, whose 2.7 int4 rows a warp would set the
  phase's wall once the Tetra rows stream. The q/k/v L2 prefetch follows the same split.
  Attention at a short context:
    - a split with 1 or 2 non-empty units stages 4 or 2 tiles of each a step (3 or 4: 1 tile, attn_dec.cu's step), so
      a row of 64 positions takes 2 steps instead of 8;
    - each unit still runs its tiles in order;
    - a __syncwarp at a tile's start orders the previous tile's state writes in the same warp;
    - a CTA with an attention job issues its L2 prefetches after the job, a CTA without one at once. In v2, 8 CTAs
      carried the phase, 737 us against a mean of 260, with up to 130 KB of prefetches issued before their job.

------------------
  tests/pers_matches_served.rs, 27 tests green; llvq-cuda and llvq-llm libraries green (364 tests in all).
    - the_4e_layer_phases_are_the_served_chain: q/k/v ('V') and o ('W') on the stream path, grids of 3 and 4 or 5

  New mutants, each caught:
    - stream_tail_half, the second tail chunk never loaded;
    - stream_lane_order, a lane's blocks reversed;
    - stream_slot, a primed item in the wrong slot;
    - k_offset and v_reads_rotated, retargeted to the stream path;
    - silu_deferred, the wrong table entry;
    - silu_last_pair, the last pair never stored;
    - down_residual_row, the residual of the next row;
    - attn_step_tile_order, a step's tiles read out of order.
  Registers (devel ptxas 12.4, indicative): pers_4e_tokens_f*_t512 120, 0 B; pers_4e_stamps_f*_t512 123, 0 B; the
  768 symbols 80 with an 8 B stack (refused on the card as in v2).
