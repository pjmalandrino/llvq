4E V2 ON THE HOST: THE ATTENTION PHASE FOUND AND REBUILT, THE NORM SPREAD, THE ROWS' PREFETCH, 2026-11-05
==========================================================================================================

Result: 4E's attention phase grew token by token because each (query head, 64-position unit) was one warp that
walked its positions one at a time through dependent 2-byte loads, about 1.43 us a position a layer on the card
(*computed* from R3: 581 to 3,835 us a token over 63 positions and 36 layers). At a short context those were 32
warps on CTAs 0 and 1, and every other CTA waited for them at a grid barrier inside the phase. At 8,192, the 129 unit
states of each head were then folded serially by one warp. v2 runs attn_dec.cu's mapping on the persistent grid,
bit for bit with attn_ref.rs. It also:
- spreads norm/RoPE/append over 48 CTAs;
- keeps the rows' next block or word in flight at 512 threads;
- stages the decoder tables once a launch;
- asks L2 for a later phase's rows;
- refuses positions below the served threshold 1,280 outside a measurement.
Host estimate for 4E v2 at 512 threads (*estimated*, ops/pers_4e_v2_estimate.py, from R3's measured table):

  context    token time               tok/s

490 GB/s on the mean CTA's work (*computed* from the same estimates), and this version is not predicted to get there.

1. WHY THE ATTENTION PHASE GREW, AND COST MORE THAN THE EAGER KERNEL
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  v1 (llvq_pers.cuh section 10 before this commit):
    - phase 1: job = (query head, 64-position unit), one warp, jobs on global warp index, so CTAs 0 and 1 hold the 32
      jobs of a row of 64 positions or less;
    - inside a job, each position j: 8 scalar __ldcg of K, a 4-step shuffle tree, then later 8 x 8 scalar __ldcg of V,
      in a loop whose trip count is the tile's length: the loads of one position wait on the previous one's chain;
    - the 4 query heads of a KV head each reload the same K and V;
    - one grid barrier, then phase 2: one warp a query head folds its units in order through dependent loads.
  Read against the card (R3, *measured*): the wall grows from 581 to 3,835 us a token over the 63 decoded positions,
  1.43 us a position a layer; CTA 0 is the slowest in 82 % of the tokens; the mean CTA's work (2,254) sits near the
  slowest's (2,314), because every CTA's work includes waiting at the inner barrier. At 8,192: 4,128 unit jobs on
  2,272 warps, two rounds of 64-position walks, then 32 warps folding 129 states each, about 280 us a layer, 10 ms a
  token, against 13.3 measured (*estimated*, the order of magnitude).
  The eager kernel computes the same arithmetic with another mapping (attn_dec.cu):
    - one CTA a (split, KV head);
    - the split's tiles staged once into shared memory for every query head, with the next step's loads in flight;
    - the splits folded 8 a round trip by a ticket's last CTA.

2. WHAT V2 DOES (each keeps every bit)
--------------------------------------
  Attention (llvq_pers.cuh section 10):
    - attn_dec.cu's mapping on a persistent CTA: a job is one (split, KV head), job j on CTA j mod grid;
    - each step stages 32 positions (16 KB, K then V) once for the KV head's query heads;
    - at 512 threads (2 chunks a thread) the next step's loads are in flight; at 768 (1 chunk) and on the host's small
      CTAs the step is staged in rounds before its tiles;
    - a task (unit, pair of query heads) is one warp, one head a half-warp, so the 16-lane butterfly stays inside
      each half and every shuffle is a full-warp one;
    - task states live in shared memory, so any number of warps covers the tasks;
    - the units fold into the split state, one thread a (head, dimension);
    - a row of one split writes its output at once; otherwise the split state goes to scratch and the KV head's last
      job (a ticket) folds the splits in order, 8 a round trip, their maxima and sums through shared memory (the
      combine of attn_dec.cu), then resets the counter;
    - no grid barrier inside: 218 a token at 36 layers, against 254 (pers.rs::n_bar_4e, 2 + 6 x layers), the same at
      every position;
    - the tickets are cleared by CTA 0 at launch start, as the argmax slots are;
    - a __syncwarp orders every lane's reads of a task's state before lane 0 rewrites its m and d. The host harness
      caught that race in a draft that read m and d after the shuffles without the __syncwarp. The draft gave wrong
      bits at l = 64; the mutant that removes the __syncwarp is now caught.
  norm/RoPE/append (section 11): head w on CTA w mod grid, one warp a head on 48 SMs. In v1, 16 heads a CTA on CTAs 0
  to 2 shared one SM's FP64 units in the f64 RoPE: 189 us on the slowest CTA, 5.7 on the mean (R3). Each lane's four
  values are read once and serve both the norm and RoPE (the same values in the same order).
  The rows:
    - PREFETCH forms of the int4 rows (pers_q4_row<true>, pers_q4g64_row) and the Tetra rows (pers_tetra_row<.., true>)
      in q/k/v, o, gate/up, down and the head, at 512 threads;
    - the 768-thread symbols run without them (24 warps a SM), because the prefetch costs registers that 80 cannot
      hold (§4);
    - pers_segment_body<.., IN4E = true> does not restage the 18,816 B of tables every layer (4E stages them once a
      launch and never writes them);
    - L2 prefetch (prefetch.global.L2, writes no register and reads no value):
        - each CTA's q/k/v slice before ln1 and the rotation;
        - its o and gate/up slices at the start of attention (8 CTAs work at a short context, the rest wait);
        - its down slice before the MLP's own barrier.
  The served threshold (persist4e_cuda.rs):
    - below position 1,280 the served config runs candle's grouped arithmetic (cuBLAS matmuls, candle's softmax);
    - no hand kernel reproduces those bits: grouped and expanded already differ, since cuBLAS's kernel and its
      reduction order change with the shape;
    - so the runtime refuses a launch that starts below 1,280; the measurement lowers it to 0 against an eager path
      with every row on the kernel (the form R1 to R3 ran, no T8);
    - fusedrun's 4E mode runs the served config itself at 8,192 (every decode row past 1,280, inside the T8);
    - at the short prompt it runs the measurement form and says so;
    - its reference door is now the served kernel-from-1280 config;
    - 4E can therefore serve only from position 1,280. A short-context 4E needs the operator's license of the kernel
      form below 1,280: a T8 extension, read by the paired perplexity at those positions.
  The one-launch pass (tetra48_persall.cu): L1's tv_tetra48_persall is left byte for byte; four variants in a
  separate template:
    - _pf: the next block's words in flight, across tile boundaries;
    - _ts: the tables and invnorm in shared memory after the tile;
    - _ts_pf;
    - _ts_pf_l2: the L2 prefetch of the CTA's next group.
  persbench.rs checks each against A4 bit for bit, times it in the same rounds, and prints its B_pa and its speed over
  tpersall round by round. These are the GB/s 4E's rows need read directly: 300 tok/s needs about 490.

3. HOST PROOFS ($0)
------------------
  llvq-cuda tests/pers_matches_served.rs, 27 tests, all green; llvq-cuda lib 122 tests (one ignored), llvq-llm lib
  green:
    - the_4e_attention_phase_is_attn_ref:
        - 8 lengths (1, 5, 64, 65, 256, 257, 1,300 and 2,049; groups 4 and 5; 1 to 3 KV heads);
        - grids 3 x 64, 5 x 96 and 1 x 512, the last on the in-flight path;
        - the f16 output and the f32 state before the division;
        - every ticket back at zero.
    - the_4e_token_loop_is_the_served_chain:
        - both variants (768 without the rows' prefetch, 512 with);
        - positions 270-271, two splits through the ticket fold, at 64 and 128 threads;
        - barriers (2 + 6 x layers) x grid;
        - EOS.
    - consecutive_4e_decodes and the phase stamps: unchanged and green; the 768-thread stamp symbols included.
    - int4_rows_are_the_served_rows: odd rows on the PREFETCH form.
    - persall_is_the_fused_kernel_site_by_site: all five, grids 1 to 12.
    - the struct mirrors: Pers4eArgs 216 bytes, attn_ticket at 168.
    - pers.rs: n_bar_4e(36) = 218; p4e_smem_bytes against the PERS_ATTN_SMEM text; the symbol tables against the
      kernel text, the stamp symbols at 768 included.
  Mutants, each caught:
    - attention:
        - attn_unit_order, units folded in reverse;
        - attn_tile, tiles of 16;
        - attn_no_fold, no CTA folds;
        - attn_no_reset, a ticket left drawn;
        - attn_stage_pos, a staged position shifted;
        - attn_split_chunk, a split's accumulator mispaired;
        - attn_no_syncwarp;
        - attn_spare_half_writes, a half-warp past the last head writes (groups of 5);
        - attn_carry_fused and attn_scale, rewritten on the new text;
    - int4: q4_pf_group, q4_pf_stale;
    - the one-launch pass: persall_pf_stale, persall_ts_unstaged, persall_pf_tile_restart.
  One mutant the host cannot see, by its nature: "the first ticket folds" behaves like the original under the host's
  serialized fibers, because each early fold resets the counter and the last job folds again on complete data. It is
  not in the suite. The ticket's memory order (fence, atomic, fence) is attn_dec.cu's and occ_sk's pattern, and on
  the card the identity gate tests it.

  Found by compiling patched copies (target/kx/): without the new attention the 768 symbol held 79 registers and no
  spill. Three changes cleared it:
    - one staged chunk a thread at 768 instead of two;
    - the split fold's maxima and sums through shared memory (8 values a thread a chunk instead of 24);
    - a task's accumulators read only after its scores.
  The rows' prefetch costs the 768 symbol about 176 B of spills, hence 768 without it.
  (ops/nvrtc-check.sh's "spill" column is stores plus loads; the stack frame is the local memory.)

5. THE HOST ESTIMATE (ops/pers_4e_v2_estimate.py, *estimated*, nothing fitted)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
    case, rows' stream on the mean CTA's work      context 5                   context 8,192
    low, 290 GB/s (no stream gain)                 5,513 us, 181 tok/s         7,721 us, 130 tok/s
    middle, 380 GB/s                               4,177 us, 239 tok/s         6,046 us, 165 tok/s
    high, 450 GB/s                                 3,478 us, 288 tok/s         5,099 us, 196 tok/s
  The attention term at 5 tokens is 163 to 357 us a token: up to 8 steps of 0.5 to 1.2 us, plus 1.2 to 3.0 us of job
  and a 0.786 us wait a layer. At 8,192 it is 1,785 to 2,566 us: the KV cache's 33.8 MB a layer at 550 to 750 GB/s,
  plus the fold and the wait. The norm term is 57 to 100 us. The rows keep R3's wall-to-mean ratio, 1.065.
  300 tok/s is 3,333 us. With the middle attention and norm (319 us), the rows must stream at 486 GB/s or more on the
  mean CTA's work. That is L1's 410 GB/s pass, plus what the variants are meant to show.
