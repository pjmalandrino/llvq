WHAT WAS MEASURED
  Teacher: the reference modules with the native experts dequantized to float32 (NVFP4 in layers 0 to 19, MXFP8 in
  20 to 23). Student: the same model with the expert matrices of a layer set replaced, by the lattice
  reconstructions or by the expert tensors of a rival GGUF file. Nothing else moves: attention, tables and router are
  the teacher's in every arm. Score: KL(teacher || arm) of the next-token distributions on raw WikiText-2 test, 8
  windows of 2,048 tokens (16,376 positions), top-1 agreement, paired NLL. Device MPS, where an unchanged model gives
  KL exactly 0 (0.00019 on CPU). Intervals: bootstrap over the 8 windows, 95 %.
  Encode: the 4B's sealed recipe (tetra1, rotation, damping 1e-2, blocks of 24, no row scales), one Hessian an
  expert and a matrix group, tokens routed to the expert weighted by the squared routing weight, summed over the
  three passes of a loop layer. Calibration 64 x 2,048 tokens of dclm-edu, on the teacher's activations.
  Lattice expert bits: 2.0371 a weight in the archive accounting (*computed*; the files on disk give 2.0374).

DEPARTURES AND PREDICTIONS

  one minute with no expert encoded. The manifest now carries `num_experts` and `experts: [{tokens, weight_sum}]`,
  weight_sum being the sum of the squared routing weights over the layer's visits, the weights the Hessian carries.
  Departure 2: the operator's sentinel exits at once on stale swap; a night copy with a swap-growth criterion ran
  instead (~/llvq-inkling-mini/logs/sentinel_night.sh). Two memory incidents at 22:50 and 22:52, before any
  measurement: a mutant test ran with autograd on and took 20 GB of swap; both runs were killed and the loader now
  turns autograd off.
