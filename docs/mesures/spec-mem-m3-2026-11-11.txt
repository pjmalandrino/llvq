WHY THE HOUSE PEAK OF a12ip (4.139) EXCEEDS ITS GSM8K PEAK (3.669)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Same arm, same drafter, same install (3.324 GB after the drafter's load in both runs, *measured*).
- GSM8K prompts are 51 to 161 tokens. With 768 new tokens and a 16-row tree the KV stays under 1,024 positions
  (0.151 GB, *computed*). The sampler holds 3,403 MiB for most of the run and touches 3,499 MiB (3.669 GB).
- The house set holds the same 3,371 to 3,403 MiB through its 40 short prompts. Its four RAG prompts then climb to
  3,947 MiB (4.139 GB), 544 MiB (0.570 GB) above that plateau (*measured*).
- That climb is the RAG prefill and its KV cache, as in the weightless run (+0.638 GB there, attributed above). Under
  a12ip the drafter's own context cache also grows; it is not attributed separately.
- The same holds for a12: 4,619 MiB plateau, 5,163 MiB at the RAG prompts.
So the "short context" peaks of the prereg are RAG-prompt peaks. Their pre-RAG plateaus are 4.843 GB (a12), 3.568
(a12ip) and 2.190 (weightless) (*measured*).
