WHY IT WAS ATTEMPTED
---------------------
The record's IQ2 figure is **XXS at 38.87** micro on 2,280 questions
(2026-08-31). XXS is the bottom rung of llama.cpp's ladder, and setting our
format against it flatters us. IQ2_S and IQ2_M are the rungs a reader asks
about.

FAILURE 1, AND IT IS THE FINDING: IQ2_S NEEDS AN IMATRIX
---------------------------------------------------------
  llama_model_quantize: failed to quantize: this quantization requires an
  imatrix!

`llama-quantize` **refuses IQ2_S outright** without an importance matrix. So
llama.cpp's 2-bit rungs are **calibrated objects, exactly as ours are**: their
imatrix is our Hessian by another name, built by a forward pass over a
calibration corpus.

That has a consequence for any comparison. Building our own imatrix would
measure **our** calibration choices against our own format, which is not the
question. The arm therefore has to read a **published** file, built by the
people who build these, which is what a user downloads.

FAILURE 2: THE SERVER WAS NOT READY, PROBABLY
-----------------------------------------------
`unsloth/Qwen3-4B-GGUF` publishes `Qwen3-4B-UD-IQ2_M.gguf` for this exact
model, 1,532,931,872 bytes, sha256 `c9ae2746`. It was fetched, pushed to the
bucket and served.

The job's whole log is ten lines. `llama-server` was launched, the health probe
passed **2 seconds later**, the scorer ran for **9 minutes** and produced no
output and no file.

Two seconds is too fast to load 1.53 GB. The health endpoint most likely
answered before the model was in memory, and the scorer spent nine minutes
against a server that could not answer, under its own 900-second timeout. The
competing reading, that `python3` is absent from a C++ image and the message
was swallowed by a `| tail -14`, is not excluded by this log.

Neither was diagnosed further: a third attempt was declined.

advertises for IQ2_XXS is not its b/param. The advertised figure excludes the
embedding. By this repository's accounting it is **2.4967**, and every earlier
comparison that used 2.06 understated their rate.
