WHY THE 4E TOKEN LOOP KEEPS THE EAGER STEP'S 7.5 MS, AND THE 8,192 PREFILL, 2026-11-05
=====================================================================================

Result: two causes hold most of the 7.51 ms token of the 4E card reading (run 2, 133.1 tok/s at a 5-token context,
*measured*). First, the work split: every phase gives each CTA a fixed slice of whole rows, and the slowest CTA of a
phase does 1.37 to 1.46 times the serial iterations of an even spread over the 2,272 warps (*computed*), 27 to 32 % of
the rows' time, about 2.0 ms of the token if the measured time has the model's structure (*estimated*). Second, the
iteration rate: 16 warps a SM, one load a lane in flight and none ahead, so even an even split would stream the
1.377 GB a token at about 290 GB/s on the measured total, 440 GB/s on an unfitted latency model (*estimated*), against
410 GB/s for L1's one-launch pass at 32 warps a SM. The fixed work every CTA repeats a layer and the 254 grid barriers
come third, about 0.7 ms (*estimated*). The unfitted model predicts 4.1 to 6.3 ms, so 1.2 to 3.4 ms stay unattributed;
the kernel now carries phase stamps that measure each phase on the card, and the next reading reads them.
The 8,192 context of the mode now prefills in chunks of 4,096.

Prereg:  none (analysis and harness at $0). The next card reading reruns proofs/preregistration-pers-4e-2026-11-04.md
         with a deviation beside it (the coordinator's).
Cost:    $0. Host tests, NVRTC 12.4 and ptxas 12.4 in nvidia/cuda:12.4.1-devel-ubuntu22.04 on the Mac.
Inputs:  pers-4e-r2-2026-11-05.txt (133.1 tok/s, grid 142 x 512, 254 barriers a token, pers_4e_tokens_f1_t512 119
         registers 0 B local); pers-l1-2026-11-04.txt (barrier 0.786 us at 142 x 768, tpersall 410 GB/s at 4 CTAs of
         256); pers-sass-2026-11-05.txt (wavefronts a block on the sealed 4B's words).
Tools:   ops/pers_4e_model.py (the phases' critical paths); pers.rs::p4e_phase_report (the stamps, read).

1. THE 8,192 PREFILL
--------------------
  Run 2 stopped on "8192 vectors at once, more than 4096": run_pers_4e prefilled the whole prompt in one call, twice
  (the served door's `generate`, then `prefill_next`). Both now go in chunks of MAX_PREFILL_ROWS, as the context
  sweep already did: the served door through `generate_timed(prompt, n, 4096)`, the reference prefill through
  `prefill_next`, which now chunks (`prefill_next_in`). At or under 4,096 positions both are one call, as before.
  The runtime takes the context: 8,256 positions round up to a Grow capacity of 8,448, under MAX_CAP 8,704.
  Host proof: model.rs::a_chunked_prefill_next_is_the_prefill_of_generate (chunks 1, 4, 12, 13 and 4,096 on a
  13-token prompt, Grow store: the caches hold the prompt and the decode after it is `generate`'s); a mutant that
  stops advancing the offset between chunks is caught.

2. WHAT 4E DOES WITH ITS 7.5 MS
-------------------------------
  The kernel's split, read from pers_4e.cu and llvq_pers.cuh:
    - one CTA of 512 threads a SM, 142 CTAs, 16 warps a SM (L1's pass ran 32);
    - each phase gives CTA c rows [n c / 142, n (c + 1) / 142) of its matrix (or of q | k | v);
    - inside a CTA each warp claims one whole row, one Tetra block or one int4 word a lane per iteration, the next
      iteration's load issued only when this one is done (pers_tetra_row<24, false, false>, pers_q4_row);
    - every CTA repeats a layer's norms and rotations (ln1 + the 2,560 rotation, ln2 + its rotation, the 9,728 down
      rotation), restages the 18.8 KB of decoder tables in each MLP segment, and waits at 7 grid barriers.
  Per iteration (devel ptxas SASS of pers_4e_tokens_f1_t512): a Tetra block 231 instructions, tables in shared; an
  int4 word 65 to 70.

  Serial iterations on the slowest warp of the slowest CTA, against the even spread (*computed*, ops/pers_4e_model.py):
    phase        rows    a CTA   a warp   slowest   even     the warps' time that works
    q/k/v        6,144    43.3    2.70     30.0     13.52    a v slice (int4 rows of 10) against a q/k slice's 12
    o            2,560    18.0    1.13     32.0     18.03    56.3 %
    gate/up      9,728    68.5    4.28     40.0     34.25    85.6 %
    down Tetra   2,560    18.0    1.13     26.0     14.65    56.3 %
    down int4    2,560    18.0    1.13     76.0     42.82    56.3 %
    head       151,936 1,070.0   66.87    670.0    668.73    99.8 %
  Over the rows' phases of a token: x1.373 to x1.460 for t_Q / t_T from 0.6 to 1.0, whatever t_T.

  Iteration times (*estimated*, the largest of three floors of a SM round, none fitted): latency tau (0.5 to 0.8 us,
  assumed) plus the warp's own chain; issue (4 warps a scheduler x 231 or 70); LSU (16 warps x 65 or 43 wavefronts).
  Latency binds both loops: t_T 0.63 to 0.95 us, t_Q 0.53 to 0.83 us. Fixed work 10.5 to 16.5 us a layer.
    model, tau 0.50 to 0.80 us     4.11 to 6.26 ms a token (0.55 to 0.83 x the measured 7.51)
    middle point (tau 0.65)        5.18 ms: q/k/v 0.73, o 0.78, gate/up 1.38, down 1.14, head 0.46, fixed 0.49,
                                   barriers 0.20 (254 x 0.786 us)
  What the model leaves out, and what the stamps measure: the slowest of 2,272 warps under a latency spread (a sum of
  30 loads at a 30 % spread each, maximized over 2,272 warps, is about 15 to 20 % over its mean), the sustained clock,
  the barrier's back-off (up to 512 ns of sleep for a CTA that waited long).

THE TWO CAUSES, AND THE THIRD
  1. The split, 1.37 to 1.46 x on the rows' phases (*computed*). o and down hold 2,560 rows for 2,272 warps: every
     CTA spends two row-times on 1.13 rows a warp. q/k/v waits on its last 24 CTAs, whose slices are int4 v rows
     of 10 iterations against 4 for q and k. On the measured total: about 2.0 ms of the 6.8 ms of rows (*estimated*).
  2. The iteration rate. One block or word in flight a lane, 16 warps a SM: an iteration costs a full load latency
     plus its decode. An even split would stream at about 290 GB/s on the measured total, 440 on the model
     (*estimated*); DRAM's floor for the 1.377 GB is 1.59 ms at 864 GB/s (*computed*).
  3. Fixed work and barriers, about 0.7 ms (*estimated*: 254 barriers at L1's 0.786 us, 36 x 10.5 to 16.5 us of
     norms, rotations and table staging every CTA repeats).

WHAT EACH WOULD TAKE, WITHIN IDENTITY (4E owes the served bits)
  - Cost-weighted slices for q/k/v: which CTA computes a row moves no bit. 30 iterations fall to about 15.
  - 24 warps a SM: o and down fit in one round (2,560 rows for 3,408 warps). Needs the 768-thread symbol without
    local memory: 8 B under the driver's ptxas today (run 2), 36 B under the devel one.
  - The next iteration's load in flight: pers_tetra_row's PREFETCH form keeps each lane's order of accumulation,
    so its bits; the int4 row takes the same change. It attacks cause 2 directly.
  - Idle warps at a phase's end prefetching the next phase's rows into L2 (L1's rule D).
  - Not within identity: splitting a row across warps (split-K) changes each lane's order of accumulation.

3. THE PHASE STAMPS
-------------------
  pers_4e.cu gains a STAMP template parameter and three symbols, pers_4e_stamps_f{0,1,2}_t512. Thread 0 of every CTA
  writes %globaltimer at each grid wait: after a __syncthreads (the CTA's work done) and after the release. Per
  token, 3 + 10 x layers events a CTA (363 at the 4B). Pers4eLaunch carries the buffer (`stamps`, offset 32, 40 bytes
  in all); the token symbols never read it.
  pers.rs::p4e_phase_report turns one launch's stamps into, per phase summed over the layers, the median over tokens
  of: the wall (how far the grid's last release moved, the walls summing to the token), the slowest CTA's work, the
  mean CTA's work, and the CTA most often slowest. It refuses stamps that contradict their barriers (a release before
  the wait's last arrival, a CTA's stamps going back, a stamp unwritten).
  LLVQ_PERS_4E runs one stamped launch at each context after the timed rounds and outside them: the same tokens or
  its stamps are void, then the table and the stamped token's time beside the timed 4E's.
  Proofs (host, $0):
    - pers_matches_served.rs::the_4e_phase_stamps_keep_the_tokens_and_bracket_the_waits: two launches through one
      state, RoPE forms 0 and 1, grids 3 and 4: the stamped symbols make the token symbols' tokens, caches and
      residual; their stamps pass the report; the walls sum to the token. Mutants caught: a release left unstamped,
      an arrival stamped after the wait.
    - pers.rs::the_4e_phase_report_reads_the_stamps: stamps made by hand, every wall, slowest and mean known.
    - pers_4e_tokens_f1_t512 and pers_4e_tokens_f1 compile to the same SASS, instruction for instruction, before
      and after (ptxas 12.4): the timed 4E does not move. The stamp symbols: 118 to 119 registers, 0 B spill.
