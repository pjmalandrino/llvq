WHAT THE TRANSCODE COST, BECAUSE IT IS THE PART THAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------
The disk stream and the served layout are not each other's byte order. Read
big-endian, the six bytes of a block give `label << 1 | gain`; the Tetra word is
`label | gain << 47` and the shader reads it little-endian at bit offset 48·j of
its row. So the gain bit crosses the word, and a byte reversal is not the
transcode. The first version was a byte reversal: 12 blocks of 12 wrong on the
fixture, caught on the first dispatch. A tolerance would have hidden nothing here,
since the values are integers, which is the whole reason this gate is exact.
