1. LOAD WIDTHS OF THE STAGED READS
---------------------------------
  - tv_tetra48_h, the served decode kernel: per block, six `ld.shared.v4.f32` at [xs_b + 0, 16,
    32, 48, 64, 80] (tv_tetra48_h.ptx lines 258-347 of the cut). No scalar shared load. `xs` is
    declared `.extern .shared .align 16`, and a block is 96 bytes, so the alignment is provable.
  - The loads are grouped by address and issued ahead of their uses; the 24 fma.rn.f32 consume the
    lanes in TETRA48_ORDER (0,1,2,3,4,7,10,12,...). Use order did not prevent vectorization, as
    persistent.md §3.8 expected after review finding 10.
  - tv_f1r_v3g and tv_tetra48_seg (bench unit): the same six v4 loads.
  - tv_tetra48_rows_h, the served prefill kernel (4 rows): rows 0, 1 and 3 get six v4 each. Row 2,
    at +24576 bytes, was split by the vectorizer into v4, f32, f32, v2, v2, v2, v4, v4, v4, plus one
    redundant scalar reload of element 15: 9 shared load instructions for that row instead of 6.
    That is a prefill kernel and not the persistent design's decode stream; it is reported, not read.

  Rule outcome (persistent.md §5, L1a row): "Six v4 loads: the stride arms stay as controls with a
  null prior." At NVVM level the 358 GB/s scalar-load cap of §3.8 does not apply to the decode
  kernel. The 2-way quarter-warp conflict of the v4 case remains (48 wavefronts a warp and block,
  *computed* in §3.8, not re-derived here). Whether ptxas keeps LDS.128 is the card's SASS (L1).

  - All three are fused into fma.rn.f32 by NVVM. The PTX holds no bare mul.f32 + add.f32 pair:
    the only mul.f32 is t = acc_b*gscale, whose consumer is the multiplicand of the site-1 fma, and
    the 29 add.f32 are 24 decode adds of the constant 0fCB000080 (bit-pattern to float) and 5
    butterfly adds of warp_sum, none fed by a multiply.
  - The 24 inner products are explicit __fmaf_rn in source and fma.rn.f32 in PTX.
  - tv_tetra48_rows_h: the same pattern per row. Site 1 is mul.f32 t = gscale*a then fma.rn.f32
    acc = invnorm*t + acc (lines 470-573 of the cut); every epilogue is fma.rn.f32 before
    cvt.rn.f16.f32. NVVM also emits a tail-free specialization, fma.rn.f32(a, rscale, +0.0); it
    rounds as a*rscale + 0.0f does, sign of zero included.
  - tv_f1r_v3g and tv_tetra48_seg: 31 fma.rn.f32, 1 mul.f32, 29 add.f32, the same profile as the
    served decode kernel.

  Rule outcome (L1a row): "fma.rn at a site: spell __fmaf_rn." All three sites take that branch.
  A persistent Tetra row that writes __fmaf_rn(__fmul_rn(acc_b, gscale), invnorm, acc) for site 1,
  __fmaf_rn in the tail loop and __fmaf_rn(acc, rscale, tv) for the epilogue reproduces this PTX's
  arithmetic by construction. The "a bare pair: the card decides" branch does not occur.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------
  - The card's SASS. The card JIT-compiles this PTX with driver 580.178.04's ptxas, not the 12.4
    devel one. ptxas keeps an fma.rn.f32 as an FFMA, so the arithmetic result is settled; whether
    the six v4 loads stay LDS.128 is not, and that is L1's cubin dump.
  - sm_120 or compute_80 (A100): only compute_89 was compiled.
  - The bench unit was assembled by the probe's own recipe, which carries fewer kernels than the
    real bench unit; its digest is not checked. A kernel's PTX is not expected to depend on its
    neighbours, which is an assumption, not a check.
  - Bank conflicts or any time: no card ran.
