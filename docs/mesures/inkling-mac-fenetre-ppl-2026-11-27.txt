INKLING 2 MINI ON THE MAC, THE PERPLEXITY OF THE WINDOW ARM, 2026-11-27

RESULT
  The window arm (`inkrun --attn-window narrow`) passes the prereg's rule on this Mac. Joined texts pooled (primary,
  12,286 positions): perplexity ratio narrow against full +0.0219 % [-0.0451, +0.0889]. Single windows pooled
  (secondary, 12,282 positions): +0.0147 % [-0.0469, +0.0764] (*computed* from the dumps, *measured* NLL). Both points
  and both 95 % intervals are inside +-0.12 %. No difference sits at a chunk or piece boundary.
  The arm carries the band the pieces carry, on this Mac. It stays off: serving it needs the operator's word and a
  reading on the other backends. The code did not change in this lot.
  Prereg: proofs/preregistration-inkling-fenetre-ppl-2026-11-27.md (commit f7db98d, anchor 227cb93). Deviations:
  -ECARTS.md beside it. Raw files: inkling-mac-fenetre-ppl-2026-11-27-brut/.

THE TEXTS
  Six WikiText-2 test windows of 2,048 tokens and two joined texts of 6,144 tokens. The prereg's conditional took
  its larger branch: the windows' maker is on this Mac (`ops/inkling/data.py`, `eval_windows`, the test parquet in
  the local Hub cache, the tokenizer of `~/llvq-inkling-mini/native`, all read offline). Its windows 0, 1 and 2 equal
  the files the Mac held, id for id; windows 3, 4 and 5 were made the same way before the first run (`windows.py`,
  `windows.log`). Joined texts: w0+w1+w2 and w3+w4+w5. The sha256 of the eight texts are in `texts.sha256`.

THE RUNS
  Part D's binary (`target/lotd/inkrun`, sha256 cb7d8d9e..., the same as part D's `inkrun.sha256`), Metal, part D's
  reference flags (arm B: pieces of 512, score chunks of 128, slack 1,024, fused glue, fused+scores attention, int4
  head), `--score-prompt on --nll-out`. Two arms that differ by `--attn-window` alone, `full` then `narrow`, text by
  text, 45 s apart, in one guarded job from 03:39 to 04:07 (`all.sh`, `all.log`). Tree clean under `llvq-llm`,
  `llvq-runtime` and `configs` at the start. Load1 5.74 before the first run (lot G's job had just ended), 1.85 to
  3.17 before the others; GPU 0 to 5 %.
  All sixteen runs finished (rc 0). Peak footprint (`/usr/bin/time -l`, *measured*): 20.4 to 21.8 GB a single
  window, 44.6 to 45.9 GB a joined text, where every row's logits are kept on the device and read to the host.
  Memory free stayed 76 to 81 % before each run.

WHAT THE READING DOES NOT SAY
  That the arm costs nothing: 12,286 positions on one machine resolve about +-0.07 % (*computed*, the half-width of
  the interval); a cost under that is not resolved, as the prereg says.
  Anything about CUDA, the CPU or the runtime: one stack, Metal, arm B, `inkrun` only.
  Anything about generation (GSM8K or chat): this reads teacher-forced perplexity only.
  Why the first 64 positions keep their bits: not traced (the first score chunk of a text starts at key 0 in both
  arms; why only half of it matches is not read).
  The speed of the arm: the read times of this lot are single runs that include reading the logits back, and
  disagree in sign on the two joined texts.
