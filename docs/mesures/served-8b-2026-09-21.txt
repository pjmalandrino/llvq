Five rounds an arm. The speed ratios are quotients of medians, envelope fused lo over
dense hi to fused hi over dense lo. fusedrun loads one arm at a time, so rounds are
never interleaved or paired (-ECARTS E4).

256 tokens identical to the dense arm, in both processes. The prefill gate at 203 tokens
gives the same argmax (max |Δ logit| 0.27 on a scale of 34). The log reads tile 64,
252 launches a token (216 Tetra + 36 int4), 144 rotation launches, two q8 tables
(untied head, 1,322.5 MB). The served door wrote a dump with `# arithmetic=served
kernel` and `# layout=tetra48` (57 questions, a smoke, no score).

The same-head ratio is ×1.47 (hard rule 4): both arms of process 2 carry the f16 head.
At the 8B the untied f16 head is 1.24 GB read a token (*computed*, 151936 × 4096 × 2 B);
both f16 tables hold 2.49 GB on the card. The f16 arm is 14.7 ms a token slower than
the q8 arm (*computed* from the medians). The cause is not measured. The q8 head runs
through `tv_q8_h`, the f16 head through a candle matmul. The f16 head reads 0.58 GB
more than the q8 head, about 0.9 ms at the 682 GB/s FP16 rate below (*computed*):
bytes explain under a tenth of the gap. The 4B left the same gap unexplained
(f1e0-2026-09-10.txt, 50.2 against 92.9 tok/s).

For reference, from another job on the same card type (not a same-process ratio):
the served Planes14 8B read 75.5 tok/s in 5.41 GB, 5.322 b/param (ETAT §2, FUSE=1,
tile 128).

formed over unequal passes, 216 matrices against 252. The 36 `v_proj` int4 are counted,
not timed, in this bench. They are 2.2 % of FP16's bytes: if FP16 time scales with bytes,
Tetra on equal support is about ×4.0 FP16 (*estimated*). Tetra against Planes14: 1.62×,
floor removed 2.56×, over the same unequal passes. The f64 row check passes on the three
compared arms (worst 2.8e-8, FP16; Planes14 and Tetra 1.6e-8, Tetra over its 216
matrices). nullk is not compared (-inf). The two "f16 lm_head" lines of the log are
struck: `planesbench.rs:3537, 3554` add the 4B's 389 M-weight head, not the 8B's 622 M.
