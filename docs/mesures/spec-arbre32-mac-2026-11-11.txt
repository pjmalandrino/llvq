SETUP
-----
Target: the sealed 4B through the MLX door (ops/mlx_tetra, hfpack ~/q4b-hf-2026-09-28); its int4 g64 table
dequantized to f32 for the drafter's embedding and tied head (the card's q4 vocabulary). Drafter
z-lab/Qwen3-4B-DFlash-b16 at b74e3a32, f32 (the card runs f16). Taps: inputs of layers 2, 10, 18, 26, 34 from one
teacher-forced forward over prompt + text.
Text:
- GSM8K rows 0-199 (all 200, not 40: the run fit in 12 min) and the house set's 20 math prompts (GSM8K rows 0-19):
  the card's own greedy text, decoder `pass` of SP8's gsm200.jsonl (sha256 346880b0), 768 tokens after `first` at most,
  cut at 256 for the house set. The dump omits `first`; it is the MLX door's argmax at the prompt's end (8 distinct
  tokens over the 200, 1654 the most common), checked by the teacher-forced agreement below.
- House chat, code, rag (24 prompts): SP8 left no token dump of them (spec-house.txt holds per-prompt figures only),
  so their text is the MLX door's own greedy decode, 256 tokens after `first`, <|im_end|> included.
Prompt ids built as specbench does (llvq_llm::chatfmt); every one of the 244 lengths equals the card's log.
Method: at each pending position m the drafter reads taps 0..m-1 and the block [x_m, MASK x 15]; the top 4 a position
and their log probabilities give a tree of the first N nodes popped best first (draft_tree_scored). Accepted: the
longest prefix of the greedy tokens whose rank path is in the tree. Rounds replayed as the loop runs them; accepted a
pass = tokens over passes minus one, which is how the card's per-prompt line counts (256 tokens, 40 passes, 5.40).
Card aggregates below are recomputed that way from the card's per-prompt lines (GSM8K 5.38; SP8's histogram mean
prints 5.41).

PROJECTION, GSM8K (*estimated*)
----------
tok/s = (accepted + 1) / (verify + drafter); verify 8.53 ms at 16 rows (SP8, measured) + 0.087 ms a row past 16
(estimated from c_16 - c_8 on the card), drafter 4.15 ms a round (SP8, measured; taken as independent of N, the
readout's top 4 being the same).
  tree15  6.37 tokens a pass  verify 8.53 ms  -> 502 tok/s (SP8 measured 502.1)
  tree23  6.73                verify 9.23 ms  -> 503 tok/s
  tree31  6.95                verify 9.92 ms  -> 494 tok/s
Breakeven (*computed*): 23 nodes pays if a row past 16 costs under 0.090 ms, 31 nodes under 0.072 ms.
