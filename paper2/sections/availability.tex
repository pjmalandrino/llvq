\section*{Availability}

Code, the measurement journals behind every number, the preregistrations and
this paper's source are at \url{https://github.com/pjmalandrino/llvq}, under
MIT or Apache-2.0. Appendix~\ref{app:record} gives the sealed file and the
commands that replay the three headline measurements. The competitor 2-bit
kernel is GPL~v3, so the benchmark fetches it at a pinned commit rather than
redistributing it. Each measurement was preregistered in a timestamped file
naming the arms, the gates and a prediction; those files are never edited
afterwards, and the deviations and the scored predictions are in
Appendix~\ref{app:record}.
