\section*{Availability}

Code, the measurement logs behind every number, the preregistrations and this
paper's source are at \url{https://github.com/pjmalandrino/llvq}, under MIT or
Apache-2.0, and this paper under CC BY 4.0. Appendix~\ref{app:record} lists the files and the commands that
replay the main measurements. The QTIP kernel used in the benchmark of
Appendix~\ref{app:bench} is GPL~v3, so the benchmark fetches it at a pinned
commit rather than redistributing it. Each measurement campaign was
preregistered in a timestamped file naming the arms, the decision rules and a
prediction. Those files are never edited afterwards; deviations are written
next to them.
