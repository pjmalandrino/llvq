Inkling 2 Mini in 8 GB

Thinking Machines' 26.4-billion-parameter mixture of experts, quantized at 2.47 bits per parameter. 8.15 GB to download against 19.5 GB native. The weights stay compressed on the card.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/pjmalandrino/llvq-tetra/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
llvq run inkling2:mini

Linux with an NVIDIA card, RTX 40 series or newer, driver 550 or newer. Files on Hugging Face.

Quality

KL divergence against the native model: 0.0880 nats on WikiText-2. GSM8K, first 200 problems: 191 through the dense reference path and 186 through the fused kernels, not separated (p = 0.18). Nothing was trained: the codes come from a Hessian-weighted search over calibration text.

Questions

Is this a GGUF of Inkling 2 Mini?

No. The files are in the Tetra format and are read by the llvq runtime. They do not load in llama.cpp, Ollama or LM Studio.

Can I use it from tools written for Ollama or OpenAI?

Yes. llvq serve exposes an OpenAI-compatible route and an Ollama-compatible one on 127.0.0.1:11435, with streaming and tool calls.

Does Inkling 2 Mini fit in 16 GB of VRAM?

The measured peak while reading a 9,000-token prompt is 11.5 GB on an NVIDIA L4 and 12.0 GB on an L40S. It has not been run on a 16 GB consumer card yet.

How much quality does 2-bit quantization cost?

The KL divergence against the native model is 0.0880 nats on WikiText-2. On the first 200 GSM8K problems the file answers 186 through the fused kernels. The native model was not run through the same GSM8K harness.

Is the vision tower included?

No. This file is text only.

Limits

Text only. Not yet run on a 16 GB consumer card. Reading a long prompt for the first time is slow; a follow-up turn reuses the cache. Use of the weights is bound by Thinking Machines' Model Acceptable Use Policy.
