Qwen3 at 2.7 bits per parameter

Qwen3 4B, 8B and 14B, quantized on the Leech lattice. The 14B loads in 5 GB of card memory.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/pjmalandrino/llvq-tetra/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
llvq run qwen3:14b

Also qwen3:8b and qwen3:4b. Linux with an NVIDIA card, RTX 40 series or newer, or macOS on Apple Silicon. Files on Hugging Face.

MMLU is 5-shot on all 14,042 test questions, GSM8K zero-shot on its 1,319. Speed is batch 1, greedy, 256 tokens after a 5-token prompt, on one NVIDIA L40S. Sources and limits are in the README and the paper.

Questions

How does it compare with 4-bit AWQ?

Model for model, 4-bit AWQ scores higher and needs about twice the bits. At about the same size, the Tetra 8B scores above AWQ's 4B and the Tetra 14B above AWQ's 8B.

How does it compare with llama.cpp IQ2_S?

At the same size Tetra scores higher on MMLU for all three models. llama.cpp decodes faster and holds less card memory after a long prompt.

Are these GGUF files?

No. They are in the Tetra format and are read by the llvq runtime, or in Python by the llvq-tetra package for transformers.
